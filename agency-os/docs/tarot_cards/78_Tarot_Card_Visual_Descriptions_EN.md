# Complete Visual Descriptions of All 78 Tarot Cards

**Rider–Waite–Smith Tarot Deck | Concise English Edition**

Each description preserves the original visual details—figures, clothing, poses, objects, symbols, colors, and backgrounds—while using concise English.

## I. Major Arcana (22 Cards)

**0. The Fool**  
A blond young man stands at a mountain cliff's edge, gazing skyward, unaware of the drop. He wears a floral tunic, yellow boots, and a feathered cap. A small bundle hangs from a staff over his shoulder; he holds a white rose. A white dog leaps beside him. Snowy mountains, a bright yellow sky, and the sun fill the background.

**1. The Magician**  
A young man in a white robe and red cloak stands behind a stone table, wearing a serpent-shaped belt biting its own tail. One hand raises a white wand skyward; the other points downward. An infinity symbol (∞) floats above his head. The table holds a wand, cup, sword, and pentacle. Red roses, white lilies, and a golden-yellow sky surround him.

**2. The High Priestess**  
A solemn woman sits between a black pillar marked B and a white pillar marked J. She wears blue-and-white robes, a chest cross, and a silver crown featuring crescent and full moons. A partially concealed scroll marked "TORA" rests on her lap. Behind her hangs a curtain patterned with pomegranates and palms; a white crescent moon lies at her feet.

**3. The Empress**  
A blond woman reclines on a cushioned red throne, wearing a flowing white gown patterned with red pomegranates. She wears a crown of twelve stars and holds a golden scepter in her right hand. A heart-shaped shield bearing the Venus symbol (♀) rests beside her. Dense forests, a flowing river, and golden wheat fields form a fertile landscape.

**4. The Emperor**  
A stern, white-bearded elderly king sits upright on a massive gray stone throne carved with ram heads. He wears a golden crown, bright red robe, and visible armor. His right hand holds an ankh-shaped scepter; his left holds a golden orb. Tall, barren, rocky mountains rise behind him, with almost no vegetation.

**5. The Hierophant**  
A religious leader sits between two gray pillars, wearing an elaborate red-and-white robe and a triple crown. His right hand gives a blessing; his left holds a staff topped with three horizontal bars. Two followers in floral robes kneel before him. Two crossed metal keys lie on the floor between them.

**6. The Lovers**  
A nude man and woman stand apart on green grass. Behind the woman grows a fruit tree entwined with a serpent; behind the man stands a tree with flame-shaped foliage. Above them, a large angel with red-purple wings spreads its arms beneath a radiant sun. A sharp mountain peak rises in the distance between the figures.

**7. The Chariot**  
A young warrior in ornate armor and a star-shaped crown stands upright inside a chariot. Above him is a dark-blue canopy covered with white stars, supported by four pillars. A black and a white sphinx rest before the vehicle without visible reins. A winged disk and shield decorate the chariot. City walls, towers, and a river appear behind.

**8. Strength**  
A woman in a white gown gently holds open the jaws of a golden lion, calming rather than attacking it. An infinity symbol (∞) floats above her head. Floral garlands decorate her hair and waist. The lion's mouth is open but nonaggressive. Green grass, shrubs, and distant mountains fill the background.

**9. The Hermit**  
A white-bearded elderly man stands alone on a snowy mountain summit, wearing a gray hooded cloak. His head is slightly lowered. One hand raises a lantern containing a glowing six-pointed star; the other grips a long wooden staff. A dark gray-blue sky and desolate snow-covered mountains surround him, with no other figures.

**10. Wheel of Fortune**  
A large orange-yellow wheel bears the letters T, A, R, O, Hebrew characters, and alchemical symbols. A blue sphinx holding a sword sits atop it; a serpent descends on the left, while a red animal-headed mythological figure appears on the right. In the cloudy corners, a winged angel, eagle, lion, and bull each hold a book.

**11. Justice**  
A solemn woman in a red robe and golden crown sits on a stone throne. Her right hand holds a long sword vertically; her left holds balanced golden scales. Two gray pillars frame a purple curtain behind her. The tip of one white shoe emerges beneath her robe.

**12. The Hanged Man**  
A man hangs upside down by his right ankle from a leafy T-shaped wooden frame. His free leg bends behind the bound leg, forming an inverted four. His hands rest behind his back, creating an inverted triangular pose. He wears a blue tunic, red leggings, and yellow shoes. His expression is peaceful, and a golden halo surrounds his head.

**13. Death**  
A skeleton in black armor rides a white horse, carrying a black banner with a white five-petaled rose. A fallen king lies beneath the horse, his crown displaced. A religious elder, a woman, and a child react differently to the rider. In the distance, a golden sun rises between two tall towers.

**14. Temperance**  
A red-winged angel in a white robe stands beside a pool, pouring water between two golden cups. A triangle inside a square marks the robe's chest, while a circular symbol appears on the forehead. One foot rests on land, the other in water. Yellow irises grow nearby, and a winding path leads toward mountains and golden light.

**15. The Devil**  
A giant goat-headed devil with curved horns and bat wings crouches on a black pedestal. An inverted pentagram marks its forehead. One hand is raised; the other points a flaming torch downward. Beneath it stand a nude man and woman, each with horns, a tail, and a metal chain linking their neck to the pedestal.

**16. The Tower**  
A tall gray stone tower rises from a steep rocky mountain. A massive yellow lightning bolt strikes its crown-shaped golden roof, blasting it into the air. Red flames erupt from the tower. Two figures, one in red and one in blue, fall with limbs outstretched. Yellow flame-shaped sparks scatter across the dark sky.

**17. The Star**  
A nude blond woman kneels beside a pool, with one foot in water and one knee on grass. She pours water from two jugs, one into the pool and another onto the ground, forming several streams. Above shines a large yellow eight-pointed star surrounded by seven smaller stars. Green hills and a bird perched in a tree appear behind.

**18. The Moon**  
A large glowing yellow moon with a closed-eyed human face illuminates the night sky. Below, a dog and a wolf howl upward from opposite sides of a winding path. The path begins beside a pool, passes between two gray towers, and continues toward distant mountains. A red crayfish emerges from the water.

**19. The Sun**  
A huge golden sun with a human face shines overhead, radiating alternating straight and wavy rays. Beneath it, a nude child rides a white horse, arms extended, carrying a bright red banner. The child wears a floral wreath with a red feather. Behind a low stone wall grow four enormous sunflowers.

**20. Judgement**  
A blond angel with red wings emerges from clouds, blowing a long golden trumpet bearing a white flag with a red cross. Below, nude men, women, and children rise from rectangular coffins in gray-blue water. They face the angel with raised arms. Snow-covered mountains appear in the distance.

**21. The World**  
A woman draped in purple fabric floats within a large oval green laurel wreath. She holds a short wand in each hand, balancing in a dancing pose with one leg crossed behind the other. Red ribbons tie the wreath at its top and bottom. A winged angel, eagle, bull, and lion occupy the four corners against a blue, cloud-filled sky.

## II. Wands (14 Cards)

**22. Ace of Wands**  
A giant hand emerges from a white cloud on the right, gripping an upright, thick brown wooden staff. Fresh green shoots sprout from its top and sides, with several leaves falling through the air. Green hills, a river, forests, and a distant castle appear beneath a pale gray-white sky.

**23. Two of Wands**  
A man in a long red robe stands atop a castle, partly facing away. He holds a small globe in his right hand and steadies one wooden staff with the other; a second staff is fixed to the wall. Roses, lilies, and a cross decorate the stonework. He overlooks mountains, sea, and vast lands beneath a gray-blue sky.

**24. Three of Wands**  
A man in a red cloak stands with his back to the viewer at a hill's edge, holding one of three upright wooden staffs. He gazes over a golden sea where three sailing ships travel. A winding coastline extends toward distant hills.

**25. Four of Wands**  
Four upright wooden staffs form two pairs supporting a decorative garland of green leaves, flowers, and fruit, resembling a ceremonial arch. Behind them, two brightly dressed figures raise bouquets in celebration, with other people farther away. A grand estate or castle with walls and towers stands in the background.

**26. Five of Wands**  
Five young men in differently colored clothing gather on open grassland, each wielding a wooden staff. Some raise their staffs, others swing them sideways or push against one another. The five staffs intersect chaotically, resembling a fight or competition. A pale-blue sky stretches overhead, with no visible injuries or blood.

**27. Six of Wands**  
A confident man wearing a laurel wreath and bright red cloak rides a white horse. He raises a wooden staff decorated with another laurel wreath. Several attendants surround him, each carrying a staff, forming a victorious procession. Green trees and a bright sky fill the background.

**28. Seven of Wands**  
A young man stands on elevated, uneven ground, leaning forward defensively while gripping a wooden staff with both hands. Six other staffs thrust upward toward him from below, although their wielders remain unseen. He wears a green tunic, orange-yellow trousers, and mismatched shoes.

**29. Eight of Wands**  
Eight long wooden staffs with green shoots fly diagonally from upper left to lower right in nearly parallel lines. No people appear. Beneath the open pale-blue sky lie green hills, a river, and woodland. The staffs appear to be moving rapidly toward the ground.

**30. Nine of Wands**  
An injured man wearing an orange tunic stands hunched slightly forward, gripping a wooden staff with both hands. A white bandage wraps around his head, and he looks sideways with a guarded expression. Eight additional staffs stand upright behind him like a defensive barrier. Green hills appear in the background.

**31. Ten of Wands**  
A man in an orange tunic and dark trousers walks hunched forward, carrying ten heavy wooden staffs against his chest. Their upper ends spread outward, nearly hiding his head and blocking his sight. Ahead lies a town with red-roofed buildings, trees, and farmland, his apparent destination.

**32. Page of Wands**  
A young page stands on dry yellow sand, holding a tall wooden staff with both hands and examining its green shoots. He wears a yellow salamander-patterned tunic, red cloak, and a hat with a long feather. Three yellow-brown mounds rise in the barren desert background.

**33. Knight of Wands**  
A young knight in silver armor rides a brown horse rearing on its hind legs. He wears a yellow surcoat decorated with salamanders and a helmet with bright red plumes, raising a wooden staff in his right hand. Desert terrain and low hills fill the background. The horse's mane and rider's clothing suggest vigorous movement.

**34. Queen of Wands**  
A crowned queen wearing a golden gown and white cloak sits on a golden throne. She holds a blooming sunflower in one hand and an upright wooden staff in the other. Lion carvings adorn the throne, with a sandy landscape behind. A small black cat sits facing forward at her feet.

**35. King of Wands**  
A crowned king in an orange-red robe and golden-yellow cloak sits on an ornate stone throne. He holds a wooden staff sprouting green leaves. Lion and salamander carvings decorate the throne, while a small lizard rests near his feet. His body turns slightly sideways, as though preparing to rise.

## III. Cups (14 Cards)

**36. Ace of Cups**  
A hand emerges from a white cloud, holding an enormous golden chalice. Above it, a white dove descends with a white disk marked by a cross in its beak, ready to place it inside. Several streams of water overflow from the cup into a pond dotted with green lily pads and white water lilies.

**37. Two of Cups**  
A man and woman face one another, each holding a golden cup. The man leans slightly forward while the woman responds gently. Above them floats a caduceus entwined with two snakes, topped by a lion's head with enormous red wings. Green fields and a distant house form the background.

**38. Three of Cups**  
Three women in differently colored long dresses stand in a small circle within a garden, raising golden cups in celebration. Their arms cross as they dance together. Pumpkins, grapes, and assorted fruits cover the ground, surrounded by lush green plants.

**39. Four of Cups**  
A young man sits cross-legged beneath a large tree, arms folded across his chest. He stares indifferently at three golden cups on the grass before him. From a cloud on the right, a mysterious hand offers him a fourth cup, which he appears unwilling to accept.

**40. Five of Cups**  
A figure wearing a long black cloak stands beside a river with head lowered. Three golden cups lie overturned in front, their contents spilled, while two intact cups remain upright behind. A stone bridge crosses the river toward a distant house or small castle.

**41. Six of Cups**  
Two young children stand in an old courtyard. The taller child offers the other a golden cup filled with white flowers. Additional flower-filled cups rest on the ground and a stone platform. Gray stone houses, old buildings, and courtyard paths surround them, with a taller figure visible in the distance.

**42. Seven of Cups**  
A dark human silhouette, seen from behind, gazes at seven golden cups floating among clouds. They contain a human head, a glowing veiled figure, a snake, a castle, jewels and gemstones, a laurel wreath, and a winged dragon. Misty clouds envelop the entire dreamlike scene.

**43. Eight of Cups**  
A man wearing a red cloak walks away toward barren mountains, leaning on a long staff. Behind him, eight golden cups form two rows—five below and three above—with a central gap. A human-faced moon hangs in the dark sky above rocky terrain, a stream, and a silent valley.

**44. Nine of Cups**  
A stout man in a white robe and red cap sits on a wooden bench, arms folded across his chest, smiling with satisfaction. Behind him, nine golden cups stand in a neat curved row on a display covered with blue fabric, resembling a prized collection.

**45. Ten of Cups**  
A couple stands on green grass, facing a huge rainbow filled with ten golden cups. The man embraces the woman with one arm and points skyward with the other; she also raises her arms. Nearby, two children hold hands and dance. A river, green valley, trees, and cozy house complete the landscape.

**46. Page of Cups**  
A young page stands beside the sea, wearing a blue patterned tunic, pink trousers, and a broad blue hat. He holds a golden cup in one hand and looks down as a small fish unexpectedly emerges from it, seemingly speaking to him. A calm ocean with gentle waves stretches behind.

**47. Knight of Cups**  
A young knight in silver armor rides a slowly walking white horse. Wing-shaped ornaments decorate his helmet and boots, while a blue robe patterned with red fish covers his armor. He carefully presents a golden cup in one hand. A stream, rocky land, and distant mountains form the background.

**48. Queen of Cups**  
A blond queen wearing a golden crown and blue-white robes sits on a stone throne beside the sea. She carefully holds an ornate, lidded golden cup decorated with angel figures, staring intently at it. Carvings of children and aquatic creatures decorate her throne. Shells, pebbles, and calm seawater surround the base.

**49. King of Cups**  
A mature king sits on a massive gray stone throne seemingly floating in a restless sea. He wears a blue robe, yellow-and-red cloak, and golden crown. One hand holds a golden cup; the other holds a short scepter. A sailing ship appears on one side, while a dolphin leaps from the water on the other.

## IV. Swords (14 Cards)

**50. Ace of Swords**  
A giant hand emerges from a white cloud, gripping an upright silver sword. Its blade passes through a golden crown, with green branches hanging from both sides. Small yellow flame-shaped symbols float nearby. Barren gray mountains rise in the distance.

**51. Two of Swords**  
A woman in a white robe sits on a stone bench, blindfolded with white cloth. Her arms cross over her chest, each hand holding a long sword, forming an X. A dark-blue sea with sharp emerging rocks lies behind her, beneath a sky containing a small crescent moon.

**52. Three of Swords**  
A large, bright-red heart floats at the center, pierced by three silver swords. One enters vertically from above, while two penetrate diagonally from opposite sides. Heavy dark-gray clouds and dense rainfall fill the background. No people or other scenery appear.

**53. Four of Swords**  
A stone effigy of a knight lies on a rectangular tomb inside a church, hands joined in prayer over the chest. One horizontal sword is carved along the tomb's side, while three swords hang vertically on the wall behind. A colorful stained-glass window depicts figures and a religious scene.

**54. Five of Swords**  
A man in green-and-red clothing stands in the foreground with a mocking smile. He holds one sword and carries two more over his shoulder; another two lie on the ground. Behind him, two defeated-looking men walk toward the sea with lowered heads. Irregular gray-white clouds cover the sky.

**55. Six of Swords**  
A narrow wooden boat crosses gray-blue water. A standing man at the stern propels it with a long pole. A hooded woman sits with her head bowed beside a small child. Six long swords stand vertically in a neat row at the bow. Calm water and low-lying land appear ahead.

**56. Seven of Swords**  
A man in bright red-and-yellow clothing tiptoes away from a military camp, glancing backward. He carries five upright swords in his arms, leaving two swords planted in the ground. Several yellow tents and a distant gathering of people appear behind him.

**57. Eight of Swords**  
A woman in a long red dress stands on muddy ground, blindfolded with white cloth and tightly bound around her torso and arms with rope. Eight upright swords surround her like an incomplete fence. A gray castle stands on a distant hill beneath an overcast sky.

**58. Nine of Swords**  
A woman sits upright in bed, covering her face with both hands, as though awakened by a nightmare. Nine horizontal swords line the black wall behind her. The bedframe bears carved battle or narrative scenes, while the blanket is patterned with red roses and astrological symbols. The room is dark and oppressive.

**59. Ten of Swords**  
A man lies face down on a desolate shoreline, partially covered by red fabric. Ten silver swords pierce his back, their handles projecting upward. The sky above is nearly black, but a faint golden dawn appears along the distant sea horizon.

**60. Page of Swords**  
A young page stands on a windswept hill, wearing a pink tunic and yellow trousers. He raises a silver sword with both hands, his body facing one direction while his head turns the other. Uneven ground, wind-bent trees, swirling clouds, and flying birds fill the background.

**61. Knight of Swords**  
A knight in silver armor charges forward on a galloping white horse, raising a silver sword in his right hand. His red cloak streams backward in the wind. The horse stretches its legs in full motion. Strong winds bend the trees, while irregular white clouds race across the sky.

**62. Queen of Swords**  
A queen wearing a golden crown, blue gown, and white cloak sits on a stone throne. She holds a sword upright in one hand and extends the other forward, as though issuing a command. Butterfly and winged-figure carvings adorn the throne. A pale-blue sky, broad clouds, and several birds appear behind.

**63. King of Swords**  
A mature king sits upright on a gray throne, wearing a golden crown, blue robe, and red-purple cloak. He holds a slightly tilted, upward-pointing sword in his right hand. Butterflies and crescent-shaped ornaments decorate the throne. Tall trees, flying birds, and a clear sky form the background.

## V. Pentacles (14 Cards)

**64. Ace of Pentacles**  
A giant hand emerges from a white cloud on the right, holding a golden disk engraved with a black pentagram. Below lies a lush green garden with white lilies and other flowers. A pathway leads through a plant-covered arch toward distant blue mountains.

**65. Two of Pentacles**  
A young man wearing a red tunic, dark leggings, and tall hat stands near the coast, juggling two golden pentacles. A green ribbon connects the disks, forming a horizontal infinity symbol (∞). He appears to dance while two ships rise and fall on enormous waves in the stormy sea behind.

**66. Three of Pentacles**  
Inside a Gothic church or building, a craftsman stands on a wooden platform, carving a stone wall. Two figures observe him: one wears religious robes, while the other holds an architectural drawing or plan. Three golden pentacles form a triangular arrangement within the stone arch overhead.

**67. Four of Pentacles**  
A crowned man sits rigidly on a stone bench, wearing a red robe and blue outer garment. One golden pentacle rests on his head, another is tightly clutched against his chest, and two more are firmly pinned beneath his feet. A dense cityscape of buildings and towers fills the background.

**68. Five of Pentacles**  
Two ragged figures struggle through snowy streets. One man walks with crutches and has a bandaged leg; the other huddles beneath worn clothing against the cold. They pass a large stained-glass window glowing with warm yellow light. Five golden pentacles form a tree-like pattern within the glass.

**69. Six of Pentacles**  
A wealthy man in a red robe and matching hat stands at the center, distributing coins to two kneeling beggars. He gives money with his right hand and holds a small golden balance scale in the other. Six golden pentacles appear above, with gray buildings and a town in the background.

**70. Seven of Pentacles**  
A young farmer wearing a blue tunic, orange trousers, and red boots leans forward with both hands resting on a long wooden staff. He studies a lush green plant bearing six golden pentacles, while a seventh lies near the ground. Rolling green farmland stretches beneath a pale-yellow sky.

**71. Eight of Pentacles**  
A young craftsman in a blue tunic, red trousers, and dark apron sits on a wooden bench, carefully engraving a golden pentacle with tools. Six finished pentacles hang on a nearby wooden post; he works on a seventh, while another lies on the ground. A gray city appears in the distance.

**72. Nine of Pentacles**  
A woman in an elegant yellow gown patterned with red flowers stands in a vineyard. A trained falcon perches on her gloved hand. Fruit-laden grapevines surround her, with nine golden pentacles distributed among the foliage. A tiny snail crawls near her feet, while an estate and hills appear in the distance.

**73. Ten of Pentacles**  
At the entrance of a wealthy family estate, a white-haired elder wearing an ornate patterned robe sits beside two dogs, one being petted by a child. A man and woman stand near an archway, with the child nearby. Ten golden pentacles form a pattern resembling the Kabbalistic Tree of Life. Detailed carvings, banners, and columns decorate the architecture.

**74. Page of Pentacles**  
A young page wearing a green robe, red hat, and yellow boots stands on grassy land. He raises a golden pentacle with both hands and studies it intently, as though examining something precious. Small blooming flowers, trees, hills, broad fields, and distant mountains surround him.

**75. Knight of Pentacles**  
A knight in silver armor sits on a large, powerful black horse, holding a golden pentacle steadily before him. Green foliage decorates his helmet. The horse stands motionless with its head slightly lowered. Behind them stretch golden agricultural fields with orderly furrows and distant hills.

**76. Queen of Pentacles**  
A crowned queen in a red robe sits on an intricately carved stone throne, gently holding a large golden pentacle with both hands and gazing down at it. Plant, angel, and goat-head motifs decorate the throne. Lush roses, vines, fruit trees, and grass surround her. A small brown rabbit hides near her feet, with blue mountains beyond.

**77. King of Pentacles**  
A mature crowned king sits on a massive stone throne, wearing a luxurious robe embroidered with grapevines, leaves, and fruit. He holds a golden scepter in one hand and rests the other on a large golden pentacle. Bull-head sculptures decorate the throne, surrounded by vines, flowers, and greenery. Ornamental plants cover the area near his feet, with a magnificent castle or palace behind.

These describe the upright card illustrations. Reversed cards use the same artwork turned upside down; their interpretations may differ, but the depicted objects remain unchanged.
