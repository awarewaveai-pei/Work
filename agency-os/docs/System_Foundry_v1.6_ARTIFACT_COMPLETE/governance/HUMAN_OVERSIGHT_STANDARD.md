# Human Oversight Standard

## Principle
Human-in-the-loop is not satisfied by placing a button after an opaque recommendation.

## Meaningful review requires
- reviewer identity and authority
- decision scope
- evidence and provenance
- uncertainty/unknowns/disagreement
- alternatives and expected consequences
- recommended controls
- ability to reject, defer, request evidence, or choose an alternative where policy allows
- durable decision/audit record

## Mandatory use
Human approval is required when mandated by risk classification, law/policy, irreversible action, unresolved high-impact uncertainty, or Foundry stage gates.

## Anti-rubber-stamp control
For high-impact workflows the reviewer interface must not preclude dissent, hide alternatives, or present model/agent consensus as fact.
