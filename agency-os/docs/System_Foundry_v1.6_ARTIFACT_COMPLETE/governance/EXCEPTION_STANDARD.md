# Exception / Workaround Standard

Exceptions are temporary controlled deviations. They MUST be visible, owned and self-expiring.

Required: id, violated rule, business/technical rationale, risk tier, scope, owner, approver, start, expiry, compensating controls, monitoring, evidence, removal task, rollback, and permanent target-state fix.

No indefinite exception. Renewal requires fresh evidence and approval at least equal to original authority.
