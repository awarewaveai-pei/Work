# How to Customize Schema Markup for WooCommerce Products

			
			

	

In this Knowledgebase article, we’ll walk you through how to customize [Schema Markup](https://rankmath.com/blog/schema-markup/) for WooCommerce Products. Schema markup is an essential part of the SEO of your e-commerce sites.

Schema markup provides a way to ensure that the data being pulled from your site is structured to make it easy for Google and other search engines to read and understand. This allows website owners to improve the SERP listings.

## Table of Contents

- [Edit Your WooCommerce Product](#edit-woocommerce)

- [Navigate to the Schema Settings for This Product](#navigate-to-schema-settings)

- [Open Rank Math’s Schema Builder](#schema-builder)

- [Choose the Schema for Your Product](#choose-schema)

## 1  Edit Your WooCommerce Product

To Customise the Product Schema, first head over to the edit Product for that by clicking **Edit**as shown below:

![Edit the Product](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202426%20344'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Navigate to the Schema Settings for This Product

Go to the Schema Settings by clicking on the **Schema tab** and clicking on Schema Generator.

![Click on Schema Generator](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201636%20525'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Open Rank Math’s Schema Builder

Once you click on the Schema Generator, Rank Math’s Schema Builder will open up as shown below:

![Available Schema types in Schema Generator](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201305%201652'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Choose the Schema for Your Product

Choose the Schema for your Product based on the information you enter into WooCommerce. Then click on Save for this Post. The Schema will then be shown in the Schema tab of Rank Math SEO.

![Use WooCommerce Product Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201311%20406'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also Customise your Schema by clicking on the Custom Schema Tab and creating a new one.

![Open Custom Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201313%20704'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

That’s how you can add Schema Markup for WooCommerce Products.
