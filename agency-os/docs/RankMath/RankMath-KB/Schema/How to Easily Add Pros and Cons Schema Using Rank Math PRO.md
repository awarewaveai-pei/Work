## 1  What Is Pros and Cons Structured Data?

Pros and Cons of a product is a popular way to help readers decide whether to buy. As of now, Google has added pros and cons structured data support for editorial reviews.

These snippets are eligible for the following languages: Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, and Turkish.

This structured data is for editorial product review pages only, not for user-generated reviews and merchant product reviews.

## 2  Why Should You Add the Pros and Cons Structured Data?

Adding pros and cons helps your editorial review pages with all the enhancements in SERPs and might rank higher in the long run.

As we know, rich snippets open up great opportunities to attract new readers for better search visibility, and the pros and cons information regarding a specific product always help the readers in their purchasing decision.

If you don’t provide the pros and cons structured data, then Google might try to generate useful information automatically. However, priority is given to the pages that add the pros and cons structured data.

## 3  How to Add a Pros and Cons Schema

Before we begin, enable the **Schema** module by navigating to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard** from your WordPress dashboard.

![enable Schema module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201820%20702'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.1  Edit Post

To add the pros and cons Schema to your post/page, navigate to **Posts → All Posts** from your WordPress dashboard, hover over the specific post, and click on the **Edit** option, as shown below.

![edit posts](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201118%20834'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Navigate to Rank Math’s Schema Tab

Select the Rank Math SEO icon at the top of the post-editing screen.

![Rank Math SEO icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20954%20130'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In Rank Math’s meta-box, click on the **Schema** tab. Next, click on the **Schema Generator** button to add the Schema type.

![Schema tab](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201240%201122'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Choose the Schema Type

You can add the pros and cons structured data to the following types of articles:

- Reviewing a [Product](https://rankmath.com/kb/product-schema/) or affiliate product.

- Writing about a [Book](https://rankmath.com/kb/book-schema/).

- When you’re writing about a [Recipe](https://rankmath.com/kb/recipe-schema/).

- When reviewing or writing about [Software Applications](https://rankmath.com/kb/software-schema/).

- When reviewing or selling a [Course](https://rankmath.com/kb/course-schema/).

- Writing content about an [Event](https://rankmath.com/kb/event-schema/).

- Reviewing a [Movie](https://rankmath.com/kb/movie-schema/).

**Note:**Google has phased out support for the Course Schema type, and no longer displays a rich result for it.

We’ve chosen the Software Schema type as an example by clicking on **Use**.

![Select the Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201304%201288'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

For further information, refer to our dedicated KB article, [Software Schema Type](https://rankmath.com/kb/software-schema/).

### 3.4  Add Pros and Cons****[PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

Add pros and cons lists and ensure that one item is added per line. As per [Google’s guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product#pros-cons-guidelines), there must be at least two positive or negative statements, consisting of a minimum of two positive notes, two negative notes, or one of each positive and negative note.

![Add Pros and Cons](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201300%201309'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, click on **Save for this Post**. Then save the post as you normally do by clicking **Publish** for the newly created post or **Update**button to reflect the changes.

### 3.5  Check the Schema

Next, you can check the Schema using [Google’s Rich Results Testing Tool](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) or click on the eye icon in **Rank Math’s Schema in Use**, as shown below.

![check the Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20588%20846'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The **Schema Builder** screen will appear; click on the **Test with Google** to check the Schema.

![Test with Google](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201306%20750'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And, in just a few clicks, you’ll be redirected to Google’s Rich Results Testing Tool, and you can [test whether the Schema was added correctly](https://rankmath.com/kb/rich-results-test/).

And that’s it! We hope you’ve understood how you can add the pros and cons Schema to your product review pages easily. If you still have questions, feel free to [contact our support team](https://rankmath.com/support/); we’re always here to help.
