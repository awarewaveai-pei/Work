# How to Choose the Right Schema for Your Content

			
			

	

Are you confused about the Schema to include in your content? Don’t worry, you’re not alone. With the hundreds of Schema Markups out there, it is normal to be overwhelmed when choosing the right one to use.

Considering that search engines use Schema Markups to understand your content better, it is important that you use the appropriate Schema. Schema Markups are also a requirement when you want your content to appear in rich results, as shown below.

![Schemas are a requirement if you want your content to appear in rich results](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Schemas-are-a-requirement-if-you-want-your-content-to-appear-in-rich-results.png)

In this article, we will guide you through the process of selecting the appropriate Schema for your content.

## 1  Guidelines on Selecting the Appropriate Schema

[Google’s John Mueller](https://youtu.be/px6UBLhWKBk?si=Y5H7lQkhdztra6sy&t=2235) says you should select your Schema Markups based on two factors. The first is the primary intent of the content. The other is whether Google can merge the Schema Markups for display in rich results. We will provide more information on both below.

### 1.1  The Primary Intent of the Content

The intent of the content is the first point to consider when selecting a Schema. For example, a blog post containing instructions on preparing a dish should have a `Recipe` Schema. The post could have a `HowTo` Schema, but the `Recipe` Schema better represents the intent.

![The intent of the content is the first point to consider when selecting a Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20356'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2  Whether the Schema Markups Can Be Merged

Some Schema Markups can be merged into a single rich result. For example, the `Review` Schema can be merged with the `Recipe` Schema. When both Schema Markups are merged, the recipe rich result will display the reviews left by other readers.

![Example of recipe with reviews](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Thankfully, with Rank Math, you don’t need to bother about what Schema Markups can be merged. Rank Math automatically merges your Schema Markups, allowing you to comply with Google guidelines and making your content eligible for rich results.

## 3  How to Select Your Primary Schema

You will encounter scenarios where you have to include multiple Schema Markups on a post or page. In this case, you should select one of the Schema Markups as your primary Schema.

To select a primary Schema, navigate to the post or page and click the **Rank Math SEO** icon, as shown below.

![Click the Rank Math SEO icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20566'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click the **Schema**icon.

![Click the Schema icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20582'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, click **Schema Generator**, as shown below.

![Click Schema Generator](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20617'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Schema in Use field will display the Schema Markups on the post or page. Click on the radio button to select the corresponding Schema as the primary Schema.

![Select the primary Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  How to Validate a Schema

After adding a Schema to your content, you will want to validate it to ensure it is correctly set up. Below, we have provided the different methods of validating your Schema using Google, Bing, and Schema.org.

### 

- [Using Google Rich Results Test Tool](#using-google-rich-results-test-tool)

- [Using Bing Webmaster Tools](#using-bing-webmaster-tools)

- [Using Schema.org Validator](#using-schemaorg-validator)

### 4.1  Using Google Rich Results Test Tool

Head to the post or page and navigate to the **Rank Math SEO**icon → **Schema** icon, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Schema icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, navigate to the applicable Schema and click the **View** icon.

![Click the Inspect icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click **Test with Google**.

![Click Test with Google](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Then, click **TEST CODE**, as shown below.

![Click Test Code](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Schema Markups in the content will be displayed, as shown below.

![The Schemas in the content will be displayed](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.2  Using Bing Webmaster Tools

To validate a Schema with Bing, head to the [Bing URL Inspection Tool](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/urlinspection) (sign-in is required) and enter your URL into the provided field. Once done, click **Inspect**.

![Enter your URL into the provided field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click **Live URL**→ **Markup types found** → **Json-LD**, as shown below.

![Click Live URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Schema Markups in the content will be displayed. You can now review the Schema Markups to ensure they are correctly set up.

![The Schema in the content will be displayed](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.3  Using Schema.org Validator

Head to the [Schema.org Validator](https://validator.schema.org/) and enter your URL into the FETCH URL field. Once done, click **RUN TEST**.

![Enter your URL into the FETCH URL field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20664'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The validator will return with the details of the Schema Markups in your content.

![The validator will return with the details of the Schemas in your content](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20664'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In conclusion, it is important to consider the primary intent of your content before deciding on what Schema to use. And, of course, if you have multiple Schema Markups and you’re looking to appear in rich results, make sure to set a supported Schema as your primary Schema.

By doing so, you can enhance your content’s visibility and improve your chances of appearing in rich results.
