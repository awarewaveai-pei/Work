# How to Use Product Schema for WooCommerce Products

			
			

	

In this tutorial, we’ll walk you through how to use the Product Schema type on products you create using WooCommerce – let’s get started.

WooCommerce Products are easier to [optimize for SEO](https://rankmath.com/blog/woocommerce-seo/) with Rank Math. Hence we recommend using the WooCommerce Product Schema Type for your products. However, note that the WooCommerce Product Schema Type is only available for WooCommerce Products.

## Table of Contents

- [How to Add WooCommerce Product Schema Type?](#add-woocommerce-product-schema)

- [Configuring WooCommerce Product Schema](#configuring-wc-product-schema)

- [Open Graph Meta Tags for Products](#og-tags)

## 1  How to Add WooCommerce Product Schema Type?

Before we begin, you’ll need to ensure that Rank Math’s Schema Module has been enabled on your website by navigating to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard**in your WordPress admin area.

![Enable Schema (Structured Data) module](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Enable-Schema-module-7.png)

### 1.1  Edit Your Product

To add the WooCommerce Product Schema Type, first head over to your Product and click on **Edit,** as shown below.

![Edit Product](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202082%20494'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2  Navigate to the Schema Settings for the Product

Go to the Schema Settings by clicking on the **Schema tab** and then click on **Schema Generator.**

![Click on Schema Generator](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201696%20444'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.3  Navigate to the Schema Builder

Find the WooCommerce Product Schema from the list and click on **Use** to open the Schema Builder.

![Use WooCommerce Product Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201311%20406'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.4  Save the Schema Type

In the Schema Builder, click on **Save for this Post**.

![Click on Save for this Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201306%20569'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Configuring WooCommerce Product Schema

Rank Math’s WooCommerce integration**automatically pulls the product data**that has been entered in the WooCommerce Product created in the WordPress admin area. This makes the entire process easier and even faster.

Here are the various WooCommerce options that you need to configure to be included in the Product Schema.

### 2.1  Name

Enter the Product Name for your WooCommerce product as shown below.

![Product name in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201633%20297'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  SKU

SKU (**S**tock **K**eeping **U**nit) is a unique identifier with reference to your product. It helps you keep track of this item in your inventory. You can add an SKU in your WooCommerce Product in the **Inventory** tab as you head over to the **Product data** section.

![SKU in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201283%20499'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  URL

The permalink of the WooCommerce Product is added as the URL. When you click the **Edit** option, you can change the permalink of the product.

![Edit permalink of WooCommerce product](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20151'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Description

You can add more details of the product in the **Product short description** field in WooCommerce. This will be added as a description to the Product Schema.

![Including description for the product in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20962%20396'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Category

For category, you can select the Product Category from the right sidebar in your WooCommerce Product editor. In case, if multiple product categories are chosen, then you can choose the primary term from the radio button displayed on the right side of the product categories, as shown below.

![Primary product category term in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20628%20661'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Image

To include the image in the Product Schema, Rank Math considers the product image as well as product gallery images that can be added from the right sidebar in your WooCommerce Product Editor.

**Note:**The images in your product gallery will only be added to your Product Schema if you upload a product image.

![Adding Product Image and Product Gallery in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20601'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Offers → Price

The price at which your customers can buy your product or service. The price can be configured for the WooCommerce product under the Product data section.

In the **General** tab, you can notice a few options for pricing. The regular price is the price at which your products can be purchased when there is no sale.

When there is no sale price set, then Rank Math will consider the **Regular price** for the price in the Product Schema.

![Regular price of product in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201058%20502'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

When a sale is live for the product, then Rank Math will include the **Sale Price** as the price for the Product Schema. As you can see from the image below, both the regular and sale prices are provided. In this case, Rank Math will use the Sale price as the price.

![Sale price for Product in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201061%20502'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Offers → Price Currency

The price currency is the same as the one that you have configured for your WooCommerce store under **WooCommerce → Settings → General**. Here under the **Currency options**, you can change the **Currency**.

The Price Currency is added to the Product Schema only if you have set a price for the product.

![Choose product currency in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201261%20683'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Offers → Price Valid Until

The Price Valid Until describes the last date beyond which the sale price is no longer valid. Once you have set a Sale price for your product, you can click the **Schedule** option and set an end date for the sale price.

![Sale Price to date set in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201059%20499'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Since the Price Valid Until is Google’s recommended property for Product Schema, you will see a warning in your search console and Google’s rich results testing tool, if not set. To prevent the warnings, Rank Math adds a Price Valid Until date automatically if no date is added. The last date of the following year will be set as the default value for Price Valid Until.

### 2.10  Availability

The Availability of the product can be set under the Product data. In the Inventory tab, you can set the **Stock Status**, and Rank Math will include the appropriate value for the Availability Schema property.

**InStock:** This value is set for the Schema property when you choose **In Stock**.

**OutStock**: This value is set for the Schema property when you choose **Out of Stock**.

**BackOrder**: This value is set for the Schema property when you choose **On Backorder**.

![Stock status in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201297%20506'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.11  Item Condition

`NewCondition` is the default value that is added by Rank Math for the Item Condition property.

### 2.12  GTIN

Rank Math PRO users will have the option to choose the GTIN key used in the Product Schema. The GTIN key is configured under **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → WooCommerce → Global Identifier**.

![Global Identifier in Rank Math General Settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20358'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

When you navigate to the **Inventory**tab under the **Product data** section of WooCommerce, you will see the GTIN, UPC, EAN, or ISBN options. It lets you add the GTIN value for the GTIN key you configured earlier in the Rank Math Settings. For FREE users, the default GTIN key will be used.

![Adding GTIN for product in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201290%20500'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.13  Weight → Value

If your product is a physical product, then you can add its weight. Under the Product data, you can switch to the **Shipping** tab and enter the **Weight** here.

![Adding product weight in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20964%20383'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.14  Weight → Unitcode

The unit at which the weight is being measured. This setting is configured under **WooCommerce → Settings → Products → General**. Here, you can change the **Weight Unit** option under the **Measurements** section.

![Weight unit in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201265%20644'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.15  Depth

For physical goods, you can enter the length (or depth) of the product. In the Product data, switch to the **Shipping** tab. Here, you can find various Dimensions fields. Add the value in the **Length** field.

![Adding product length in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20383'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.16  Width

Similar to the Length, you can enter the **Width** of the product in its respective Dimensions field.

![Adding product width in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20965%20385'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.17  Height

Similar to the length and breadth, you can also add the **Height** in the dimensions field for the physical goods.

![Adding product height in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20964%20386'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.18  Unitcode for Height, Width, and Depth

The unit code for Height, Width, and Depth are all configured from the WooCommerce settings. To set the dimensions unit, head over to **WooCommerce → Settings → Products → General**. Under the **Measurements** section, change the **Dimensions unit**.

![Dimensions unit in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201264%20644'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.19  AggregateRating

AggregateRating is automatically added by Rank Math when your customers leave reviews for your product.

- **Rating Value**: Indicates the aggregate rating value

- **Rating Count**: Total number of ratings

- **Review Count**: Total number of reviews

![Review to be added in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201672%201008'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.20  Reviews

Rank Math will automatically include the individual reviews left by the customers. For each approved review, the Schema will include,

- **ID:** Link to the review comment

- **Description**: Review left by the customers

- **Date Published**: Date at which the customer has left the review

- **Review Rating → Rating Value:** The rating left by the customer for the product

- **Review Rating → Best Rating:** Rank Math sets it to a default value of 5

- **Review Rating → Worst Rating:** Rank Math sets it to default value of 1

- **Author Name:**Name of the customer who has left the review

### 2.21  Value Added Tax (VAT) Included

Is your product price inclusive or exclusive of VAT? This can be configured as you navigate to **WooCommerce → Settings →** **General**. On this page, under the General Options, you can choose to **Enable Tax rates and Calculations** option.

![Enable Tax rates and calculations](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20643'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now you can configure the Tax options in your WooCommerce store [with this detailed guide](https://woocommerce.com/document/setting-up-taxes-in-woocommerce/).

### 2.22  Brand

You can choose to [include the Brand for your products](https://rankmath.com/kb/woocommerce-brand-names/) under **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → WooCommerce**. In the **Select Brand** option here, you can choose to use a product taxonomy for adding the Brand to your Product Schema.

![Select Brand for WooCommerce products in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201004%20210'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Rank Math PRO users will have the option to add Custom Brand value here. When the **Custom** option is selected, users will see an option to add the [custom Brand name](https://rankmath.com/kb/woocommerce-custom-brand-schema/) below.

![Select custom brand for WooCommerce products](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201012%20319'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

That’s it! Then, simply update the page as you normally would after making a change or click **Publish**if this is a newly created page. You can then check your Schema with the help of [Google’s Rich Results Testing Tool](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results).

### 2.23  Variations [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

If your product is available in multiple variations, for example, if you sell a t-shirt available in different colors and sizes, you can create a dropdown menu to allow your customers choose the colors and sizes they want.

![Sample of the variations on the product page](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20603'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To get started, head to the Product data metabox and click the **Variable product** dropdown, as shown below.

![Select Variable product](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20590'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click **Variations**.

![Click Variations](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20517'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**If you haven’t previously created attributes for your product, you will be required to create them to proceed.

To create your attributes, click the **Attributes**tab and enter the details of your attribute into the Name and Value(s) fields. You can create multiple attributes by clicking **Add new**. Once done, click **Save attributes**.

![Create attributes and click Save attributes](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20602'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Optionally, if you prefer using your existing attributes, click the **Add existing**dropdown to select the existing attribute. Once done, click the **Value(s)** dropdown and select the values to include in the attribute. Then, click **Save attributes**, as shown below.

![Select attributes and click Save attributes](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20603'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After creating or selecting your attributes, return to **Variations**and click**Generate variations**. Then click **OK**to create your variables.

![Click Generate variations](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20589'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, click **Edit** and fill in the required fields. Make sure to fill in the **Regular price ($)**field.

![Fill in the regular price field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20600'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, scroll down and click **Save changes**. You can then publish or update the product page.

![Scroll down and click Save Changes](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20607'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After creating your variants, you can review your Schema Markup by heading to the **Rank Math SEO**metabox and clicking **Schema**, as shown below.

![Head to the Rank Math SEO metabox and click Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20599'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, navigate to the WooCommerce Product Schema and click the **View**icon.

![Click the View icon of the WooCommerce Product Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20602'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, click **Ctrl+F** on your keyboard and search for **productgroup**. You will be presented with the `productGroupID`, `variesBy`, and `hasVariant` properties of the Schema Markup, as shown below.

![Review the details of your variables](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20631'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Open Graph Meta Tags for Products

Open Graph Meta Tags are small snippets of code that describe what the content of a web page is and allow you to have control over how the display should be when it is shared on social networking sites like Facebook and Twitter.

There are several [different open graph meta tags](https://rankmath.com/kb/open-graph-meta-tags/) that you can use to make your website or product easier to find. Each tag provides additional information about your site or product, helping to improve its visibility. Rank Math makes it easier for you by picking up these data based on what you’ve configured for WooCommerce Product Schema.

Some of Open Graph Tags for Product Schema include:

### 3.1  Product Price Amount

This particular meta tag `product:price:amount` displays the price at which the product is available for sale. This price is the same as what is configured for [Offer Price](#offers-price) in the Product Schema.

### 3.2  Product Price Currency

The meta tag `product:price:currency` shows the currency in which you are selling the product. By default, it will be set in $ (USD). If you want to change it, navigate to **WooCommerce** → **Settings** → **General**, scroll down and change it under **Currency options**.

![Change Currency in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201261%20683'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Product Availability

The **`product:availability`** meta tag displays the stock status that you have configured previously in the Inventory tab of Product data. The available stock statuses include **In stock, Out of stock, and On backorder**.

![Product availability](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201302%20505'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Product Retailer Item ID [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

The **`product:retailer_item_id`** is the legal entity-specific identification of the released product. For example, marvel_tshirt_001.

Rank Math uses the SKU that you’ve configured for the product, when you have the **Manage Stock** option selected.

![Manage stock and SKU in WooCommerce](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201302%20600'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Product Brand

The `product:brand` meta tag includes the brand name of the product you’re selling. Rank Math includes this brand name based on what you’ve configured under **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → WooCommerce → Select Brand**.

![Select Brand](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201004%20210'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
