# Podcast Schema

			
			

	

Rank Math PRO includes a dedicated module for podcasts with all the essentials to create your own podcast. The built-in Podcast RSS feed generator of Rank Math aids you with creating one for your podcast automatically, which is compatible with Google Podcasts, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, etc.

Creating a podcast couldn’t be easier as Rank Math takes care of everything from creating your podcast to publishing and then submitting it to Google Podcast, without the need for any external plugin. To give you a competitive advantage in search results, Rank Math also offers Podcast Schema along with other [Schema types](https://rankmath.com/kb/rich-snippets/), so your site could feature rich results that Google offers for podcasts.

![TED talks podcast rich result](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/TED-talks-daily-podcast-rich-result.png)

In this Knowledge Base article, we’ll walk you through how to get started with your own Podcast using Rank Math PRO.

## 1  Podcast Requirements

There are several applications to stream and distribute podcasts like Google Podcasts, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, etc. To become eligible to feature your podcast through these services, you will need to **create a dedicated Podcast RSS Feed and submit it to their respective platforms**.

Once you submit your RSS feed, every time a new podcast episode is released, these platforms will automatically fetch and display it on their platform.

Apart from the technicalities in creating these Podcast RSS feeds, the process sounds simple. Rank Math makes it even easier by handling the RSS feeds with a [Google-compliant RSS feed generator](https://support.google.com/podcast-publishers/answer/9889544?hl=en) and makes podcasting a breeze.

## 2  Enable the Podcast Module

To get started, you need to enable the Podcast module from **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO**, as shown below.

![Podcast Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201381%20519'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Since the Podcast module makes use of the Schema, you need to ensure that the **Schema (structured data) module** is also enabled.

## 3  Configuring Podcast Settings

Now that you’ve enabled the Podcast module, you should configure the Podcast settings. If you’ve already configured the settings, head over to the [next section to create Podcasts](#add-podcast-episode-schema).

To configure the podcast settings, you can simply click the **Settings** button in the Podcast module.

![Open Podcast settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201381%20519'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Alternatively, you can head over to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → General Settings → Podcast**.

![Navigate to Podcast settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202440%201336'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can find the following set of options available to configure here:

![Podcast settings in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201016%201692'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, let’s look at each of these options one by one.

### 3.1  Podcast Name

Enter the name of the podcast in this field. By default, Rank Math adds your site name as the Podcast Name, but you can always change it.

![Podcast Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201522%20383'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Podcast Description

In the next field, enter the description for your podcast here. Rank Math also supports adding variables and enables you to create a dynamic description.

To add one, click the down arrow in the lower-right corner, and the list of all available variables will be displayed. You can click and add any required variable from here.

![Podcast description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201527%20519'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Owner Name

Here, enter the name of the podcast owner.

![Owner Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201537%20293'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Owner Email

Add the email of the podcast owner here in this field.

![Owner Email](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201531%20330'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Podcast Category

This field allows you to select a category where your podcast better fits in. If your podcast does not belong to any of the listed podcast categories, then choose None.

![Podcast category](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20393'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.6  Podcast Image

In the next field, select an image for the podcast. A squared image is the preferred one by search engines.

![Podcast Image](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201532%201088'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.7  Tracking Prefix

If you want to make use of any tracking service Chartable, Podsights, Podtrac, or any other, then enter the tracking prefix here. For instance, `https://trackingurl.com/prefix/`

Rank Math would add this prefix in front of your Podcast URL in the RSS feed.

![Tracking Prefix](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20323'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.8  Is Explicit

Does your podcast include explicit language or adult content? If so, enable this option.

![Is Explicit](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.9  Copyright Text

In the last field, enter the details of the Copyright text.

![Copyright Text](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201522%20294'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once you’re done configuring these options, click the **Save Changes** button at the bottom of the page.

![Click Save Changes button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201540%20207'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  How to Add Podcast Episode Schema to Your Posts/Pages?

Now that you have configured the basic details of the Podcast, you can now start adding podcast episodes to your website. To do so, follow the steps we walk you through below.

### 4.1  Edit Your Post/Page

Open the post/page where you want to add the Podcast by clicking the **Edit** option as shown below.

![Edit your post or page](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20289'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.2  Open Rank Math in the Gutenberg Sidebar

Next, click on the **Rank Math SEO icon** on the top-right of the screen to open the SEO settings for the page.

![Click Rank Math SEO icon in Gutenberg Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201270%20511'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Alternatively, you can click the three vertical dots in the right corner of the page and select **Rank Math** from the **Plugins** section, as shown below.

![Open Rank Math from Plugins menu](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201192%20961'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.3  Navigate to the Schema Settings

Click on the **Schema** tab in the Rank Math SEO Meta Box and then click on the **Schema Generator**.

![Open Schema tab from Rank Math metabox](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201260%20535'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.4  Open the Podcast Schema Builder

In the Schema Generator that appears on your screen, click on **Use** against the **Podcast Episode** as shown below.

![Use Podcast Episode Schema](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201278%201665'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Schema Builder will then appear with all the available settings.

![Podcast Episode Schema builder](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20640'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 5  Podcast Episode Schema Setup & Available Options

Here are all the settings available in the Podcast Episode Schema to configure. Let’s discuss each one of them in detail.

**Note:** The fields marked with a red asterisk are required for the PodcastEpisode Schema, and the rest of the fields are optional.

### 5.1  Episode Name (Required)

Add the name of the episode here. By default, Rank Math will include the title of the post here.

![Episode Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201266%20175'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.2  Episode Description

Enter a description of the Podcast Episode here without any HTML tags. By default, Rank Math will include the SEO description configured for the post.

![Episode Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201242%20386'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.3  Shortcode

This field displays the shortcode created for the Podcast Episode. You can [paste this shortcode](https://rankmath.com/blog/how-to-add-shortcode-in-wordpress/) inside your post to display the Podcast details.

![Shortcode](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201229%20321'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.4  Author Name (Required)

Enter the name of the author here. If left empty, then Rank Math will make use of the author of the current post.

![Author name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201235%20184'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.5  Episode Duration

Enter the episode duration here in the ISO 8601 format. You can [refer to this tutorial](https://rankmath.com/kb/iso-8601-format/) to convert standard time to ISO 8601 format.

![Episode Duration](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201238%20276'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.6  Episode URL

Enter the URL of the Episode here in this field. By default, Rank Math would automatically include the current post URL here.

![Episode URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201228%20226'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.7  Episode Image URL

Add the Image URL of the podcast episode here. If left empty, then Rank Math will make use of the Podcast channel image that we’ve configured earlier in the Rank Math Settings.

![Episode Image URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201250%20238'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.8  Is Episode Family Friendly?

Is your podcast episode family-friendly? Then leave the toggle enabled.

![Is Episode Family Friendly](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201263%20128'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.9  Audio File URL (Required)

Add the URL of the podcast episode audio file here. This audio file could either be on your WordPress website, your own server or anywhere else. Hence to help Rank Math and podcasting services to find and play the audio file correctly, you need to add a fully-qualified URL. For example: `https://rankmath.local/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/s2-e5-audio.mp3`

![Audio File URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201250%20311'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.10  Season Number

In the next field, add the season number to which the current episode belongs to.

![Season Number](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201250%20240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.11  Season Name

Here, you can add the name of the Podcast season.

![Season Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201233%20180'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.12  Season URL

In the next field, enter the URL for the podcast season, if any.

![Season URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201256%20178'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.13  Episode Number

In the last field, enter the current episode number for the podcast.

![Episode Number](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201261%20242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 6  How to Display Podcast Data on Frontend?

Once you’re done making the changes to the Schema data, click the **Save for this Post** option that appears at the bottom.

![Save for this Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201267%20367'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

As of now, we’ve only added the Schema data to the page. For the podcast to appear on the frontend for the viewers, you need to add the Rank Math Schema Block or the Rank Math Schema shortcode, as explained below.

### 6.1  Display Podcast on Gutenberg Editor

In the Gutenberg Editor, click the **plus** icon that appears at the end to add a new block. A list of all the available blocks would then be displayed, from which you can search and select the **Schema by Rank Math** block.

![Rank Math Schema block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20715'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Or, you can simply type `/schema` in a new line and choose the **Schema by Rank Math** block from the list of blocks.

![Schema by Rank Math block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20563'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now the Schema block would display the podcast details along with the podcast player, as shown below.

![Podcast Schema block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20812'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 6.2  Display Podcast on Classic Editor, Divi, Elementor:

In the case of editors such as Classic Editor, Divi, and Elementor, you’ll need to copy the [Podcast Schema shortcode](#shortcode) that was shown in the Schema Generator and paste them to the content of the page.

![Rank Math Schema shortcode](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20704'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Here is how the podcast would appear on the frontend.

![Podcast Schema block on frontend](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201191%20771'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 7  Podcast RSS Feed

As we mentioned earlier, Rank Math automatically creates a Podcast RSS feed. To view the Podcast RSS feed, you can head over to **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → Podcast** and find the Podcast RSS Feed here.

![Podcast RSS feed](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201100%20435'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now you can submit this RSS feed to any Podcast service of your choice. As soon as you create a new Podcast Episode, Rank Math will automatically ping your RSS feed to Google.
