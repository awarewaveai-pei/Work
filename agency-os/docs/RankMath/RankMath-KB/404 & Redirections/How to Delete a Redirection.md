# How to Delete a Redirection

			
			

	

Redirections are a powerful way to improve the user experience of your visitors and maintain your [link juice](https://rankmath.com/seo-glossary/link-equity/). But after a while, you may no longer need a redirection or may have created a new one to replace it. In both cases, the now-obsolete redirection has to go.

So the question is: How do you properly delete a redirection? How do you know when to deactivate a redirection and when to delete it? What happens after you delete a redirection? Is it finally gone, or is there an opportunity for you to restore it in case you need it again?

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll show you how to delete a redirection in Rank Math.

## 1  How to Delete a Redirection in Rank Math

There are a few ways to delete a redirection. The method you use depends on the number of redirections you want to delete and whether you wish to restore them later.

Whichever method you settle for, the first step is to navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Redirections**from your WordPress Dashboard.

![Navigate to Redirections in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20638%20744'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’ll be navigated to the **Redirections** page, as shown below.

![Redirections page in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You might want to either delete a single redirection, delete multiple redirections, or even deactivate it. We’ll discuss all the ways to delete redirections in Rank Math.

### 1.1   How to Delete a Single Redirection

To delete a single redirection, hover over the redirection and click **Trash**,****as shown below.

![Delete a single redirection](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201922%201160'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Your redirection will be deleted, and you can find it in the **Trash**.

![Redirection moved to Trash](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202516%20478'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2   How to Delete Multiple Redirections

To delete multiple redirections on your site, select all the redirections you wish to delete, as shown below. Next, select the **Move to Trash**option from the **Bulk Actions** drop-down menu and click on **Apply**.

![Delete Multiple Redirections](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201930%201002'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The selected redirections will be deleted and moved to the **Trash**.

### 1.3  How to Deactivate a Redirection

You can choose to deactivate a redirection if you want to use it in the future. To deactivate a redirection, hover over it and click **Deactivate**, as shown below.

![Deactivate single redirection](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201914%201004'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To deactivate multiple redirections, select all the redirections you wish to deactivate. Next, choose the **Deactivate** option from the drop-down menu and click **Apply**, as shown below.

![Deactivate multiple redirections](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201624%20898'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The redirections will now be deactivated.

![Redirection deactivated](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201960%201123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Optionally, you can choose to automatically activate or deactivate a redirect at a certain point in time. You can refer to our detailed article on [scheduling redirections in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/setting-up-redirections/#scheduled-activation).

## 2  How to Restore or Permanently Delete a Redirection

Suppose you accidentally delete a redirection or want to reuse the one you already deleted; we have good news! Rank Math moves all your deleted redirections to **Trash**. This means you can quickly restore them if you need them again.

In order to restore or permanently delete your redirections, navigate to **Trash**,****as shown below.

![Delete a redirection](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201932%20996'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Hover over the redirections you want to restore or delete. Click on **Restore** to restore it or **Delete Permanently**to permanently delete the redirection.

![Restore or Permanently Delete a redirection](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201624%201018'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
