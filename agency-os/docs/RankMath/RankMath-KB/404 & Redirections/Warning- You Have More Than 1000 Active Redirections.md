# Warning: You Have More Than 1000 Active Redirections

			
			

	

Rank Math PRO allows you to export or sync your redirections. You can click on **Export to .htaccess**or **Export to Nginx config file**in the **Rank Math SEO → Redirections** section to download your most recent redirections. You can also click on **Sync to .htaccess** to copy your most recent redirections to the .htaccess file on your server.

However, you may receive a warning message if you have more than 1000 active redirections on your site, as shown below.

![Redirections Warning Message](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/redirections-warning-message.jpg)

You’ll see this message as Rank Math only exports 1000 redirections to your .htaccess or Nginx configuration files.

Rank Math has this limit because of the possible SEO issues that may arise if you have too many redirections stored on your server. While you can set up as many redirects as you want, too many will put extra load on your server and reduce your server speed and page load time.

We hope you understand why we don’t sync or export more than 1000 redirections to your server configuration file.
