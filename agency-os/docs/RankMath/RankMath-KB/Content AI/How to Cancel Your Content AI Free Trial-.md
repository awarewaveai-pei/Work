# How to Cancel Your Content AI Free Trial?

			
			

	

When you upgrade to Rank Math PRO, Business, or Agency membership, you will receive a complimentary **15-day free trial** of [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/). Depending on your membership, you’ll have access to the Starter, Creator, or Expert plan.

After the 15-day free trial ends, you will be automatically billed for the upcoming year’s Content AI subscription. However, if you decide that Content AI is not for you, you can cancel the free trial anytime before it expires.

In this knowledgebase tutorial, we’ll walk you through how to cancel your Content AI trial.

## 1  Navigate to Rank Math Dashboard

First, log in to your [Rank Math account](https://rankmath.com/my-account/) and navigate to **Rank Math Dashboard → Subscriptions**

![Open Subscriptions tab in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Open-Subscriptions-tab-in-Rank-Math.jpeg)

## 2  Open Content AI Subscription

In the Subscriptions and Billing page, switch to the [**Content AI subscription** tab](https://rankmath.com/my-account/subscriptions/?tab=cai-subscription).

![Open Content AI subscription](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Open-Content-AI-subscription.jpeg)

## 3  Cancel Trial

Under the Content AI trial subscription, click the **Cancel Trial** button, as shown below.

![Cancel Trial](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Cancel-Trial.jpeg)

## 4  Confirm Cancellation

In the confirmation popup that appears on the screen, choose **Cancel FREE trial** if you’ve decided to cancel the trial.

![Cancel free trial](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20619'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! We hope the tutorial helped you cancel the Content AI free trial. If you wish to give Content AI another try in the future, you can always get started with a [Content AI subscription from here](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) and it includes a 30-day money-back guarantee.

If you have absolutely any questions about our Content AI, please feel free to [open a support ticket here](https://rankmath.com/support/), and our dedicated support team will be more than happy to get back to you.
