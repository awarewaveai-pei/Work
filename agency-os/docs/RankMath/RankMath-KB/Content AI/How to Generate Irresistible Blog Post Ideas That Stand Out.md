## 1  Creating a Blog Post Idea

Let us now discuss how to create a blog post idea using our [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/) tool.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down and enable the **Content AI**module, as shown below.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Blog Post Idea AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select ****Blog Post Idea****. Alternatively, you can choose the Blog Post Idea AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![select the Blog Post Idea AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202518%201228'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Blog Post Idea AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Blog Post Idea AI Tool settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20593'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Blog Post Idea AI Tool

Below, we’ve listed all available options in the Blog Post Idea tool in detail.

### 2.1  Describe Your Industry/Niche

Enter a brief description of your industry/niche in this field so Content AI can do its research for you and generate relevant blog post ideas. This is a required field.

![Describe Your Industry/Niche](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20974%20333'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Audience

The Audience section will contain the details of your target audience. You can type in a description that best describes your audience or select from the dropdown menu that appears when you begin typing. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Tone

The Tone setting allows you to enter the words that best describe your writing voice. You can type in the word that best describes your writing tone or select from the dropdown menu that appears when you begin typing. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Style

In the Style setting, you will enter the format you intend to publish the blog idea you are about to generate. You can enter multiple styles. You can determine an appropriate style by asking yourself:

- “What will the content look like?” Is it a tutorial, a case study, or maybe a list?

- “In what format will the content be presented?” Is it a blog post, a podcast, a video, or a quiz?

![Select Style](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201154%20896'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Here is the complete list of styles available for you to select:

- Listicle

- Tutorial

- Review

- Case Study

- Opinion

- News

- Newsjacking

- Personal

- Story-telling

- Guide

- Research-based

- Interview

- Infographic

- Debate

- Video Blog

- Vlog

- Podcast

- Audio Blog

- Quiz

- Contest

- Poll

- Comparison

### 2.5  Output Language

The Language setting allows you to select the language for the blog post idea. You will click on the Language field and select a language from the dropdown menu that displays. You can only enter one language.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**If the Output Language field already contains a language, you will remove it before entering a new one.

### 2.6  Number of Outputs

Content AI can generate between one and 20 blog post ideas at a go. You can select how many blog post ideas you want it to generate by entering a number into the Number of Outputs field.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

Once you have filled in the required fields, click **Generate**. You will watch Content AI create your blog post ideas in real-time. The suggested blog post ideas will appear in the Output section.

![Generate Blog Post Idea](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want more ideas, click **Generate More**, as shown below.

![click Generate More](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201912%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Insert

After generating your blog post idea, the next step is to select the post ideas you want. To do that, hover over any blog post idea you wish to develop further and click **Insert**, as shown below.

![Copy or Insert blog post ideas](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The blog post idea will be inserted into the Content Editor. You can then develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

## 3  Use Cases of the Blog Post Idea AI Tool

The Blog Post Idea AI tool generates fresh, engaging blog topics tailored to your niche, audience, and writing style, helping you balance creativity with reader interests. Here’s how you can use it to your advantage:

### 3.1  Generating Blog Topics for a Well-Balanced Content Calendar

A strategic content calendar should include a mix of evergreen and seasonal topics to maintain long-term traffic while capitalizing on timely trends. This AI tool helps brainstorm a diverse set of blog ideas that ensure consistency in publishing and audience engagement.

Evergreen topics drive steady, long-term traffic, while seasonal content captures spikes in interest during key times of the year.

![Generating Blog Topics for a Well-Balanced Content Calendar with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Generating Topics for Guest Post Pitches

A strong guest post pitch requires unique, compelling blog topics that align with the target website’s audience and expertise. This AI tool generates fresh, high-value topic ideas that increase the chances of getting accepted by high-authority blogs.

By ensuring the topics are engaging, well-researched, and relevant, this tool helps writers craft pitches that stand out.

![Generating Topics for Guest Post Pitches with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20929'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Creating Content for Business and Brand Authority

If you run a business or are building a personal brand, this AI tool can help generate insightful blog topics that position you as an expert in your industry. Writing authoritative content on industry trends, case studies, or in-depth strategy breakdowns helps establish credibility.

![Creating Content for Business with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20925'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

These types of topics show your expertise, attract potential clients, and boost trust in your brand.

### 3.4  Generating Blog Topics for Niche-Specific Audiences

Some industries require highly specific content that caters to a unique audience. This AI tool ensures that you get blog topics tailored to your niche, whether it’s sustainability, pet care, or gaming.

These topics help you connect with the right audience and create content they genuinely care about.

![Generating Blog Topics for Niche-Specific Audiences with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Generating Blog Topics for E-Commerce Businesses

If you run an online store, creating blog content that supports your products can drive more sales. This AI tool can generate blog ideas that blend storytelling with product promotion, making your content both engaging and conversion-friendly.

![Generating Blog Topics for E-Commerce with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

These types of blogs attract potential customers and subtly guide them toward making a purchase.
