# Resolving Content AI Related Issues

			
			

	

Our Content AI delivers the power of artificial intelligence to your content creation process, allowing you to research, generate, and improve content within seconds, all right inside your WordPress dashboard.

Sometimes, however, Content AI may encounter errors that prevent it from generating your content. Instead, it will return some message about the issue it encountered.

In this knowledgebase article, we will review the error messages you may encounter while using Content AI and show you how to resolve them.

## In this guide, we’ll cover:

- [Content AI Subscription is Not Active](#content-ai-is-not-active)

- [Website is Not Connected to Rank Math](#website-is-not-connected-to-rank-math)

- [Rank Math is Not Updated to the Latest Version](#rank-math-is-not-updated)

- [Sent Too Many Requests in Content AI](#sent-too-many-requests-in-content-ai)

- [Exhausted Content AI Credits](#content-ai-credits-exhausted)

- [Reached Content AI Credits Quota For the Domain](#credits-quota)

- [Content AI is Unable to Generate Content](#content-ai-unable-to-generate-content)

## 1  Content AI Subscription is Not Active

When you click on Content AI from your WordPress dashboard, you may encounter the “To access this Content AI feature, you need to have an active subscription plan.” message, as shown below.

![To access this Content AI feature, you need to have an active subscription plan.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Similarly, you may encounter “You do not have a Content AI plan. Choose your plan” message when you attempt to use the Content AI Assistant in your WordPress editor.

![You do not have a Content AI plan.Choose your plan](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20616'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You are seeing these messages because you do not have an active Content AI plan. To proceed, head to this page to [select a Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/). Once done, you will be able to access Content AI.

## 2  Website is Not Connected to Rank Math

When you click on Content AI from your WordPress dashboard, you may encounter a message that says, “Start using Content AI by connecting your RankMath.com Account.”

![Start using Content AI by connecting your RankMath account.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20619'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Similarly, you may encounter the “Start using Content AI by connecting your Rank Math account. Connect Now” message when you attempt to use the AI Assistant.

![Start using Content AI by connecting your RankMath account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’re seeing these messages because you have not logged into your Rank Math account. Below, we will show you how to log into your Rank Math account.

### 2.1  Click Connect Now

To get started, click the **Connect Now**button or link.

![Click Connect Now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20622'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Activate Rank Math

You will be redirected to the Rank Math activation page. Now, crosscheck the email on the page to ensure it is the same email linked to your account.

If you have multiple Rank Math licenses, select the appropriate license from the License dropdown menu. Once done, click **OK, Activate Now**, as shown below.

![Click OK, Activate Now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20630'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Rank Math is Not Updated to the Latest Version

When you try to access some Content AI features, you may encounter a “Please update the Rank Math SEO plugin to the latest version to use this feature.” message, as shown below.

![Please update the Rank Math SEO plugin to the latest version to use this feature.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20621'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You are seeing this message because you’re using an older version of Rank Math that doesn’t support the feature you’re trying to access. You can resolve this by upgrading to the latest version of Rank Math, as explained below.

### 3.1  Head to Plugins

To update Rank Math, head to **WordPress Dashboard**→**Plugins**.

![Head to Plugins](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20617'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Update Rank Math

Next, click **Update Now**, as shown below.

![Click Update Now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20599'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Rank Math will begin updating to the latest version. Once completed, you will see an “Updated!” success message, as shown below. You can now return to using Content AI.

![You will see an Updated success message](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Sent Too Many Requests in Content AI

Sometimes, you may encounter “Oops! Too many requests in a short time. Please try again after some time.” message while using Content AI.

You are seeing this message because you have assigned too many tasks to Content AI within a short duration. You should wait a minute or two before entering more prompts into Content AI.

## 5  Exhausted Content Feature Uses

While using Content AI, you may encounter a “You’ve used up all available credits from the connected account” error message, as shown below.

If you have exhausted your credits for this month, you can [reset your credits from here](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/).

It is possible that your account has credits, but Content AI might not have checked the available credits yet. You can quickly resolve this by refreshing your Content AI credits.

![You've used up all available credits from the connected account.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

We will show you how to refresh your credits below.

### 5.1  Head to General Settings

To get started, head to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO** **→ General Settings**.

![Head to General Settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20601'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.2  Head to Content AI Settings

Next, scroll down and click **Content AI**.

![Click Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20616'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.3  Refresh Content AI

Now, scroll down and click the refresh icon, as shown below. Once done, your Content AI credits will refresh. You can now continue using Content AI without issues.

![Click the reset icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20616'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 6  Reached Content AI Credits Quota For the Domain

Rank Math Business and Agency users can allocate a Content AI quota for each domain. If a domain reaches or exceeds its assigned quota, you’ll need to allocate more to that site. Let’s show you how to do that step-by-step.

### 6.1  Open Client Management Dashboard

First, log in to your Rank Math account and head over to the [Connected Websites](https://rankmath.com/my-account/) section.

![Click Websites](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20648'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 6.2  Increase the Content AI Quota

Here, you will be presented with a list of the websites connected to your Rank Math account. Now, head to the affected site. In the Content AI Credits settings, click **CHANGE LIMIT**, as shown below.

![Click Change Limit](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20648'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, increase the Content AI credits assigned to the site and click **Save**.

![Increase the Content AI credits assigned to the site and click Save](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The maximum credits assigned to the site will be increased. You can now return to your WordPress dashboard to continue using Content AI.

![The maximum credits assigned to the site](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 7  Content AI is Unable to Generate Content

Content AI may sometimes return a “Could not generate. Please try again later.” message, as shown below.

![Could not generate. Please try again later.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI is returning this message because it identified your prompt as unsafe. You can resolve this by modifying your prompt to ensure it doesn’t contain unsafe words and is not intended to generate content that promotes unsafe actions.
