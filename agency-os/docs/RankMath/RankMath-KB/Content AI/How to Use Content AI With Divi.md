# How to Use Content AI With Divi

			
			

	

The integration of our [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/) in Divi streamlines the content creation process, saving time and effort.

As more businesses use AI to streamline content creation, it’s worth noting that [**47% of users are producing content faster**](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/)**, and 35% are shifting their focus from quantity to quality.**

In this knowledgebase article, we will walk you through how to take advantage of Content AI & its features within Divi page builder, enabling you to create your webpages effortlessly.

## 1  Using Content AI in Divi

Let’s begin to leverage our powerful Content AI tool in Divi to enhance your website’s content easily and deliver a compelling user experience.

### 1.1  Enable Content AI Module

To use Content AI in Divi, first, you need to ensure that the Content AI module is enabled in Rank Math. To do so, navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard and enable the Content AI module, as shown below.

![Enable Content AI](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/enable-content-AI-40.jpeg)

### 1.2  Edit the Post/Page in Divi Page Builder

Open the Post/Page you want to edit in the Divi page builder.

![Edit post with Divi](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/edit-post-with-Divi.jpg)

### 1.3   Navigate to Rank Meta Box

In the Divi page builder, click the **Rank Math SEO** icon, as shown below.

![Click Rank Math SEO icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202406%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Rank Math SEO Metabox will be displayed on the screen.

![Rank Math Metabox in Divi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202010%201272'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** The [**Fix With AI**](https://rankmath.com/kb/fix-seo-tests-with-content-ai/) feature in Content AI is currently available only for the default WordPress Editors. Support for Divi will be available soon.

### 1.4  Open Content AI Panel

In the Rank Math Meta Box, scroll down to the **Focus Keyword** section and click the **Content AI** option, as shown below.

![Open Content AI panel](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202010%201272'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, the dedicated Content AI Panel will open in your Divi page builder, and you can access all the Content AI features.

![Content AI Panel in Divi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201174'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Content AI Views

To help you produce the perfect article for your audience, Content AI offers several features. These features are conveniently organized into views like Research, AI Tools, and Chat so you can quickly access them.

### 2.1  Research

The Research view enables you to research the target keyword and use the smart suggestions from our Content AI. Enter the keyword in the FOCUS KEYWORD field, click the **Research** button, and let the magic unfold.

![Research View in Divi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201996%201102'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  AI Tools

In the AI Tools section, you can find [40+ Content AI](https://rankmath.com/?post_type=ht_kb&p=1177288&preview=true#content-ai-tools) tools that you can use right within the Divi page builder.

![Divi AI tools](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202014%201136'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can use any of the listed AI tools of your choice. The corresponding Content AI tool will pop up on the screen, where you can fill in the relevant fields and click the **Generate** button.

![Using Introduction AI tool in Divi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202508%201322'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once the content is generated, click the **Copy** option to copy and then insert the copied content in the Divi page builder.

![Copy the AI generated content](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202550%201332'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Chat

RankBot is well integrated into the Divi page builder, enabling you to ask anything about your topic or SEO-related queries.

![Chat option in Divi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201994%201120'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! We hope this tutorial helped you take advantage of Content AI & its features right within your favorite Divi page builder.
