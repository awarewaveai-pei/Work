## 1  A Closer Look at Content AI

Before we dive into the details of Content AI features and plans, let’s take a moment to understand the core [essence of Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/#how-content-ai-works).

**Content AI Research:** _Stay Updated and Informed_

Content AI Research constantly delivers the latest information, offering users invaluable recommendations. This dynamic process enhances the effectiveness and relevance of your content.

**Content AI Writing:** _Crafting Optimized Content with AI_

Content AI Writing utilizes artificial intelligence to generate user-friendly content optimized for search engines. It’s a powerful tool to enhance your online presence.

**Content AI****Images****:** _Generating Images Alt__Texts with AI_

Content AI Images leverages artificial intelligence to [generate alternative text (alt text) for images](https://rankmath.com/kb/generate-alt-text-with-content-ai/) on your WordPress website. It analyses the visual elements of your images and generates descriptive text that accurately represents their content.

**AI Link Genius:**  _Generating context-aware link suggestions_

AI Link Genius uses artificial intelligence to [suggest relevant internal links inside your WordPress Editor](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-add-internal-links/). It analyzes all your existing posts in WordPress and suggests context-aware anchor texts and related links to use.

Each of these features is assigned a different monthly use, depending on the plan, and we’ll explore that in detail shortly. Our Content AI is available in the following [Content AI membership plans](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/#pricing): Starter, Creator, and Expert.

## 2  Feature Allocation for Different User Categories

The plan and feature-based system is at the core of our Content AI, designed to serve different categories of users:

**1. Rank Math Free Users:** Limited Access

Rank Math Free users will have access to some Content AI features. This is typically limited to 10 uses per available feature.

**2. Paid Plugin Subscribers:** Enjoy a 15-Day Free Trial

Subscribers of the plugin (PRO, Business, & Agency) are **eligible for a 15-day free trial** of Content AI. The Content AI plan you receive depends on the Rank Math plan you’ve purchased. Specifically:

- Rank Math [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) users receive a trial of Content AI [Starter](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) plan

- Rank Math [Business](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) users receive a trial of Content AI [Creator](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) plan

- Rank Math [Agency](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) users receive a trial of Content AI [Expert](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) plan

**3. Content AI Subscribers:** An Annual Model with Monthly feature uses.

Content AI plan subscribers (Starter, Creator, Expert) operate on a unique model. You’re billed annually, but your feature uses refreshes every month. It’s essential to note that unused uses do not roll over to the next month. Be sure to utilize all your available monthly features effectively, whether for optimizing existing content or creating new content.

## 4  Frequently Asked Questions

### How Can I Get More Feature Uses Within the Month?

When you purchase a Content AI subscription, you pay a yearly fee. However, the allocated feature uses are provided to you on a monthly basis. If you exhaust your uses in a month, you have the following options:

– You can wait till next month for the feature uses to replenish
– You can upgrade your subscription to a higher plan
– If you already have the highest plan, then you can reset your uses for the remainder of that month by paying a nominal one-time fee

You can upgrade your Content AI plan [here](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/).

### Will I Lose My Monthly Feature Uses if I Don’t Use Them?

Any unused feature uses in a given month will not carry over to the following month and will expire/renew at the same time.

### How Many Content AI Feature Uses Will I Receive Each Month?

Depending on your Content AI subscription level (Starter, Creator, or Expert), you will receive applicable monthly uses. Trial users will receive a one-time feature use.

### Can I Stack Content AI Monthly Feature Uses?

Rank Math’s Content AI feature uses are designed for one-time use and do not roll over to the next month. Therefore, there’s no benefit in attempting to stack these feature uses.

### Do You Offer Monthly Plans for Content AI?

We do not offer monthly plans for Content AI. Content AI is only available as an annual subscription. Depending on your annual plan, a set amount of feature uses will be added to your Content AI account every month.

### I don’t see Content AI Feature Use Balance on my website – what should I do?

First, head over to the [Rank Math account area](https://rankmath.com/my-account/websites/) and ensure that your account has a Content AI plan.

Then head over **to Rank Math SEO → General Settings → Content AI** on your website and refresh your Content AI plan. If your feature use balance still don’t appear, then you should [reconnect your account as shown here](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-restore-missing-content-ai-credits/).
