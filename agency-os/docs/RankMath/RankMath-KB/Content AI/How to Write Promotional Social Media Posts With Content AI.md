# How to Write Promotional Social Media Posts With Content AI

			
			

	

Writing engaging promotional posts for social media can be challenging. If you’ve been struggling with this, there’s no need to worry anymore.

With our Content AI tool, you can generate tailored posts effortlessly for your social platforms. Whether you’re promoting a product, sharing a blog post, highlighting an offer, or driving engagement, Content AI has you covered—no matter how many variations you need.

In this knowledgebase guide, you’ll learn how to use Content AI to craft posts that capture attention and inspire action, helping you achieve the results you’re looking for.

**Note:**If you run out of credits, you can subscribe to a [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements. You can enable the Content AI module from**Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules.**

## 1  Access RankBot

We’ll use the **RankBot Content AI tool** to generate promotional social media posts. It already has a built-in prompt, so you don’t need to worry about crafting one from scratch.

Before you begin, ensure the main post or product you want to promote is published and ready. This will ensure that users land on the correct page when they click the link in your social post.

To access RankBot, navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Chat** from your WordPress dashboard.

![Accessing RankBot via Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201600%20804'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Alternatively, you can access RankBot directly from your WordPress Editor, whether you’re using the Block or Classic Editor.

Locate the page or post you’re working on and click the **Content AI** icon. In the Content AI metabox, click the **Chat**icon to open RankBot.

![Accessing RankBot via WordPress Block Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201600%20758'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Generate the Promotional Social Media Posts

As mentioned, there is a pre-made prompt you can use. To access it, click the**Prompts Library** button to view available prompt options.

![Click the Prompts Library](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%201042'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Then, search for “social” to quickly find the prompt you need or locate the **Social Media Promo** prompt. Next, click the**Use Prompt** button under the prompt preview, as shown below.

![Select the Social Media Promo prompt](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201226%20882'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This will add the prompt to the RankBot Chat interface:

![Update the Social Media Promo prompt](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20842'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’ll see that the prompt requests some information in brackets:

- `[number]`: Enter the number of promotional posts you want to generate.

- `[blog post summary]`: Provide a summary of the content or product you’re promoting. The more details you provide, the more accurate the posts will be.

For instance, if you’ve published an article titled “10 Easy Ways to Boost Your Productivity at Work” and want to generate 5 promotional posts, you might modify the prompt like this:

_“Create 5 social media posts promoting: “The blog post ’10 Easy Ways to Boost Your Productivity at Work’ provides practical and actionable tips to help you become more productive in the workplace. It covers a range of strategies, including time management techniques, prioritization methods, and ways to minimize distractions. Whether you’re looking to improve your workflow, enhance your focus, or simply get more done in less time, this blog post offers valuable insights to help you achieve your productivity goals.”_

Feel free to customize your post summary to fit your needs rather than using the exact example.

Once you’ve added the customized prompt to the chat interface, click the **Enter**icon to proceed, as shown below.

![RankBot Enter icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20799'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Sit back and watch the magic happen! RankBot will generate various promotional posts based on your input, which will look something like this:

![RankBot generates various promotional posts](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201093%201399'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**The generated posts may have placeholders like `[Link]`. Remember to replace these placeholders with the actual link to the content or product you’re promoting on social media.

And that’s it! We hope this guide helps you create effective promotional social media posts using Content AI.
