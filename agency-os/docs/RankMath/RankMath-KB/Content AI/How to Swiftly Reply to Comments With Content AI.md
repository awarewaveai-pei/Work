## 1  Creating a Comment Reply

Let us discuss how to create a comment reply using [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Comment Reply AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Comment Reply**. Alternatively, you can choose the Comment Reply AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Click Comment Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202522%201216'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Comment Reply AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Comment Reply AI Tool

Let us discuss the options available in the Comment Reply tool.

### 2.1  Reply Brief

Enter a few words describing the comment you want to create. Content AI will use this to understand the direction to develop the comment. This is a required field and should contain 400 characters or less.

![Reply Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20959%20352'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Original Comment

Copy the original comment you want to reply to and paste it into this field. Although it is not a required field, the original comment will provide more context for Content AI and generate a personalized reply. If the comment exceeds 600 characters, remove some unessential sections before entering it into the field.

![Original Comment](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20355'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Choose the audience that best describes the person you’re replying to.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Focus Keyword

Enter the focus keywords you want Content AI to include in the comment. Content AI will include variations of these keywords in the comment. Remember to separate multiple words or phrases with a comma. The keywords should be at most 200 characters.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone. You can select one from the dropdown menu or add your custom tone. You can enter multiple tones in this field.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the reply. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Select the number of outputs you want Content AI to generate in one go. The AI tool can generate a max of 8 variations of comment replies in one go.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your comment replies within a few seconds.

![Generate Comment Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more comment variations, click **Generate More**.

### 2.9  Copy

After creating your comment, hover over it and click **Copy**. You can then proceed to paste it wherever you want.

![Click Copy to copy the output](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Comment Reply AI Tool

Engaging with your audience via comments boosts relationships and SEO, but it’s time-consuming. To streamline this process, the AI Comment Reply tool creates relevant, professional replies in seconds. Here’s how it can help you:

### 3.1  Quickly Responding to Customer Feedback

When customers leave comments on your blog or website—whether they’re praising your content or sharing concerns—it’s important to acknowledge them promptly. This AI tool generates polite, professional, and appreciative replies, ensuring customers feel heard and valued.

![Quickly Responding to Customer Feedback with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Handling Negative Comments with Professionalism

Not all comments will be positive, and some might even be critical. Responding in a calm, professional, and solution-oriented manner is key to maintaining a good reputation. The Comment Reply AI tool helps you craft responses that acknowledge concerns while steering the conversation in a positive direction.

![Handling Negative Comments with Professionalism with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Encouraging Further Engagement

Sometimes, a comment opens the door for a deeper discussion. Whether someone asks a question or expresses interest in your content, a well-crafted response can encourage them to continue engaging. This AI tool creates thoughtful replies that invite further discussion.

![Encouraging Further Engagement with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Maintaining a Consistent Brand Voice Across Replies

If multiple team members are managing your blog comments, maintaining a consistent brand tone and messaging can be challenging. This AI tool ensures all responses align with your brand’s personality—whether it’s friendly, professional, or authoritative.

![Maintaining a Consistent Brand Voice Across Replies with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
