# How to Expand a Sentence With Content AI

			
			

	

While planning to write a piece of content, it is common to jot down points and ideas so you can develop them further while you write.

But this could be time-consuming in most instances. Instead, let Content AI do all the heavy lifting for you and let it further develop your ideas.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to expand a sentence with [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

## Table Of Contents

- [Expanding a Sentence](#expanding-a-sentence)

- [Available Options in the Sentence Expander AI Tool](#sentence-expander-ai-tool-options)

- [Use Cases of the Sentence Expander AI Tool](#use-cases-of-the-sentence-expander-ai-tool)

## 1  Expanding a Sentence

Let us now discuss how to expand a sentence using our Content AI to enhance the clarity, depth, and overall quality of your writing.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** f you ever run out of credits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Sentence Expander AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202162%201040'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Sentence Expander**. Alternatively, you can choose the Sentence Expander AI tool from your favorite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Click Sentence Expander](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202526%201228'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Sentence Expander AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Sentence Expander AI Tool

Let us now discuss the options in the Sentence Expander tool.

### 2.1  Sentence

In this field, enter the sentence you want to expand. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Sentence](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20952%20350'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Topic

You can provide Content AI with more context about the sentence by entering the article’s topic here. You can enter a maximum of 200 characters.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20950%20219'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma. The keywords should be at most 200 characters.

![enter your Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the expanded sentence. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and five into this field, depending on how many expanded sentences you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will expand your sentences within a few seconds.

![Generate sentence expander](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to suggest more sentences, click **Generate More**.

![Generate more sentences](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Copy and Insert

Here, hover over the expanded sentence and click **Copy**or **Insert**.

- **Copy** will copy the expanded sentence to your clipboard. You can then proceed to paste it elsewhere.

- **Insert** will insert the expanded sentence into the Content Editor. You can then improve it using the other AI tools in the Content Editor.

![Copy sentence](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201910%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases of the Sentence Expander AI Tool

The Sentence Expander AI tool is useful for various purposes, including creating marketing content, composing academic essays, or refining business correspondence. This tool boosts clarity, depth, and effectiveness. Here’s how to utilize it effectively:

### 3.1  Enhancing Short Marketing Copy

You can take a simple product or service description and expand it into a more persuasive and engaging message. This helps in making marketing content more appealing to potential customers.

![Expand a marketing copy with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Refining Email Communication

This AI tool helps you expand short, vague email phrases into well-structured, thoughtful messages that get your point across effectively.

![refine email communications with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Strengthening Storytelling in Content Writing

If you’re writing a story, blog post, or creative content, the tool helps bring your words to life. It transforms simple descriptions into immersive, vivid, and expressive narratives, making your writing more compelling and enjoyable to read.

![strengthen storytelling in content writing with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20922'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
