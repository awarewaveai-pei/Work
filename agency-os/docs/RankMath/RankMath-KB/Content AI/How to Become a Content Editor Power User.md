# How to Become a Content Editor Power User

			
			

	

The Content Editor in [Rank Math is AI-powered writing assistant](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that helps to generate copy for your blogs, social posts, marketing emails, and much more. So, no more writer’s block, switching between different tools, or learning new ones.

![Content Editor: Writing Amazing Content Made Easy!](https://img.youtube.com/vi/tkEgfr4jZMM/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

Our power-packed content editor is here to help you take your writing to the next level. In this knowledgebase article, we’ll walk you through how to use the content editor.

## 1  How to Access Rank Math Content Editor?

We’ll first discuss how to access the Content Editor in Rank Math.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

Start by ensuring that you have the Content AI module enabled on Rank Math. To do so, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO** and enable the **Content AI** module.

![enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2  Open Content AI

Once you have the Content AI module enabled, navigate to **Rank Math SEO →** **Content AI**, as shown below.

![navigate to Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201122'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.3  Navigate to Rank Math’s Content Editor

From the Content AI page that appears on the screen, navigate to the **Content Editor** tab, as shown below.

![navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  How to Use Content Editor

The Content Editor is similar to the Block Editor and enables you to quickly get started with writing content. You can click the **Plus** icon to start adding the blocks.

![click plus icon to add blocks](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202528%201224'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Write Your Content Using Content AI

The **[Write](https://rankmath.com/kb/using-content-ai-in-block-editor/#write-view-block-editor)**view****in the right corner of the Content Editor allows you to feed instructions and let Content AI craft the perfect article for your readers.

![Write view](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202528%201224'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, let’s discuss the various options available in the Content AI one by one.

### 3.1  Instructions

Here, you can add a short summary of what you want Content AI to write for you in not more than 600 characters.

![Instructions](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20585%20336'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Tone

You can choose the tone in which Content AI should generate the content. By default, it is set to the default content you’ve selected in the [Content AI Global settings](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/#configure-content-ai).

![set tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20594%20200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Learn more about each tone in detail [here](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/).

### 3.3  Focus Keyword

Enter the main focus keyword you want to target for your content.

![enter focus keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20594%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Length

Here, you can select whether Content AI should generate short, medium, or long-form content.

- **Short** will generate an output with 1-2 sentences

- **Medium** will generate an output with 3-4 sentences

- **Long** will generate an output with at least 5 sentences

![specify Length ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20494%20198'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Generate Content

Once you’ve filled the essential fields, click the **Generate** button and let the magic of Content AI unfold.

![click generate button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20670%20994'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use `Ctrl + /` to generate the output. This keyboard shortcut works only when the Write tab is active in the Content editor. This sends the tone and instructions as well.

The `Cmd + enter` keyboard shortcut will continue writing the content by reading what was previously written, as shown below.

![Keyboard Shortcut to continue writing the content](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201276%20626'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Using AI Tools

In addition to the Write view, you can write for your specific use cases with our pre-trained[40+ AI Tools](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai-tools/), which you can access on the right side of the Content AI page.

All these AI tools are neatly classified into various categories, as shown below.

![AI tools](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202498%201202'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use the Search field to identify the AI tool that you’re looking for quickly.

![Search field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202506%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Also, you can type two forward slashes `//` followed by the first few letters of the AI tool you want to use. The AI tool will display as you type. Select it, as shown below.

![AI Assistant block in the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202510%201074'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In case you do not find a pre-trained AI tool for your requirement, you can make use of your custom prompts with the AI command.

## 5  How to Effectively Write Your Content Using Content AI

The Content Editor provided by our platform offers a range of [writing tools](https://rankmath.com/blog/best-ai-copywriting-tools/) to ensure a seamless AI writing experience.

Choose the [AI Tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai-tools/) that best fits your needs, and provide all the details in it. For instance, to create a blog introduction, select the **Blog Introduction** AI tool. Our Content AI will generate an introduction based on the preferences you provided. It will look something like this.

![Introduction Example](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202544%201286'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can further review the introduction and edit it if required. You can then add [paragraphs](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-paragraph-writing-tool/) and a [conclusion](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-blog-post-conclusion-tool/) to your post using our AI tools.

## 6  Creating New Post With Content AI

Creating a new post with Content AI is effortless, ensuring that no formatting is lost. Our powerful editor allows you to generate posts while retaining the desired layout and structure seamlessly.

### 6.1  Copy as Text

Click the **Copy as Text** button, and now your content will be copied in the Text format to paste into the Classic Editor or any other text editor of your choice.

![Copy as Text](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202498%201196'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 6.2  Copy as Blocks

To transition your content to the Block Editor or any other blocking editor, click the **Copy as Blocks**button, and all of them will be inserted as blocks in your preferred editor.

![Copy as Blocks](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202498%201196'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 6.3  Create New Post

Rank Math makes creating a new post effortlessly convenient. With a simple click on the **Create New Post**button, a fresh post will be generated, incorporating the content from the Rank Math Content Editor.

![Crete New Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202498%201196'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 7  Command Center

You can use Content AI for not only creating new content, but also for enhancing and editing what you already have. That’s why we’ve set up a Command Center, which you can access right from your Content Editor.

To use it, simply place your cursor over a block in the Content Editor, and in the toolbar that appears at the top, click the **Content AI** icon. The Command Center will appear with all the available features.

![Command Center in Classic Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202500%201194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use the keyboard shortcut `//` to enable the Command box in the Content Editor.
