# How to Get Started With Content AI

			
			

	

**Content AI** is an AI-powered assistant within the Rank Math SEO plugin that helps you write, optimize, and improve your content directly within WordPress.

The most significant advantage of using AI tools is saving time; **[71% of users cite it as their number one benefit.](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/)**

This knowledgebase article will guide you through setting up Content AI step by step and help you start using its writing and optimization tools right away.

If you want to learn how other professionals are using AI in their SEO workflows, our [Future of Search Report](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/) shares insights from over 700 WordPress users.

## 1  Install the Rank Math SEO Plugin

Content AI is included in the Rank Math SEO plugin, so the first step is to install and activate the plugin on your WordPress site.

From your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for “Rank Math SEO,” click **Install Now**, then click **Activate**.

![Install Rank Math SEO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20568'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once activated, **Rank Math SEO** will appear in your WordPress admin menu.

## 2  Connect Your Rank Math Account

When you install Rank Math for the first time, you may be taken through a **Setup Wizard**. While the Setup Wizard helps you configure your SEO settings, it’s **not required** to use Content AI.

If you prefer to skip it for now, click **Return to dashboard** at the bottom of the screen. You can always complete the Setup Wizard later.

![Rank Math setup wizard](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%201306'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To access the dashboard manually, go to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard**.

Before using Content AI, you’ll need to connect your website to a Rank Math account. From the dashboard, go to the **Help** tab and click **Connect Now**.

![click Connect Now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20620'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, log in to your Rank Math account and click **OK, ACTIVATE NOW** to enable the connection.

![Activate your Rank Math account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201012%20878'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Enable the Content AI Module

After connecting your account, the next step is to enable Content AI.

Navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules**, locate the **Content AI** module, and toggle it on, as shown below.

![Enable Content AI in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20703'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Start Using Content AI

With the module active, you can start using Content AI to generate suggestions, structure your content, and optimize it for SEO.

If you’re using the free version of Rank Math, you’ll receive between **one to five free uses**across most of our AI-powered features per month. If you run out of uses, [you can upgrade to a Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that fits your needs.

To access Content AI tools, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI** from your dashboard.

![Content AI dashboard](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201051%201399'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

![Content AI tools](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201270%201399'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use Content AI directly within the WordPress post or page editor. Simply open a post or page, and you’ll find the **Content AI** meta box at the top-right corner of the screen.

![Content AI in WordPress editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201383%201399'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

It also integrates seamlessly with popular page builders like [Elementor](https://rankmath.com/kb/using-content-ai-in-elementor/) and [Divi](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-in-divi/), making it easy to use regardless of how you build your content.

And that’s it! We hope you’ve learned how to get started with Content AI. If you have any doubts or need help with anything related to Rank Math, feel free to [reach out to the support team](https://rankmath.com/support/). They’re always available and happy to help.
