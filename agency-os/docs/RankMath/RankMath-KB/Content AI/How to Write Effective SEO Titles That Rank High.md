## 1  Creating an SEO Title

We’ll now discuss how the SEO Title AI tool will help you create amazing titles.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the SEO Title AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **SEO Title**. Alternatively, you can choose the SEO Title AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select SEO Title AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202542%201058'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the SEO Title AI tool settings. Let’s go after the fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the SEO Title AI Tool

We’ve discussed the options in the SEO Title AI tool below.

### 2.1  Post Title

In 200 characters or less, enter the title/headline of the content in this field.

**Note:**If you do not have a post title, you can quickly generate one using the [Post Title AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-post-title-tool/).

![Post Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20959%20217'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Focus Keyword

In 200 characters or less, enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for in this field. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Post Brief

Enter a short summary of your content in this field that provides more context to Content AI about the article. You can enter a maximum of 400 characters.

![Post Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20959%20367'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can add multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to create the SEO title. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and 25, depending on the number of titles you want to generate.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your SEO title within a few seconds.

![Generate SEO Titles](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201894%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more SEO titles, click **Generate More**.

![Generate more SEO Titles](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Copy

After creating your SEO title, hover over it and click **Copy**. You can then insert it wherever it is required.

![Copy SEO Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the SEO Title AI Tool

An SEO-optimized title is one of the biggest factors in ranking higher on search engines. It’s not just about adding a keyword—it’s about making the title click-worthy while still being optimized for Google. The SEO Title AI tool ensures your titles attract both search engines and real people, leading to higher rankings and more clicks.

### 3.1  Creating High-Ranking Titles with the Right Keywords

Your SEO title must _include the focus keyword naturally_while still sounding engaging. This AI helps craft titles that meet search intent while avoiding keyword stuffing.

![Creating High-Ranking Titles with the Right Keywords with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20924'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Generating Multilingual Titles

For global audiences, multilingual titles expand reach and inclusivity. The SEO Title AI tool supports creating titles in different languages while maintaining SEO integrity.

Translating keywords and adapting tone to cultural nuances ensures relevance across regions. This approach helps businesses connect with international markets effectively.

![Generating Multilingual Titles with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
