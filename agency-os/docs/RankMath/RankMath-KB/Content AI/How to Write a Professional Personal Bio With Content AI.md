## 1  Creating a Personal Bio

Let us see how to create an outstanding personal bio using [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Personal Bio AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Personal Bio**. Alternatively, you can choose the Personal Bio AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Personal Bio](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202504%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Personal Bio AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Personal Bio AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Personal Bio AI Tool

We’ll now discuss all the available options in the Personal Bio AI tool.

### 2.1  Personal Information

Enter the details you want your readers to know about you. You can enter your name, what you do, and where you work. This is a required field and should contain 400 characters or less.

![Personal Information](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20954%20358'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Purpose

Where do you intend to use the personal bio? Is it on your resume, on your business website, or in an email? In 200 characters or less, enter it into this required field.

![Purpose](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20957%20227'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Personal Achievements

Enter the positive things you want anyone reading the personal bio to know about you. This is a required field and should contain no more than 400 characters.

![Personal Achievements](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20953%20349'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Focus Keyword

Enter the keywords you want Content AI to include in the bio. Content AI will include the word or its variation in the output. The keywords should be at most 200 characters in total. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Tone

Choose the option that best describes the writing tone. You can select one from the dropdown menu or choose a custom option. You can add multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the personal bio. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin generating your personal bio.

![Click Generate on Personal Bio](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 2.8  Copy or Insert

After creating your personal bio, hover over it and click **Copy**or **Insert**.

- **Copy** will copy the personal bio to your clipboard. You can then proceed to paste it elsewhere.

- **Insert** will insert the personal bio into the Content Editor. You can also improve it using the other AI tools available in the Content Editor.

![Click Copy or Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Case Studies for the Personal Bio AI Tool

Your personal bio is one of the most important ways to introduce yourself, whether online or in professional settings. Below are some key use cases where this AI tool can help you create the perfect bio effortlessly.

### 3.1  Writing a Personal Bio for Social Media Profiles

Your bio on social media is a key part of your online presence. Whether it’s [LinkedIn](https://rankmath.com/kb/write-linkedin-bio-with-content-ai/#personal-linkedin-bio), Twitter, or Instagram, each platform requires a different tone. A Personal LinkedIn Bio, for example, should be more formal and professional, while an Instagram bio can be more casual and engaging.

Instead of manually tweaking your bio for each platform, the Personal Bio AI tool helps you rewrite and optimize your bio to match the tone and audience of each site—ensuring that your brand is well-represented everywhere.

![Writing a Personal Bio for Social Media Profiles with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201182%20851'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Creating an Engaging Bio for Websites & About Pages

If you’re a freelancer, entrepreneur, or creative professional, your website’s “About Me” section is a powerful way to build trust. The Personal Bio AI tool helps you craft a narrative-driven bio that tells your story while keeping it engaging and professional.

![Create an Engaging Bio for Websites with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201175%20849'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Optimizing Freelancer and Consultant Bios

As a freelancer or consultant, your bio is often the key to attracting potential clients. Whether you’re building your profile on Upwork, Fiverr, or a personal website, the Personal Bio AI tool can help you craft a bio that emphasizes your skills, experience, and what makes you unique.

By ensuring clarity and professionalism while avoiding generic descriptions, this AI helps you stand out in competitive marketplaces.

![Optimize Freelancer bios using Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201175%20849'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Writing a Compelling Author Bio

If you’re an author, blogger, or content creator, your bio is an essential part of your brand. It appears on your website, book covers, and social media profiles, helping readers connect with you.

The Personal Bio AI tool can assist in summarizing your background, writing style, and achievements in a way that feels authentic and engaging.

![Write compelling author bio using Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201175%20850'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Creating Professional Speaker Bios

If you’re invited to speak at an event, webinar, or panel discussion, you’ll likely need a speaker bio that introduces you in a way that builds credibility.

The Personal Bio AI tool can generate a polished, concise bio highlighting your qualifications, expertise, and why you’re the perfect fit for the event.

![Create a professional speaker bios with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201177%20851'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
