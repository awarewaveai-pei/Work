## 1  Creating a Product Review

We’ll now discuss creating a product review using the Product Review AI tool in Rank Math.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Product Review AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Product Review**. Alternatively, you can choose the Product Review AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Click Product Review](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202532%201232'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Product Review AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Product Review AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Product Review AI Tool

We’ll now discuss all the options available in the Product Review tool.

### 2.1  Features and Benefits

In 600 characters or less, enter the characteristics of the product, along with the problems it will solve for the user or the advantages the user will derive from using it.

- The features include things like its color, origin, and construction material.

- The benefits explain how the features solve a problem or work to the advantage of the user. For example, a thick coat will keep the wearer warm even in the coldest winter.

**Note:**You can generate your features and benefits with the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-topic-research-tool/). Remember to set the **Format** to ‘List’****and enter your product name followed by ‘features and benefits’ as the **Topic**. E.g., iPhone 14 features and benefits.

![Features and Benefits](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20363'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Product Name

Enter the product’s name exactly as you want it to appear in the description. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20195'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Limitations and Drawbacks

In 600 characters or less, list the improvements you would like to see in the product.

**Note:** You can create your limitations and drawbacks using the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-topic-research-tool/) in Content AI. You will include ‘limitations and drawbacks’ after the product name.

![Limitations and Drawbacks](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20952%20358'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Enter the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the product review. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten, depending on the number of reviews you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your review.

![Generate Product Review](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 2.9  Insert

Hover over a review you wish to use and click **Insert**. It will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Insert Product Review](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Product Review AI Tool

Writing detailed and engaging product reviews can be time-consuming and challenging. The Product Review AI tool simplifies this process by generating well-structured reviews tailored to your specific needs. Here are some practical applications:​

### 3.1  E-commerce Product Listings

For online store owners, creating unique and persuasive product descriptions is crucial to stand out in a competitive market. The Product Review AI tool can generate comprehensive reviews highlighting key features and benefits, enhancing the appeal of your products.

![E-commerce Product Listings review with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20913'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Affiliate Marketing Content

Affiliate marketers rely on in-depth product reviews to drive conversions. This tool can help create unbiased, detailed reviews that provide value to readers and encourage informed purchasing decisions.

![Crafting Affiliate Marketing Content Product Reviews with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Tech Blog Product Comparisons

Tech bloggers often compare similar products to guide their audience in making choices. The Product Review AI tool can generate consistent reviews for multiple products, facilitating objective comparisons.

![Craft tech Blog Product Comparisons with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once you’ve generated the review content for one product, you can repeat the same process for the remaining products you want to review to create comparative content.

### 3.4  Customer Review Responses for Online Retailers

Managing customer reviews is vital for online retailers. This tool can assist in crafting professional responses to customer feedback, addressing concerns, and highlighting product features.

![Making Customer Review Responses for Online Retailers with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20913'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
