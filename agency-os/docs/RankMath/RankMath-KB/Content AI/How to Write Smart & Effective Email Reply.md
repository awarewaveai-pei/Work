## 1  Creating an Email Reply

Creating an email reply requires careful consideration of the recipient’s needs and expectations. Let us now create an email reply to provide a clear and concise response, addressing all relevant points and inquiries with the Email Reply AI tool.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Email Reply AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202162%201040'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Email Reply**. Alternatively, you can choose the Email Reply AI tool from your favorite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Email Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202516%201222'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Email Reply AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Email Reply AI Tool

Let us now discuss the options available in the Email Reply tool.

### 2.1  Email

In 1000 characters or less, enter the email you want to reply to into this field. You can also include the Email signature to help Content AI personalize the email response.

![Email](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20857%20322'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Reply Brief

In no more than 400 characters, enter a short description of the response you want Content AI to create. If you need assistance, fill in the gap in the below text and paste it into the field.

![Reply Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20959%20352'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Length

Here, you will set the word count of the reply. This required field contains three options: short, medium, and long.

- **Short** will generate an email response with 1 or 2 sentences

- **Medium** will create an email response with 3-4 sentences

- **Long** will create an email response with at least 5 sentences

![Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate an email reply. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten into this field, depending on how many replies you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your reply.

![Generate Email Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

![Generate More Email Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201238'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Insert

Hover over the reply and click **Insert**, as shown below. It will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Insert the Email reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201908%201236'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for Email Reply AI Tool

​The Email Reply AI tool streamlines the process of crafting thoughtful and effective email responses. By inputting the original email and specifying key parameters, you can generate tailored replies that maintain professionalism and clarity.​ Here’s how:

### 3.1  Customer Support Responses

Handling a high volume of customer inquiries can be challenging. The Email Reply AI tool can assist in generating personalized responses, ensuring customer satisfaction.​

An__example is a customer inquiring about the availability of a product currently out of stock.

![craft Customer Support Responses with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Collaborative Project Correspondence

Coordinating with team members on project updates requires clear and concise communication. This tool can help draft responses that address multiple points effectively.​

![Email Reply](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20915'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Addressing Customer Complaints

Effectively managing customer complaints is vital for maintaining a positive reputation. This tool can generate empathetic and solution-oriented responses.​

For instance, you can use it when a customer expresses dissatisfaction with a delayed order.

![Addressing Customer Complaints with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20916'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Job Application Follow-Ups

Following up on job applications shows initiative and genuine interest. This tool can help in drafting professional follow-up emails to potential employers. For example, if a candidate emails you stating that a week has passed since they submitted a job application without receiving any acknowledgment, you can use this tool to address their concerns.

![Crafting Job Application Follow-Ups with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
