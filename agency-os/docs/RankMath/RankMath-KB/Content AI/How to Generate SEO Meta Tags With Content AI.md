## 1  Using the Content Editor

Head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor** from your WordPress dashboard.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and then select **SEO Meta**. You will be presented with the SEO Meta AI tool settings.

![Select SEO Meta](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202524%201148'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Using the WordPress Editor

Head over to the post or page you want to add the SEO meta. Once done, click the **Rank Math SEO** icon at the top-right corner of the screen.

![Click the Rank Math SEO icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202540%20882'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click **Edit Snippet**, as shown below.

![Click Edit Snippet](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202544%20974'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Preview Snippet Editor, click **Generate With AI**.

![Click Generate with AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201306%201224'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The SEO Meta AI tool settings will appear. Let’s see them one by one.

## 3  Available Options in the SEO Meta AI Tool

We’ll now discuss the options available in the SEO Meta AI tool.

### 3.1  Topic

In 200 characters or less, enter the title/headline of your content in this field. This is a required field.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Post Brief

In 400 characters or less, enter a short summary of your content in this field.

![Post Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20949%20330'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for in this field. The keywords should be no longer than 400 characters. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.6  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the SEO Meta tags. You can only add only one language here. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and 25 into this field, depending on how many variations of SEO Meta you want to generate in one go.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201040%20130'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.8  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your SEO meta.

![Generate SEO Meta](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201908%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 3.9  Copy

After creating your SEO meta, hover over it. It will display two new options: Copy and Insert.

If you created the SEO meta using the Content Editor, click **Copy**. The SEO meta will be copied to your clipboard. You can then proceed to paste it wherever you want.

If you created the SEO meta using the WordPress Editor, click **Insert**. The SEO meta will be inserted into the Snippet Editor.

![Click Copy or Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Use Cases for the SEO Meta AI Tool

The SEO Meta AI tool helps generate highly relevant, keyword-rich, and compelling meta tags that improve both search rankings and click-through rates (CTR). Here are ways to use it effectively:

### 4.1  Crafting Click-Worthy Meta Tags for Blog Posts

Your blog post might be insightful and well-researched, but if the meta title and description are weak, no one will click on it. This AI tool crafts compelling SEO metadata that makes your post stand out in search results.

It ensures the title is engaging yet optimized for keywords, and the description provides just enough information to spark curiosity without revealing everything. The result? Higher organic CTR and better Google rankings.

![Crafting Click-Worthy Meta Tags for Blog Posts with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.2  Optimizing E-Commerce Product Pages for Higher Search Visibility

E-commerce SEO goes beyond just adding keywords to product descriptions. A strong meta title and description ensure your products appear prominently in search results and encourage more clicks.

This AI tool analyzes product briefs and generates SEO-rich meta tags that not only boost rankings but also increase purchase intent.

![Optimizing E-Commerce Product Pages for Higher Search Visibility with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
