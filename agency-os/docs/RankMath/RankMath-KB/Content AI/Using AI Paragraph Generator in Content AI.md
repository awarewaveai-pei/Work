## 1  Using the Paragraph AI Tool

We’ll now discuss how to use the Paragraph AI tool effectively.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard** from your WordPress dashboard. Once done, scroll down to **Content AI**and enable it, as shown below.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Paragraph AI Tool

The next step is to navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, click **AI Tools** and select **Paragraph**, as shown below.

![Open Paragraph AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202504%201222'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Paragraph AI tool settings. Let’s go after the fields one by one.

![Paragraph](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20950%20591'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Paragraph AI Tool

Let us now see each option of the Paragraph AI tool in detail.

### 2.1  Topic

Enter a summary of the topic you want to address in the paragraph. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Main Argument

Enter the main point you want to address in the paragraph. This is a required field and should contain 400 characters or less.

![Main Argument](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20951%20345'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Audience

Select the option from that dropdown menu that best describes your target audience. You can enter multiple audiences or write a custom one.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Supporting Points

Enter any other supporting points you want to include in the paragraph. You can leave this field blank if you do not have a supporting point. If you’re entering a supporting point, it should contain 500 characters or less.

![Supporting points](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20945%20348'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Length

Here, you will set the length of the paragraph field. This a required field.

- **Short** will set the output to be around 1-2 sentences

- **Medium** will set the output to be around 3-4 sentences

- **Long** will set the output to be more than 5 sentences

![Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Output Language

A dropdown menu will appear when you click the Language field. Select your desired language from the menu.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**The dropdown menu will not appear if the field contains a language. In this case, you will delete the language. Once done, you will select your desired language from the dropdown menu.

### 2.8  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and five into the Number of Outputs field, depending on how many outputs you want Content AI to generate.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Generate

Content AI will begin creating your paragraphs when you click the **Generate**button.

![Generate paragraph](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201256'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can click **Generate More** if you want Content AI to create more paragraphs.

![Generate more paragraphs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.10  Copy and Insert

The next step is to hover over the paragraph you are interested in and click **Copy**or **Insert**.

- When you click **Copy**, the paragraph will be copied to your clipboard.

- If you click **Insert**, the paragraph will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop the inserted paragraph using the other AI tools in Content AI.

![Copy paragraph](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
