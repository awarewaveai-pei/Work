# Content AI Overview

			
			

	

Content AI includes a suite of AI-powered writing tools to change the way you do SEO inside WordPress. Using its AI SEO features gives you a competitive edge in content creation and ranking in SERP.

The top benefit of using AI tools? Saving time! **In fact, [71% of users say it’s their #1 advantage](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/).**That’s a significant productivity boost for anyone seeking to streamline their content workflow.

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll discuss all the exciting features of our Content AI in brief.

## How to Use Content AI?

To use our Content AI, [install Rank Math by following this tutorial](https://rankmath.com/kb/installing-through-the-admin-panel/) and grab a **Content AI membership** of your choice [from here](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/#pricing).

Once done, follow the below tutorials to activate the Content AI module and configure its settings.

- [How to Use Rank Math’s Content AI for SEO?](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/)

- [How to Configure Content AI’s Global Settings?](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/)

## WordPress + Your Favorite Editor

Once configured, you can use Content AI inside your favorite WordPress Editors like [Block Editor](https://rankmath.com/kb/using-content-ai-in-block-editor/), [Classic Editor](https://rankmath.com/kb/using-content-ai-in-classic-editor/), [Elementor](https://rankmath.com/kb/using-content-ai-in-elementor/), and [Divi](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-in-divi/). Alternatively, you can use our dedicated [Content Editor](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-editor-ai-tool/) to leverage all the AI features Content AI offers.

## RankBot and Prompts

Content AI brings you **RankBot, a personal AI assistant within your WordPress Editor**, to help you with research and gathering insights.

You can write prompts to ask RankBot any question related to your topic, and it will provide you with relevant and reliable answers.
