# How to Write a Testimonial With Content AI

			
			

	

A business you patronize often encourages you to share a testimonial. But, often, users struggle to share their experiences in words.

Our Content AI includes a dedicated AI tool for testimonials that can help you create an authentic and persuasive testimonial effortlessly.

The most significant advantage of using AI tools is saving time; **[71% of users cite it as their number one benefit](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/).** In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create a testimonial using Content AI.

## Table Of Contents

- [Creating a Testimonial](#creating-a-testimonial)

- [Available Options in the Testimonial AI Tool](#testimonial-ai-tool-options)

## 1  Creating a Testimonial

When creating a testimonial, it is essential to provide specific details about the product or service, highlighting the positive experiences and outcomes. With the help of our [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/) Testimonial tool, you can create a well-written testimonial in just a few minutes.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Testimonial AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Testimonial**. Alternatively, you can choose the Testimonial AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Testimonial](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202494%201216'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Testimonial AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Testimonial AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Testimonial AI Tool

We’ll now discuss each option in the Testimonial AI tool in detail.

### 2.1  Product or Service

Enter the product or business name exactly as you want it to appear in the testimonial. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product or Service](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20221'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Features and Benefits

In 600 characters or less, enter the product’s characteristics and how they helped solve a problem you were experiencing.

**Note:**You can generate your features and benefits with the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/topic-research/). Remember to set the **Format**to List.****The **Topic**should begin with the product name followed by ‘features and benefits.’ E.g., iPhone 14 features and benefits.

![Features and Benefits](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20363'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Limitations and Drawbacks

In 600 characters or less, list the reasons a user may not like the product in this field. This is a required field.

**Note:** You can create your limitations and drawbacks using the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/topic-research/) in Content AI. You will include ‘limitations and drawbacks’ after the product name. Remember to set the **Format**to List.

![Limitations and Drawbacks](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20952%20358'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for in this field. The keywords should be at most 200 characters in total. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Length

Here, you will set the word count of the write-up. This is a required field.

- **Short** will set the output to 1-2 sentences

- **Medium** will set the output to 3-4 sentences

- **Long** will set the output to more than 5 sentences

![Select Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the testimonial. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten, depending on how many testimonials you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.10  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your testimonial.

![Click Generate on Testimonial](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 2.11  Copy and Insert

After creating your testimonial, hover over it and click **Copy**or **Insert**, as shown below.

- **Copy** will copy the testimonial to your clipboard. You can then proceed to paste it elsewhere.

- **Insert** will insert the testimonial into the Content Editor. You can then improve it using the other AI tools in the Content Editor.

![Copy/Insert testimonial.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
