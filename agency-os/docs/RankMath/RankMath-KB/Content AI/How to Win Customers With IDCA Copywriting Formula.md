# How to Win Customers With IDCA Copywriting Formula

			
			

	

IDCA ranks among the most popular copywriting formulas out there. It stands for Identify, Develop, Communicate, and Ask, the four steps the copy uses to persuade your audience to patronize your product or service.

![Copywriting Formulas Every Business Needs to Know (Plus Free Tools!)](https://img.youtube.com/vi/C0ANqGHT6_U/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

A winning IDCA copy begins by identifying and addressing the needs of your audience. It then informs them about your product or service before asking them to make a purchase. In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create IDCA-style content using Content AI.

## 1  Creating IDCA-style Content

Let us create IDCA-style to ensure high-quality and engaging content with our Content AI tool.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the IDCA AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **IDCA**. Alternatively, you can choose the IDCA AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select IDCA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202520%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the IDCA AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the IDCA AI Tool

We’ll now discuss the options in the IDCA AI tool in detail.

### 2.1  Product Name

Enter the product’s name exactly as you want it to appear in the content you are about to generate. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20195'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Product Description

In no more than 200 characters, enter the details of the product in this field. You should enter as many details as possible. Remember to separate the entries with a comma.

**Note:**If you don’t have a product description, you can generate one using the [Product Description AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-product-description-tool/).

![Product Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20957%20374'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can add multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Output Language

Select the output language for the content you are about to create. You can choose only one language here. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Number of Outputs

Enter one or two into this field, depending on how many IDCA-style content you want Content AI to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your IDCA-style content within a few seconds.

![Generate IDCA output.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to suggest more content, click **Generate More**.

![click Generate More](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Insert

Hover over the content you just generated and click **Insert**, as shown below. It will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Click Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the IDCA AI Tool

The IDCA AI tool organizes persuasive content through a logical flow, helping businesses tackle pain points, establish credibility, and encourage action. Here are some practical applications:

### 3.1  Crafting High-Impact Landing Page Copy

A landing page must instantly grab attention and convert visitors into customers. The IDCA AI tool structures landing page copy by clearly identifying a common problem, developing it to highlight why it matters, communicating the value of a product or service, and asking visitors to take action. This approach ensures clarity and improves conversions.

![Crafting High-Impact Landing Page Copy with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Build Before-and-After Narratives for Your Business

The IDCA AI tool helps businesses present a clear before-and-after narrative by identifying the client’s original problem, developing the challenges faced, communicating the solution and results, and finally asking prospects to take the next step (e.g., book a demo or sign up).

![Structuring Effective Customer Case Studies with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20922'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
