# How to Write Perfect SEO-Friendly Blog Posts with 1-Click

			
			

	![Write High Quality Articles in 1-Click Using Blog Post Wizard](https://img.youtube.com/vi/QtID9XnMx6s/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

Are you struggling to write a blog post and feeling stuck staring at a blank screen? Worry no more! Our Blog Post Wizard AI tool can generate long-form blog posts in seconds.

Among the [40+ AI tools](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-overview/#using-ai-tools) available in Content AI, the Blog Post Wizard AI tool is unique. Unlike other tools that modify or create small sections of content, this tool generates long-form content entirely from scratch.

Simply provide a few details about your blog post, and it will quickly transform them into a complete article.

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll show you how to write an entire blog post using our Blog Post Wizard.

## In this guide, we’ll cover:

- [Enable Content AI](#enable-content-ai)

- [Select the Blog Post Wizard AI Tool](#select-the-blog-post-wizard-ai-tool)

- [Step 1: Generate a Post Title](#generate-a-post-title)

- [Step 2: Generate a Post Outline](#generate-a-post-outline)

- [Step 3: Generate Your Blog Post](#generate-your-blog-post)

## 1  Enable Content AI

Head to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO**and enable the **Content AI** module.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**To use our [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/) features, you’ll need an active [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/).

## 2  Select the Blog Post Wizard AI Tool

Once done, navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI**. You will be presented with the 40+ AI Tools available in Content AI. Click on **Blog Post Wizard**, as shown below.

![Select blog post wizard tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201546%20908'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Step 1: Generate a Post Idea

You will be presented with the Blog Post Wizard AI tool, which offers various options to guide Content AI in generating the type of content you need. Let’s explore these options in detail.

### 3.1  Describe Your Industry/Niche

Enter a few words describing your industry or niche in this field. Content AI will use this to determine a relevant post idea for your blog post. If you do not wish to generate the blog post idea, you can click on the **Skip** button and proceed to the next step.

![Describe Your Industry or Niche](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202152%201298'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Audience

Enter the term that best describes your target audience in this field.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202040%201302'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Tone

Here, select the tone that best describes your writing style.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202064%201304'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Style

Select the style that best describes the format of the finished content.

![Select Style](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202080%201294'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Output Language

In the Output Language field, select your desired output language. You can select only one language. If a language is already specified, you must remove it and replace it with your preferred language.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202146%201284'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.6  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**.

![Click Generate button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202146%201284'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will generate a title for your upcoming post. If you need other post ideas, click **Regenerate**, and it will suggest another post title.

Once you get your desired post title, click **Next Step**.

![Click Next Step](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202130%201304'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Step 2: Generate a Post Outline

After generating your post title, the Blog Post Wizard AI tool will display some new fields. Let’s fill in these fields to generate a relevant post outline.

### 4.1  Topic

Content AI automatically uploads the title you just generated into this field. You are free to modify the title as you wish.

![Select topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201992%201284'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.2  Main Points & Ideas

In this field, outline the main points and ideas you want to discuss in the article in 400 characters or less. Start each new idea on a separate line. You can also generate ideas using AI by clicking the **Gener****ate with AI**link**.**

Optionally, you can leave this field blank and let Content AI generate ideas on its own.

![Add main points & ideas](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202092%201292'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.3  Focus Keyword

Enter the keyword you want to rank for into this field. If you have multiple keywords, the primary keyword should come first, followed by the secondary keywords. Ensure to click **Enter**on your keyboard after adding each keyword.

![Add Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202004%201304'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4.4  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will generate an outline for your article.

![Click Generate ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202296%201284'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 5  Step 3: Generate Your Blog Post

It’s time to turn the outline you just generated into a full-length blog post. To do so, click **Write Post**, as shown below.

![Click Write Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202146%201298'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will begin writing your post. Once it’s done, click **Create New Post**.

![Click Create New Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202250%201286'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The content will be transferred to a new WordPress post. You can now edit and format it to your liking. When you’re finished, click **Publish**.

![Content is transferred to a new post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201065'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
