# How to Use YouTube Video Description Generator in Content AI

			
			

	

Similar to website pages, YouTube videos include a Title and Description to feature them in search results. These metadata are not only helpful for Google Search but also for YouTube’s in-built ranking system.

**Simply put a clear and compelling description with relevant keywords can boost the visibility of your videos.**

That said, a poorly written description often puts off potential viewers without giving them a clear overview of what the video is about. If crafting the most compelling video description seems a daunting task for you, then fortunately, Content AI includes a YouTube Video Description Generator to help you craft the most relevant description for your videos in just a few clicks.

With the help of AI, **[47% of users are producing content faster](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/), and 35% are shifting their focus from quantity to quality.**

In this knowledge base article, we will show you how to create a YouTube video description with Content AI.

## 1  Creating a YouTube Video Description

Let us create YouTube video descriptions to maximize the impact of your YouTube videos and attract a wider audience using Content AI.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the YouTube Video Description AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO →** **Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **YouTube Video Description**. Alternatively, you can choose the YouTube Video Description AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select YouTube Video Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202524%201202'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the YouTube Video Description AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the YouTube Video Description AI Tool

We’ll discuss each option in the YouTube Video Description AI tool in detail.

### 2.1  Topic

In 200 characters or less, enter the title of your video or a short summary of your video. This is a required field and will help Content AI understand what your video is about and craft the most relevant description.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can generate a video title using the [Post Title](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-post-title-tool/) AI tool in Content AI. In the Post Title AI tool, you should set the **Style** of the Post Title AI tool to Vlog.

### 2.2  Audience

Select the audience from the dropdown menu that appears, or add a custom one that best describes your target audience.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Focus Keyword

In the Focus Keyword field, enter the keywords your video intends to rank for. Here, you can enter multiple keywords you wish to rank for, with each keyword being separated by a comma. This required field should contain 200 characters or less.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

The Tone field allows you to set your writing style. You can type in your desired tone or select from the dropdown menu that displays when you begin typing. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Output Language

The Output Language field allows you to choose the language in which the description will be generated. Click the output language field and select a language from the dropdown menu that appears. Please note you can select only one language here.

[See the full list of all the Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Number of Outputs

In the Number of Outputs field, enter the number of YouTube video descriptions you want Content AI to generate. Content AI can generate a max of 10 descriptions at once. But once your output is generated, you can always generate more.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

Once you’ve configured, click the **Generate** button.

![Generate Youtube Video Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201910%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will create your descriptions and display them in the Output field. If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**, as shown below.

![Generate More YouTube Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Copy and Insert

As you hover over the generated descriptions, you’ll see two options, namely, **Copy** and **Insert**.

Click the **Copy** button on the description of your interest to get it copied to your clipboard. You can now paste it directly to your YouTube video editor or anywhere. You can also click the **Insert** button to add the video description to Content Editor, where you can continue to edit it until it is perfect.

![Copy the YouTube video description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201256'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Finally, don’t forget to add the description to your YouTube video.

Here’s how this AI tool helps create optimized video descriptions for different content types:

### 3.1  Writing SEO-Optimized Video Descriptions for More Views

A well-crafted video description helps your content rank higher on YouTube search and appear in suggested videos.

This AI tool structures descriptions with focus keywords, timestamps, and compelling CTAs to boost discoverability and engagement.

![Writing SEO-Optimized Video Descriptions for More Views with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Descriptions for Brand Promotions

Brand promotion videos require descriptions that build trust and credibility. The AI ensures that focus keywords reflect brand values while highlighting unique selling points. For example, a skincare line launch might emphasize natural ingredients and customer testimonials.
