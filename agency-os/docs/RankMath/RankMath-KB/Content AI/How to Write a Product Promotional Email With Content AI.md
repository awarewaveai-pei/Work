# How to Write a Product Promotional Email With Content AI

			
			

	

Writing an effective product promotional email can be a game-changer for your marketing campaigns. But crafting an email that grabs attention, conveys the right message, and drives conversions can be challenging.

That’s where Content AI steps in to simplify the process. This powerful AI tool helps you optimize your email copy, ensuring it’s engaging, persuasive, and tailored for your target audience.

The top benefit of using AI tools? **Saving time!** In fact, [**71% of users in our say it’s their #1 advantage**](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/). That’s a huge productivity boost for anyone looking to streamline the content workflow.

In this knowledgebase guide, we’ll explore how to leverage Content AI to create product promotional emails that not only stand out but also achieve your marketing goals efficiently.

**Note:** If you run out of credits, you can [subscribe to a Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that meets your needs. You can enable the Content AI module by navigating to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** in your WordPress dashboard.

## 1  Navigate to the Email AI Tool

To create a promotional email for your products, you’ll need to use the Email AI tool, which is part of the Content AI suite. Once the Content AI module is enabled, navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → AI Tools** from your WordPress dashboard.

Once you’re on the AI Tools tab, use the search bar to look for “email” and select the **Email tool**option that appears, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Email AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201397%20821'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After selecting the Email tool, the full interface will open, allowing you to provide essential information about your product promotional email. This step helps guide the AI in generating a well-optimized and targeted response.

## 2  Create the Product Promotional Email

On the Email tool’s interface, you’ll find several fields, including **Email Brief**, **Call to Action**, **Audience**, **Tone**, **Length**, **Output Language**, and **Outputs**.

![The Content AI Email tool interface](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20924'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Before filling in these details, ensure you have the following information prepared: your product name, key features you’d like to highlight, the target audience, and the specific action you want recipients to take.

Once you have this information, let’s walk through how to fill in the details in the Email AI tool.

For instance, imagine you have a product called the **Smart Water Bottle**. You want to promote it to health-conscious individuals who struggle to stay hydrated throughout the day, with the goal of encouraging them to purchase the product.

Here’s how you can fill in the fields:

**1. Email Brief**: Provide a short description of the email you want to create. Highlight the key features and benefits of your product. For example:
“Stay hydrated with our Smart Water Bottle! This innovative bottle tracks your hydration levels and sends reminders to drink water throughout the day. Sync it with your smartphone to set personalized hydration goals and monitor your progress. Perfect for fitness enthusiasts and anyone looking to improve their health.”

**2. Call to Action**: Specify what action you want the recipients to take after reading the email. In this example, we’ll select **“Purchase a product or service.”**

**3. Audience**: Choose the audience you’re targeting. For this example, we’ll select **General Audience**, **Health Enthusiasts**, and **Fitness Enthusiasts**.  So, the fields we’ve filled in so far look this way:

![Fill in your information in the email ai tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20919'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**4. Tone**: Decide on the tone of your email. Here, we’ll choose a **Friendly, Motivating, and Enthusiastic** tone.

**5. Length**: Select the desired length for your email content. Options include short, medium, or long. For this example, we’ll choose **Long**.

**6. Output Language**: Choose the language for the generated email. We’ll use **US English** for this example.

**7. Outputs**: Indicate how many response variations you want. Here, we’ll select **2**.

Once you’ve customized the fields, click the **Generate** button to process your query, as shown below.

![Send your details to the Email AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Email AI tool will then quickly generate responses based on the details you’ve provided and the number of outputs selected.

To use a response, hover over your preferred email and click the **Copy** button to copy it to your clipboard.

![Copy your generated product promotional email from Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Before using the promotional email generated by Content AI, ensure that you review it thoroughly to confirm that it meets your requirements. Pay close attention to placeholders and replace them with accurate details. Personalize your email where needed, such as adding the recipient’s name in the greeting.

And that’s it! We hope this guide helps you create compelling promotional emails for your products using Content AI.
