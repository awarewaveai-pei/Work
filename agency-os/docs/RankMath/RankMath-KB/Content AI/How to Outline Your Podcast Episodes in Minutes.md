Are you spending too much time structuring and organizing your thoughts into a podcast episode?

## 1  Creating a Podcast Episode Outline

Let us create a podcast episode outline using Content AI.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Podcast Episode Outline AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO**→ **Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Podcast Episode Outline**. Alternatively, you can choose the Podcast Episode Outline AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Podcast Episode Outline](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202482%201196'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Podcast Episode Outline AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Podcast Episode Outline AI Tool

We’ll now discuss the options available in the Podcast Episode Outline AI tool.

### 2.1  Topic

In 200 characters or less, enter a summary of whatever you want to discuss in your podcast. This is a required field.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Host

In 200 characters or less, enter the name of the main host of this podcast.

![Host](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20949%20348'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Guest(s) or co-host

In 200 characters or less, enter the names of the other hosts and guests on the podcast exactly as you want them to be mentioned in the podcast. Remember to separate different names with a comma.

![Guest or co-host](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20953%20359'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Key Points

In 400 characters or less, enter the main ideas you want to discuss in the podcast. Remember to separate them with a comma. This is a required field.

![Key points](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20953%20342'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option(s) that best describes your podcast listeners from the dropdown field.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can add multiple tones here.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the podcast outline. You can only select one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten into this field, depending on how many podcast outlines you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your podcast outlines within a few seconds.

![Generate Podcast Outline](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more podcast episode outlines, click **Generate More**.

![Generate More ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201894%201240'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.10  Insert

Hover over an outline you wish to use and click **Insert**. The podcast outline will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Insert the Podcast episode outline](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Case Studies for the Podcast Episode Outline AI Tool

The Podcast Episode Outline AI tool is specifically designed to help podcast creators structure engaging and informative episodes by generating detailed outlines tailored to their topics, guests, and audience. Below are some use cases for this tool:

### 3.1  Interviewing Industry Experts

Well-structured interviews keep conversations engaging while extracting valuable insights from guests. This AI tool helps outline key discussion points, ensuring the episode remains informative and well-paced while giving the expert room to share deep insights.

![Create the Podcast Outline for interviewing Industry Experts with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201177%20849'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Creating Solo Episodes

Solo podcast episodes require a clear structure to keep the host on track and ensure listeners stay engaged. This AI tool helps break down topics into digestible sections, making solo episodes fluid, informative, and easy to follow.

![Creating Solo Podcast Episodes with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201180%20851'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Hosting Panel Discussions

Panel discussions bring multiple perspectives together, but without structure, they can become chaotic. This AI tool helps generate a balanced outline, ensuring each panelist gets the opportunity to contribute while keeping the discussion on track.

![Hosting Panel Discussions with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201177%20851'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
