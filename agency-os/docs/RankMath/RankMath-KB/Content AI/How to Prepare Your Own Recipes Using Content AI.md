Are you struggling to find new and exciting recipes that match your dietary preferences?

Our Content AI includes a dedicated recipe tool that takes the guesswork out of coming up with new recipes for your audience by providing you with an endless array of delicious and diverse recipes at your fingertips.

## 1  Preparing a Recipe

Let us now create delicious recipes using Content AI.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Recipe AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO**→**Content AI →** **Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Recipe**. Alternatively, you can choose the Recipe AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Recipe](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202508%201212'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Recipe AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Recipe AI Tool

We’ll now discuss the available options in the recipe AI tool.

### 2.1  Cuisine

In 200 characters or less, enter the cuisine for the recipe. This is a required field.

![Cuisine](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20949%20203'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Type of Dish

In 200 characters or less, enter the type of dish in this field. This is a required field.

![Type of Dish](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20948%20224'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Ingredients

In 1000 characters or less, enter the ingredients used in the recipe in this field. This is a required field. Remember to separate multiple entries with a comma.

![Ingredients](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20356'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Dietary Restrictions

In 400 characters or less, enter any specific meal requirements your audience may have in this field. Content AI will use this to decide which ingredients or cooking methods to include or exclude from the recipe. For example, the recipe will not contain meat products if you enter “Vegan” into this field.

**Note:**Content AI may include a few other ingredients in the recipe.

![Dietary restrictions](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20365'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-recipe-tool/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the recipe. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten into this field, depending on how many recipes you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your recipe in real-time.

![Generate Recipe](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will generate your recipe and display it in the Output field. If you need more recipe suggestions, click **Generate More**, as shown below.

![Generate Recipe](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201912%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.10  Insert

Once you identify a recipe you wish to use, hover over it and click **Insert**.

![Click Insert to insert the recipe in the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201890%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The recipe will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

## 3  Use Cases for the Recipe AI Tool

Creating delicious, well-structured, and easy-to-follow recipes can be time-consuming, especially when you need to tailor them to specific dietary needs or audience preferences. The Recipe AI tool simplifies this process by generating clear, engaging, and customized recipes based on your input. Here’s how:

### 3.1  Crafting Unique and Creative Recipes for Food Blogs

For food bloggers, standing out requires more than just listing ingredients- it’s about telling a story. sharing tips, and making the recipe feel special.

This AI tool helps by generating creative, well-structured recipes while allowing you to customize them based on cuisine type, audience, and dietary preferences.

![Crafting Unique and Creative Recipes for Food Blogs with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20924'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Creating Nutritional Meal Plans for Health & Fitness Blogs

For fitness enthusiasts, meal prep planners, and health-conscious audiences, recipes need to be nutritionally balanced, easy to follow, and aligned with specific dietary goals.

This AI tool helps generate healthy meal ideas tailored to specific restrictions, such as keta, gluten-free, or high-protein diets.

![Creating Nutritional Meal Plans for Health & Fitness Blogs with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Assisting in Recipe Adaptation for Vegan & Allergy-Friendly Cooking

For people with food allergies or plant-based diets, finding the right recipes can be difficult.

This AI tool helps by modifying traditional recipes to make them suitable for vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, or nut-free diets while maintaining flavor and texture.

![Assisting in Recipe Adaptation for Vegan with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Developing Kid-Friendly Recipes for Parents & Family Blogs

Parents often look for quick, nutritious, and kid-approved meals that make mealtime easier. This AI tool can help create fun, simple recipes that appeal to young taste buds while ensuring balanced nutrition.

![Developing Kid-Friendly Recipes for Parents with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
