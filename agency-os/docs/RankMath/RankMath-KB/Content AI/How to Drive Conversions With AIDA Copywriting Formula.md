# How to Drive Conversions With AIDA Copywriting Formula

			
			

	

AIDA stands for Attention, Interest, Desire, and Action. It is among the best copywriting methods for creating persuasive and compelling content that prompts the reader to take action.

_But how to write persuasive content using the AIDA framework?_

Use Content AI. With Content AI, you can create AIDA-style content that captivates and engages your audience. Just enter the product name into Content AI and sit back as it does the research and returns with your AIDA content within a few seconds.

![AIDA Tool: Write Sales Copy Easily with the AIDA Framework](https://img.youtube.com/vi/pHH1w_yNy4o/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create AIDA-style content using Content AI.

## Table Of Contents

- [Creating AIDA-style Content](#creating-aida-style-content)

- [Available Options in the AIDA AI Tool](#aida-ai-tool-options)

## 1  Creating AIDA-style Content

Let us create AIDA-style content with our Content AI tool to engage your audience, communicate the value of your offering, and motivate them to take the desired action, resulting in successful marketing campaigns.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the AIDA AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **AIDA**. Alternatively, you can choose the AIDA AI tool from your favorite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select AIDA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202510%201208'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the AIDA AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the AIDA AI Tool

We’ll now discuss all the available options in the AIDA AI tool.

### 2.1  Product Name

Enter the product’s name into this field. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20195'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Product Description

In no more than 200 characters, enter the details of the product in this field. You should enter as many details as possible. Remember to separate them with a comma.

**Note:**If you don’t have a product description, you can generate one using the [Product Description AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-product-description-tool/).

![Product Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20957%20374'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the AIDA-style content. You can only add one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and two into this field, depending on the number of outputs you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your AIDA-style content within a few seconds.

![Generate AIDA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201914%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to suggest more content, click **Generate More**.

### 2.8  Insert

Hover over the content you just created and click **Insert**, as shown below. The content will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Click Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%201254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
