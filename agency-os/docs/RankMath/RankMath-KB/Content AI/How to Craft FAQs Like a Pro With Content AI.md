# How to Craft FAQs Like a Pro With Content AI

			
			

	

Frequently Asked Questions are a great way to improve your readers’ experience, as they are bound to include valuable answers for the most common queries a reader might have.

_But how do you research the possible questions and relevant answers?_

Manually researching and crafting responses can be tedious and time-consuming. As more businesses use AI to streamline content creation, it’s worth noting that [**47% of users are producing content faster**](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/)**, and 35% are shifting their focus from quantity to quality.**

Our Content AI includes a dedicated tool for FAQs that streamlines your workflow and generates well-crafted FAQs.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create your frequently asked questions using Content AI.

## Table Of Contents

- [Creating Frequently Asked Questions](#creating-a-frequently-asked-questions)

- [Available Options in the Frequently Asked Question AI Tool](#frequently-asked-questions-options)

## 1  Creating Frequently Asked Questions

Let us discuss how you can create FAQs to provide clear and accurate responses for your readers using [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Frequently Asked Questions AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Frequently Asked Questions**. Alternatively, you can choose the Frequently Asked Questions AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Click Frequently Asked Questions](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202510%201226'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Frequently Asked Questions AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Frequently Asked Questions AI Tool

Let us now discuss the available options in the frequently asked questions AI tool.

### 2.1  Topic

In 200 characters or fewer, enter your post title in this field. This is a required field.

**Note:** You can create a post title using the [Post Title AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-post-title-tool/).

![Enter a Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Features and Benefits

Enter a list of product or service features followed by their corresponding benefits, separated by commas. For best results, keep each feature–benefit pair short and clear.

Example:_Fast page load times – improves user experience, Built-in SEO tools – helps rank higher on Google._

Remember, this field is limited to 600 characters.

![FAQs AI tool features and benefits option](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20657%20360'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword here, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for in this field. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma. The keywords should be at most 200 characters.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Tone

Select the option(s) that best describe your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the frequently asked questions. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and 25 into this field, depending on how many frequently asked questions you want to create in one go.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your frequently asked questions within a few seconds.

![Click Generate to FAQ AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more frequently asked questions, click **Generate More**.

![The Generate more button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Insert

Hover over the frequently asked questions and click Insert, as shown below. The Frequently Asked Questions accordion will be inserted into your Content Editor.

![Insert button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20922'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** Utilize Rank Math’s Content AI tool to effortlessly incorporate [frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://rankmath.com/kb/faq-schema-block/) into your website. Access the FAQ block and click on the AI icon to allow Content AI to generate the corresponding answers for you automatically.
