## 1  Using the Post Title AI Tool

Let us now see how the Post Title AI tool helps you to create attention-grabbing titles.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Post Title AI Tool

The next step is to navigate to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Post Title**. Alternatively, you can choose the Post Title AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Open Post Title AI Tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202534%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Post Title AI tool settings. Let’s go after the options one by one.

![Post Title AI Tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201890%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Post Title AI Tool

Let us look closely at the options in the Post Title AI tool.

### 2.1  Post Brief

In 400 characters or less, summarize your content and enter it into this field. This is a required field.

**Note:**Optionally, you can generate a blog post idea using the [Blog Post Idea AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-blog-post-idea-tool/) and paste it into the field.

![Post Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20959%20367'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Tone

In this field, select the option that best describes your writing tone. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Style

Enter the format of your content in this field so our Content AI can create a more accurate title. You can add multiple styles here. You can either select from the dropdown or choose custom. Here is the complete list of styles available to choose from:

- Listicle

- Tutorial

- Review

- Case Study

- Opinion

- News

- Newsjacking

- Personal

- Story-telling

- Guide

- Research-based

- Interview

- Infographic

- Debate

- Video Blog

- Vlog

- Podcast

- Audio Blog

- Quiz

- Contest

- Poll

- Comparison

- How-to

- FAQ

![Select Style](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201154%20896'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Length

Here, you will set the word count of the post title. This is a required field.

- Short will set the output to around 5 words

- Medium will set the output to around 10 words

- Long will set the output to around 15 words

![Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to create the post title. You can only enter one language here. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and 25, depending on how many post titles you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

Once you are ready to begin creating your post titles, you will click **Generate**.

![Generate Post Titles](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After creating your post title, click **Generate More**if you want Content AI to create more post titles.

![Generate more post titles](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201910%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Copy

The next step is to hover over the post title and click **Copy**, as shown below. You can then proceed to paste the title into your post.

![Copy Post Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Post Title AI Tool

Your blog title is like a storefront sign—if it’s dull, people walk right past. The Post Title AI tool ensures your titles are not just SEO-friendly but also engaging and relevant to your audience. Here’s how it helps:

### 3.1  Create Titles That Boost Engagements

People don’t just search for information—they want content that excites them. A title that sparks curiosity or emotion will naturally attract more clicks. This AI can suggest variations like intriguing questions or powerful words that make readers eager to learn more.

![Create Titles That Boost Engagements with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  A/B Testing Titles for Performance

Sometimes, the first title you think of isn’t the best. Smart marketers run A/B tests—trying different headlines to see which gets the most clicks. The Post Title AI helps by generating multiple variations so you can test and pick the winner.

![Generate titles for a/b testing using Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20925'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Creating Emotion-Driven Titles That Connect with Readers

People don’t just click on articles because they’re informative—they click because the title makes them feel something. Whether it’s excitement, curiosity, urgency, or even fear of missing out (FOMO), emotion-driven titles can drastically improve engagement.

By using storytelling, personal experiences, or urgency, Post Title AI helps you craft titles that connect on a deeper level and make readers feel like they _must_ click.

![Creating Emotion-Driven Titles using Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
