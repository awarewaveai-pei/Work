# Freeform Writing Using Content AI

			
			

	

Remember how you sometimes write without thinking about it? This is called freeform writing and is one of the [popular ways to generate great ideas](https://rankmath.com/blog/content-ideas/).

You can create your freeform writing using Content AI. This time, instead of typing the entire content from start to finish, you will type in a few words and leave Content AI to do the rest.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create freeform writing with Content AI.

## Table Of Contents

- [Freeform Writing AI Tool](#freeform-writing-ai-tool)

- [Available Options in the Freeform Writing AI Tool](#freeform-writing-options)

- [Use Cases for the Freeform Writing AI Tool](#use-cases-for-the-freeform-writing-ai-tool)

## 1  Freeform Writing AI Tool

Let us begin creating freeform writing using our Freeform Writing AI tool.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard**→ **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Freeform Writing AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Freeform Writing**. Alternatively, choose the Freeform Writing AI tool from your favorite WordPress Editors or the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select the Freeform Writing AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202496%201196'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Freeform Writing AI tool settings.

## 2  Available Options in the Freeform Writing AI Tool

Let’s discuss each of the fields one by one in detail.

### 2.1  What Do You Want to Write?

In 2000 characters or less, hint Content AI about the write-up you wish to create. This is a required field.

![What do you want to write?](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20363'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Main Points & Ideas

In 400 characters or less, enter the key points you want to cover in the write-up. Optionally, leave the field blank to allow Content AI to decide on the points to cover.

![Main points and ideas](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20352'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Length

Here, you will set the word count of the write-up. This is a required field.

- **Short** will set the word count to 1-2 sentences

- **Medium** will set the word count to 3-4 sentences

- **Long** will set the word count to more than 5 sentences

![Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the write-up. You can add only one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your write-up within a few seconds.

![Click Generate output in Freeform writing AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20957%20597'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more write-ups, click **Generate More**.

### 2.8  Copy and Insert

After creating your write-up, hover over it and click **Copy**or **Insert**, as shown below.

- **Copy** will copy the write-up to your clipboard. You can then proceed to paste it elsewhere.

- **Insert** will insert the write-up into the Content Editor. You can then improve it using the other AI tools in the Content Editor.

![Copy or Insert the Freeform Writing ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201894%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Freeform Writing AI Tool

The Freeform Writing AI tool is designed for situations where you want to generate text based on prompts, giving you the flexibility to create content that’s imaginative, technical, or informative. Here are some scenarios you may use it:

### 3.1  Crafting Engaging Storytelling Content

Storytelling is one of the most powerful ways to capture attention and connect with an audience emotionally. Whether for brand storytelling, fiction writing, or case studies, this AI tool helps craft compelling narratives that engage readers and deliver a memorable message.

It can generate dialogues, plot twists, or even entire short stories based on your input, making it useful for bloggers, marketers, and fiction writers.

![Crafting Engaging Storytelling Content with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20915'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Creating Fictional World-Building & Creative Writing Pieces

For authors and screenwriters, crafting a unique fictional world is a challenging yet exciting process. This AI tool can generate vivid descriptions of places, characters, and lore, helping writers develop immersive settings and engaging stories.

![Creating Fictional World-Building with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
