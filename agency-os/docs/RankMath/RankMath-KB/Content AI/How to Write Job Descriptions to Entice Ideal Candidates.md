Job descriptions are vital for communicating the exact requirements for the role and attracting top talent. However, most businesses struggle to craft them and are often prone to inconsistencies or lack of clarity.

Using Content AI’s Job Description AI tool, you can eliminate these issues and create customized job descriptions highlighting key responsibilities, qualifications, and company culture, ensuring you attract the perfect candidates for your organization.

## 1  Creating a Job Description

Creating a job description is a crucial step in the hiring process. It serves as a comprehensive guide for potential candidates, outlining the responsibilities, qualifications, and expectations for the role.

By utilizing our Job Description AI tool, you can efficiently generate accurate and detailed job descriptions, ensuring a clear and concise representation of the position.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Job Description AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Job Description**. Alternatively, you can choose the Job Description AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Job Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202502%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Job Description AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Job Description AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201892%201238'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Job Description AI Tool

We’ll now discuss all the available options in the Job Description AI tool.

### 2.1  Company Name

Enter the company name exactly as you want it to appear in the job description. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Company Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20206'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Job Title

Enter the job title exactly as you want it to appear in the job description. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Job Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Requirements

In 400 characters or less, enter the skills, experience, and qualifications you require from anyone applying for the job. This is a required field. Remember to separate multiple requirements with a comma.

![Requirements](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20362'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Responsibilities

In 400 characters or less, enter the tasks the applicant will be expected to perform if they get the job. This is a required field. Remember to separate multiple responsibilities with a comma.

![Responsibilities](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20962%20376'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Output Language

Choose the output language in which you want Content AI to create the job description. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and three into this field, depending on how many job description suggestions you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your job description.

![Click Generate on Job Description AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201910%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 2.10  Insert

Once you identify a job description you wish to use, hover over it and click **Insert**. It will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Insert Job Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201908%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Job Description AI Tool

The Job Description AI tool helps HR teams, recruiters, and businesses create clear, engaging, and standardized job descriptions effortlessly. Here are some scenarios you can use it:

### 3.1  Standardizing Job Postings Across Departments

Ensuring uniformity in job descriptions across different departments can be difficult, especially for large organizations. The Job Description AI tool helps create standardized descriptions that maintain company branding, use consistent language, and align with hiring policies.

This prevents miscommunication and ensures all job postings reflect the company’s values and expectations.

![Standardizing Job Postings Across Departments with Content Ai](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20912'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Attracting Diverse Talent Pools

Creating inclusive job descriptions is essential for building a diverse workforce. You can use this tool to generate job postings with bias-free, inclusive language, encouraging applications from candidates of all backgrounds. It ensures that job ads don’t unintentionally discourage qualified candidates from applying.

![Attracting Diverse Talent Pools in Job descriptions with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Supporting Small Businesses Without Dedicated HR Teams

Many small businesses lack a dedicated HR department, making it difficult to create polished job descriptions. This AI tool helps business owners craft professional-quality job postings that clearly communicate job expectations, making it easier to attract skilled applicants.

![Content AI helps create Job descriptions for Small Businesses](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
