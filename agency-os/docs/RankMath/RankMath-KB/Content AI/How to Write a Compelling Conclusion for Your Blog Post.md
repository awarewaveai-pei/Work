# How to Write a Compelling Conclusion for Your Blog Post

			
			

	

Many readers drop off when they realize the content is coming to an end. This means they may not accurately understand your take on the topic and do not get to see your call to action.

So, how do you create a relevant conclusion that keeps your readers hooked and encourages them to respond to your call to action?

**AI is helping [34% of users generate more creative content ideas](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/), and 44% spend less time on planning.**

__

With Content AI, you will generate a relevant and compelling conclusion for your blog post in seconds. In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create a blog post conclusion using Content AI.

## 1  Creating a Blog Post Conclusion

Let us now discuss how you can create a well-crafted conclusion for your blog post with the help of [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Blog Post Conclusion AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Blog Post Conclusion**, as shown below. Alternatively, you can choose the Blog Post Conclusion AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Open Blog Post conclusion AI Tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202500%201210'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Blog Post Conclusion AI tool settings. Let’s go after the fields one by one.

![Blog Post Conclusion settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Blog Post Conclusion AI Tool

Let us discuss each option in the Conclusion AI tool.

### 2.1  Topic

In no more than 200 characters, summarize your post and enter it into this field. Optionally, you can enter your focus keyword. This is a required field.

![Topic](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20955%20194'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Main Argument

In 400 characters or less, summarize the conclusion you want a reader to reach after consuming your content. Optionally, enter your post title, and Content AI will suggest an appropriate conclusion. This is a required field.

**Note:**If you don’t have a post title, you can generate one using the [Post Title AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-post-title-tool/).

![Main Argument](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20951%20345'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Call to Action

You may want your visitor to perform an action after consuming your content. Maybe you want them to consume other content or sign up for your newsletter.

![Call to Action](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201173%20758'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in a tone or select one from the dropdown menu when you begin typing. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Audience

Select the option that best describes your readers in this field. You can type in a tone or select one from the dropdown menu when you begin typing. You can add multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

In this field, choose the language in which you want to generate the conclusion. If the field already has a language, you will remove it before entering a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and five into this field, depending on how many blog post conclusions you want it to generate.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

When you are ready to generate your conclusions, click the **Generate**button.

![Generate conclusion](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will create and display your conclusions in the Output field. Click **Generate More** if you want Content AI to create more conclusions.

![Click Generate More](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201910%201254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Insert

Hover over the conclusion and click **Insert**. It will be inserted into the Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools available in Content AI.

![Insert blog post conclusion](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
