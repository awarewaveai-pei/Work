## 1  Using the Paragraph Rewriter AI tool

We’ll now discuss the Paragraph Rewriter AI tool in detail.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Paragraph Rewriter AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Paragraph Rewriter**. Alternatively, you can choose the Paragraph Rewriter AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Open Paragraph Rewriter](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202518%201220'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Paragraph Rewriter AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Paragraph Rewriter AI Tool

Let us now discuss the options available in the Paragraph Rewriter tool.

### 2.1  Original Paragraph

Copy the paragraph you want to rewrite and paste it into this field. The paragraph should be at most 1000 characters. If it is over 1000 characters, you should split it before entering it into the field. This is a required field.

![Original Paragraph](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20986%20343'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma. The keywords should be at most 200 characters.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the rewritten paragraph. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and three into this field, depending on how many suggestions you want Content AI to generate.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin rewriting your paragraph.

![Click generate output in paragraph rewriter](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

![Generate more outputs in Paragraph Rewriter](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Copy or Insert

Hover over to the rewritten paragraph and click **Copy**or **Insert**, as shown below.

- If you click **Copy**, the paragraph will be copied to your clipboard.

- If you click **Insert**, the paragraph will be inserted into the Content Editor.

![Copy rewritten paragraph](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201894%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases of the Paragraph Rewriter AI Tool

The Paragraph Rewriter AI tool can help you effortlessly enhance clarity, readability, and content engagement in several scenarios. Here’s a few of them:

### 3.1  Content Editing and Revision

Rewriting is a crucial part of the editing process. Whether you’re polishing an article, blog post, or report, this AI tool helps you restructure sentences, improve flow, and eliminate redundancy without changing your core message.

Instead of manually rewriting each paragraph, you can use this tool to create multiple refined versions and choose the one that best fits your style.

![Rewrite the paragraph of a sentence with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  SEO Optimization and Keyword Integration

For bloggers and marketers, rewriting content to include keywords naturally is crucial for SEO (Search Engine Optimization). This AI tool helps you restructure paragraphs **without [keyword stuffing](https://rankmath.com/seo-glossary/keyword-stuffing/),** making the content more search-engine-friendly while keeping it reader-friendly.

![Optimizing a content for SEO using Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Language Improvement

Even grammatically correct content can sometimes sound unnatural or awkward. This AI tool refines sentence structure, enhances fluency, and makes your writing sound more professional or conversational, depending on your needs.

It’s particularly useful for non-native English speakers who want to improve their writing or for professionals ensuring their communication is clear and polished.

![Improve languages with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Repurposing Content for Different Platforms

Businesses and content creators often need to tailor their messaging for different platforms, such as blogs, social media, and newsletters. Instead of manually rewriting content for each channel, you can use the Paragraph Rewriter AI tool to rephrase your existing text while keeping the message intact.

This allows you to adapt content efficiently while ensuring consistency across different platforms. Whether you’re repurposing a long-form blog post into a concise social media update or adjusting a professional article into a more conversational format, the tool helps you modify phrasing while preserving clarity and meaning.

![repurpose content with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20920'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
