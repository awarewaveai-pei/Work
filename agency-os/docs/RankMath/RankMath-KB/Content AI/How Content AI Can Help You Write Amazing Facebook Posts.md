## 1  Creating a Facebook Post

Let’s begin creating a Facebook post that resonates with your audience and achieves your desired objectives using [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Facebook Post AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Facebook Post**. Alternatively, you can choose the Facebook Post Description AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Facebook Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202506%201216'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Facebook Post AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Facebook Post AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201898%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Facebook Post AI Tool

We’ll now discuss each option in the Facebook Post tool in detail.

### 2.1  Topic Brief

In 400 characters or less, enter a summary of the Facebook post you want to create. This is a required field.

![Topic Brief](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20953%20362'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Audience

Enter the details of your target audience in this field. You can select multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Focus Keyword

Enter the words or sentences you want Content AI to include in the Facebook post. Content AI will include the keywords or their variations in the Facebook post. The keywords should be at most 200 characters in total. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Length

Here, you can choose the length of the output.

- Short –  generates an output with 1-2 sentences

- Medium – generates an output with 3-4 sentences

- Long – generate an output with at least 5 sentences

![Length](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Output Language

Select the output language in which you want Content AI to generate the Facebook post. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Select Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and ten into this field, depending on how many Facebook posts you want to create.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin creating your Facebook post.

![Click Generate on Facebook Post AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201892%201254'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

### 2.9  Copy

After creating your Facebook post, hover over it and click **Copy**. You can then proceed to paste it into Facebook.

![Copy the Facebook Post](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201902%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Facebook Post AI Tool

​The Facebook Post AI tool empowers you to create engaging and tailored Facebook posts effortlessly. By leveraging AI-driven content generation, this tool streamlines the process of crafting posts that resonate with your target audience, maintain consistency, and enhance your social media presence.​ Here’s how:

### 3.1  Promoting New Products or Services

Announcing new offerings effectively on Facebook can significantly boost awareness and sales. This AI tool assists by generating compelling posts that highlight key features and benefits, ensuring the message aligns with your brand voice and appeals to your audience. This approach saves time and ensures consistency across promotional content.

![Promoting New Products or Services  oon Facebook with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20921'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Sharing Blog Posts or Articles

Driving traffic to your website through Facebook requires engaging posts that entice users to click through. This AI tool crafts summaries or teasers of your blog content, emphasizing intriguing aspects to encourage readership. This method enhances content reach and fosters audience engagement.

![Sharing Blog Posts or Articles on Facebook with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201398%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Announcing Events or Webinars

Effectively promoting events on Facebook is crucial for maximizing attendance. This AI tool generates informative and engaging posts detailing event specifics, such as date, time, and key highlights, while maintaining an inviting tone that encourages participation.

![Announcing Events or Webinars on Facebook  with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20914'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Celebrating Company Milestones

Sharing achievements and milestones humanizes your brand and strengthens customer relationships. The Facebook Post AI tool helps articulate these accomplishments in a way that resonates with your audience, expressing gratitude and reinforcing brand loyalty.

![Celebrating Company Milestones on Facebook with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
