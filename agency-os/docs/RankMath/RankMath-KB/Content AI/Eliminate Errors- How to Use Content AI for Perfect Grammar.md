Most individuals and businesses struggle with writing error-free content due to grammatical errors. These mistakes can negatively impact credibility, and existing manual proofreading methods are time-consuming and prone to human error.

Thankfully, our Content AI includes a dedicated AI tool that fixes grammatical errors on the go. 
The primary benefit of using AI tools is time savings, and [71% of users list this as their top advantage](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/).

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll show you how to fix grammar errors using [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

## 1  Using the Fix Grammar AI Tool

Let us now discuss the Fix Grammar AI Tool that’ll produce polished and grammar-free written content.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Fix Grammar AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Fix Grammar**. Alternatively, you can choose the Fix Grammar AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select Fix Grammar AI tool.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202512%201166'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Fix Grammar AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

![Fix Grammar AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201252'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Available Options in the Fix Grammar AI Tool

Let us now discuss all the available options in the Fix Grammar AI tool.

### 2.1  Text

Paste the text that you want to check for grammatical errors. The text should be at most 2000 characters. If it exceeds 2,000 characters, you should split it before entering it into the field. This is a required field.

![Text](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Number of Outputs

Content AI can generate a single output and fix the sentence.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Generate

Click **Generate**after filling in the required fields. Content AI will begin fixing your sentence.

![Generate](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201904%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you need more suggestions, click **Generate More**.

![Generate More](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201242'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Copy or Insert

After fixing your sentence, hover over it and click **Copy**. You can then proceed to paste it wherever you want. Click **Insert** to insert the sentence in the Content Editor.

![Copy/Insert Option in the Fix Grammar AI Tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
