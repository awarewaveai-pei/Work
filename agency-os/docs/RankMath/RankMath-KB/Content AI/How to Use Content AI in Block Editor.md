# How to Use Content AI in Block Editor

			
			

	

Content AI offers several AI Tools to help you with your writing tasks. To ease your workflow and save you from the burden of switching tabs while writing, we’ve integrated all these AI features into Block Editor, so you can focus on creating quality content for your readers.

Before we discuss the various Content AI features available in the Block Editor, please ensure that you’ve enabled the Content AI and Link Genius modules. To do that, head to **Rank Math SEO** from your WordPress dashboard. Then, enable the Content AI module, as shown below.

**Note:** If you ever run out of credits, you can easily subscribe to a [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

![Enable the Content AI module](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Enable-the-Content-AI-module-1.png)

Once done, enable the **Link Genius** module, as shown below.

![Enable the Link Genius module](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Enable-the-Link-Genius-module-2.png)

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll walk you through how to use Content AI in Block Editor.

## 1  // Shortcut

Content AI can be used anywhere with the Block Editor using the // shortcut. To use this shortcut, simply follow the below steps.

### 1.1  Edit the Post in Block Editor

Open the post that you want to edit in the Block Editor.

![Edit the post in Block Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202056%20594'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2  Open Content AI by Rank Math Block

In the Block Editor, press // to add the AI Assistant Block instantly, as shown below.

![Open Content AI using shortcode](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202386%201054'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.3  Write a Prompt or Select the AI tool

In the AI Assistant Block, enter your prompt and hit Enter to let Content AI start generating the output.

![AI Assistant Block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202346%20642'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Alternatively, you can type the Content AI tool that you want to use and select it from the dropdown list.

![Open Content AI tool using shortcode](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201834%20750'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, the corresponding Content AI tool will pop up on the screen, where you can fill in the relevant fields and hit the **Generate** button.

![AI tool will open](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201900%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, the Content AI will start generating the output on the right side of the screen. As you hover over the generated output, you will notice two options, namely, **Copy** and **Insert**.

![Copy and Insert options in output](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202032%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The **Copy** option allows you to copy the output of your choice to your clipboard, so you can paste it anywhere you prefer to use it.

The **Insert** option will insert the output directly into the Block Editor.

## 2  Content AI Blocks

You can also access the Content AI blocks while you write within your block editor, using the:

- AI assistant block

- Related posts block

Here’s how to access both Content AI blocks.

### 2.1  Edit the Post in Block Editor

Open the post that you want to edit in the Block Editor.

![Edit the post in Block Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202056%20594'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Open Block Library

In the Editor window, you can add a new block by clicking the **+** icon that appears on the left sidebar as well as on the right end of a new line. Alternatively, you can type / in a new line to open the block library.

![Access Block Library](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201840%20826'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Open Content AI Block

Once done, type **Content AI** into the block inserter’s search bar. You will see two Content AI blocks:

- **AI assistant [Content AI]**: Inserts the Content AI assistant into your editor

- **Related posts** **[Content AI]**: Inserts the related posts block into your editor

Click on the block you want to add to your article.

![Content AI (Related posts and AI assistant) blocks in the Block library](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20711'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you selected the AI assistant [Content AI] block, you can proceed to write a prompt and let the AI assistant generate the output. You can also use the AI assistant to open any AI tool, as we walked through earlier.

![AI Assistant Block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202544%20766'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you selected the Related posts [Content AI] block, a related posts block will be inserted into your content. If you want to customize it, you can refer to this guide on [customizing the related posts block](https://rankmath.com/kb/customize-related-posts/) so it meets your needs.

![Related posts block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20714'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Content AI Panel

Apart from these methods, you can also access Content AI features from the dedicated Content AI panel. To access it, click the **Content AI icon** that appears at the top-right corner of the Block Editor screen.

![Click Content AI icon to open Content AI panel](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20576'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Alternatively, you can access the Content AI Panel from the familiar Rank Math Meta Box.

![Open Content AI from Rank Math Meta Box](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20885'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Here’s how the Content AI Panel would look like, where you can access the **Research tool**, **Writing tool**, **AI Tools**, and **RankBot**.

![Content AI Panel](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201236'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.1  Research

In the Research section, you can [research your desired target keyword](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/#research-keywords) and receive insightful recommendations from our Content AI. Enter the keyword in the FOCUS KEYWORD field, click the **Research** button, and wait for the magic.

![Research View in Block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201218%20990'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Write

In the [Write](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-editor/#write-tab) section, add the relevant details, and our Content AI will start generating the output in the Block editor, as shown below.

![Write section in Block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201438%20654'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use `Ctrl + /` to generate the output. This keyboard shortcut works only when the Write tab is active in the block editor. This sends the tone and instructions as well.

### 3.3  AI Tools

In the AI Tools section, you can find [40+ Content AI](https://rankmath.com/?post_type=ht_kb&p=1177288&preview=true#content-ai-tools) tools that you can use right within the Block Editor.

You can use any of the listed AI tools of your choice. The corresponding Content AI tool will pop up on the screen, where you can fill in the relevant fields and click the **Generate** button.

![AI tool in Block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201232'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once the content is generated, click the **Copy/Insert** option to either copy the content or insert it in the Block Editor.

![Copy/Insert in Block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201222'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Chat

You can ask questions about your subject or regarding SEO, as [Rankbot](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-rankbot-ai/) is effectively integrated into your Block Editor.

![Chat section in Block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20606'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Command Center

Content AI is available for every use case, and we understand that you’ll need these AI tools not just for writing new content but also for editing and improving your existing content. Hence, we have a dedicated Command Center that is available to access within your Block Editor.

To use it, place your cursor over a block, and in the Block Toolbar that appears at the top, click the Content AI icon. The Command Center will appear with all the available features.

![Command Center](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20713'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also use the keyboard shortcut `//` to enable the Command box in the Block Editor.

### 4.1  Run as Command

This option lets you convert a block of text into a prompt and generate output on the fly.

### 4.2  Write More

Do you want to continue writing content? Click the Write More option and watch the Content AI write the article for you.

### 4.3  Improve

Not happy with the existing content? Simply click the Improve option to rewrite the existing paragraphs.

### 4.4  Summarize

Have a long block of text? Click the Summarize option to let Content AI read them and generate a brief summary for you.

### 4.5  Write Analogy

Are you unsure how to explain a difficult concept to your readers? Choose this option, and Content AI will write analogies for your audience to understand them with a familiar example.

### 4.6  Fix Grammar

Do you want to proofread your block of text? Choose the **Fix Grammar** option, and Content AI will check your paragraph and ensure your text is free from Grammatical errors.

### 4.7  Generate Link

This option lets you add a relevant internal link to your content. Highlight the relevant text, and Content AI will suggest internal links to include. The highlighted text will serve as the anchor text of the link.

## 5  Generate Alt Texts

Content AI also allows you to generate alt texts for your images right from your Block Editor. To generate one, follow the below steps

### 5.1  Edit the Post in Block Editor

Open the post that you want to edit in the Block Editor.

![Edit the post in Block Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202056%20594'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.2  Upload an Image to Block Editor

You can use the Content AI to generate alt texts for your images while uploading them from your Block Editor.

To do this, simply click on the ‘Add block’ icon and type ‘image’ in the search bar. Then, select the Image block when it appears, as shown below.

![Selecting the Image block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201381%20577'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This will insert the Image block into your content, providing options to add images. Next, choose the ‘Media Library’ option to upload or insert your desired image.

![Selecting the Media Library option in block editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201354%20701'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once you’ve uploaded the image, click on it and navigate to the right side. You will see the **Generate Alt**button next to the Alt text field. Click on it, and the AI will swiftly generate an alt text for your image, as illustrated below.

![Using Content AI Generate Alt button to generate alt text for an image](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201835%20822'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 5.3  Generate Alt Text for Inserted Images

If you have already inserted images into your content, you can still generate alt texts for them using Content AI.

To do this, click on the image and access the image settings from the Settings panel. Here, you will find the ‘Generate Alt’ option next to the ALTERNATIVE TEXT field. Click on it, and the AI will immediately generate the alt text for the image, as illustrated below.

![generate alt text](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20957'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once you’re done editing your content, click the**Publish/Save**button above to save your changes.

Please note that the Alt Text generation feature is currently available only in English. Support for more languages will be added in the future.

## 6  Generate Internal Links

Together, Content AI and AI Link Genius let you generate relevant internal links to include in your content. The process is automatic and requires no additional input from your end. Instead, Content AI and AI Link Genius will scan your content and site, and recommend internal links.

To get started, click the **Rank Math SEO**icon. Once done, scroll down to the **Link opportunities** panel and click on it.

![Click on the Link opportunities panel](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20711'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Then click **Generate**.

![Click Generate](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20711'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI and AI Link Genius will generate some relevant internal links to include in your article, and show them to you. Each link includes two options. There is also a Refresh button at the bottom that lets you check for additional links.

The available options include:

- **Insert link**: Click to insert the link into your content

- **Copy link URL**: Click to copy the link’s URL to your clipboard

Click **Insert link** to add the link to your content. It will then be added to your content.

![Click Insert link to add the link to your content](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20703'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 7  Generate Related Posts Block

The related posts block allows you to add a relevant related posts block to your content. To get started, click the **Rank Math SEO** icon, as shown below.

![Click the Rank Math SEO icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20705'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, scroll down to the **Related posts** panel, and click on it. Then click **Generate**.

![Click on the Related posts panel and click Generate](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20711'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Some relevant internal links will be generated, as shown below. It also contains two buttons:

- **Refresh**: Check for additional related posts

- **Insert related posts**: Insert the related posts block into your content

Click **Insert related posts** to add the related posts block to your content. The related posts block will now be added to your content. If you want to customize it, head to this guide on [customizing your related posts block](https://rankmath.com/kb/customize-related-posts/).

![Click insert related posts to add the related posts block](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20706'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
