# How to Craft Headlines Using HERO Framework

			
			

	

The HERO copywriting formula means Headline, Explanation, Reveal, and Offer. It is one copywriting formula that works well when you need to capture your reader’s attention and persuade them to get your product.

With the assistance of Content AI, you can now effortlessly create headlines using the HERO formula that grabs your audience’s attention and keeps them hooked.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create a HERO-style headline with Content AI.

## 1  Creating HERO-style Content

Creating HERO-style content involves crafting high-quality, engaging, and impactful content that resonates with a wide audience. Let us create HERO-style content that can effectively communicate a brand’s values, build trust, and ultimately achieve marketing objectives using our Content AI.

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Rank Math SEO**. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the HERO AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **HERO**. Alternatively, you can choose the HERO AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Select HERO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202498%201202'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the HERO AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the HERO AI Tool

We’ll now discuss the options available in the HERO AI tool.

### 2.1  Product Name

Enter the product’s name exactly as you want it to appear in the description you are about to generate. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20195'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Product Description

In no more than 200 characters, enter the details of the product in this field. You should enter as many details as possible. Remember to separate the entries with a comma.

**Note:**If you don’t have a product description, you can generate one using the [Product Description AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/product-description/).

![Product Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20957%20374'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

Select the output language for the content you are about to create. You can choose only one language here. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

## 3  Use Cases for the HERO AI Tool

The HERO AI Tool follows the HERO copywriting framework, which stands for:

- **Headline** – Captures immediate attention.

- **Explanation** – Briefly expands on the topic.

- **Reveal** – Introduces the solution or key message.

- **Offer** – Provides a compelling call to action.

This structure is highly effective for marketing content, landing pages, social media, and advertisements because it guides the reader toward taking action. Here are some practical ways to use the HERO AI tool:

### 3.1  Crafting High-Converting Landing Pages

Landing pages need to immediately hook visitors and guide them toward taking action, whether it’s signing up, purchasing, or learning more. The HERO framework ensures that landing page content is clear, engaging, and persuasive by structuring it with an attention-grabbing headline, a brief explanation, a strong message, and a call to action.

For example, a landing page for an online business course would use a powerful headline that speaks directly to entrepreneurs’ pain points, followed by a short explanation, the reveal of what the course offers, and a strong CTA encouraging sign-ups.

![Crafting High-Converting Landing Pages with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20916'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Writing Click-Worthy Social Media Ads

Social media ads must stop the scroll and instantly hook the audience. The HERO AI tool ensures ad copy is structured for maximum impact—starting with a bold headline, followed by a concise explanation, then revealing the main benefit or offer, and ending with a compelling CTA.

![Writing Click-Worthy Social Media Ads with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20922'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
