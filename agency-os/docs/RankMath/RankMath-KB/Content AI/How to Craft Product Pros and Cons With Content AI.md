# How to Craft Product Pros and Cons With Content AI

			
			

	

If you review products or create review-style content, you will often want to create content that reveals the pros and cons of a product.

However, product pros and cons can be hard to write. They require in-depth research, and you must be careful about your word choice to avoid praising or ridiculing the product.

Thankfully, writing product pros and cons is about to get easier. Using Content AI, you can create a product description containing the advantages and disadvantages of the product in a few seconds.

And it’s worth noting that the most significant advantage of using AI tools is saving time; [**71% of users cite it as their number one benefit.**](http://The%20most%20significant%20advantage%20of%20using%20AI%20tools%20is%20saving%20time%20-%20according%20to%20our%20Future%20of%20Search%20Report,%2071%25%20of%20users%20cite%20it%20as%20their%20number%20one%20benefit.)

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to create a product’s pros and cons with Content AI.

## 1  Creating a Product Pros & Cons

Let’s now discuss creating Product Pros & Cons using Rank Math’s [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

### 1.1  Enable the Content AI Module

The first step is to head over to **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Modules** from your WordPress dashboard. Here, scroll down to find the **Content AI**module and enable it as shown below if you haven’t already.

![Enable Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202160%20710'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you exhaust your feature usage limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

### 1.2  Select the Product Pros & Cons AI Tool

Next, head over to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Content Editor**, as shown below.

![Navigate to the Content Editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20800'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Content Editor, click **AI Tools** and select **Product Pros and Cons**. Alternatively, you can choose the Product Pros and Cons AI tool from your favourite WordPress Editors or from the AI Tools tab, whichever suits your writing flow.

![Click Product Pros & Cons](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202516%201218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the Product Pros and Cons AI tool settings. Let’s discuss each of these fields one by one.

## 2  Available Options in the Product Pros & Cons AI Tool

Let us now discuss each option in the Product Pros & Cons AI tool.

### 2.1  Product Name

Enter the product’s name exactly as you want it to appear in the pros and cons. This is a required field and should contain 200 characters or less.

![Product Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20956%20195'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Features and Benefits

In 600 characters or less, enter the characteristics of the products, along with the problems it will solve for the user or the advantages the user will derive from using it.

- The features include things like its color, origin, and construction material.

- The benefits explain how the features solve a problem or work to the advantage of the user. For example, a thick coat will keep the wearer warm even in the coldest winter.

**Note:**You can generate your features and benefits with the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-topic-research-tool/). Remember to set the **Format**to ‘List’****and enter your product name followed by ‘features and benefits’ as the **Topic**. E.g., iPhone 14 features and benefits.

![Features and Benefits](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20363'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Limitations and Drawbacks

In 600 characters or less, list the reasons a user may not like the product. This is a required field.

**Note:** You can create your limitations and drawbacks using the [Topic Research AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-topic-research-tool/) in Content AI. You will include ‘limitations and drawbacks’ after the product name.

![Limitations and Drawbacks](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20366'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Audience

Select the option that best describes your target audience in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple audiences.

[See the full list of all the Audience options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#audience)

![Select Audience](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201181%20988'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Focus Keyword

Enter your primary focus keyword, followed by any other keyword you want to rank for in this field. Remember to separate multiple keywords with a comma. The keywords should be at most 200 characters.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20963%20205'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.6  Tone

Select the option that best describes your writing tone in this field. You can type in the word or select from the dropdown menu. You can enter multiple tones.

[See the full list of all the Tone options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/blog/tone-in-writing/)

![Select Tone](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201162%20927'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.7  Output Language

Enter the output language in which you want Content AI to create the pros and cons. You can only enter one language. If the field already contains a language, delete it and select a new one.

[See the full list of all the Output Language options we offer ➜](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-content-ai-global-settings/#languages)

![Output Language](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201870%201310'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.8  Number of Outputs

Enter a number between one and five, depending on the number of pros and cons you want to generate.

![Number of Outputs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20815%20123'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.9  Generate

After filling in the required fields, click **Generate**. Content AI will create your pros and cons within a few seconds.

![Generate Product Pros and Cons](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201892%201248'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you want Content AI to create more pros and cons, click **Generate More**.

![Generate More Product Pros & Cons](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201906%201246'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.10  Insert

Hover over the pros and cons and click **Insert**, as shown below. The product pros and cons will be inserted into your Content Editor. You can further develop it using the other AI tools in Content AI.

![Click Insert to insert the Pros & Cons](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201908%201244'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Use Cases for the Product Pros & Cons AI Tool

When writing product reviews or comparison articles, highlighting a product’s strengths and weaknesses helps build trust and transparency with your audience. The Product Pros & Cons AI tool simplifies this by generating clear, balanced, informative, unbiased, and SEO-friendly lists. Here’s how you can use it:

### 3.1  Handling Customer Questions & Concerns for Online Stores

Sometimes, customers hesitate before making a purchase because they don’t have enough information. A well-placed pros & cons section on your FAQ or product page can address their concerns before they even ask. You can use this AI tool to create one.

![Handling Customer Questions with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20917'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Making E-Commerce Product Listings More Informative

Including a Pros & Cons section on product pages improves SEO by targeting long-tail keywords and user queries like ‘Is  worth it?’ this AI helps create SEO-friendly content that enhances both search visibility and user engagement.

![Making E-Commerce Product Listings More Informative with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Writing Affiliate Product Reviews That Actually Convert

Let’s be real: Nobody likes those product reviews that only talk about the good stuff. People trust realistic reviews. So, if you’re in affiliate marketing, this AI tool can help you write authentic, well-structured pros & cons that drive clicks and conversions.

![Writing Affiliate Product Reviews with Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20918'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
