# How to Use Rank Math on a Staging Site

			
			

	

When it comes to optimizing your website for search engines, [using a staging site](https://rankmath.com/blog/how-to-create-staging-site/) can be a game-changer. It allows you to test changes, experiment with new ideas, and ensure everything is perfect before going live.

By using Rank Math in your staging environment, you can test, experiment, and fine-tune your SEO to perfection before going live. And you know the best part? You can use the same Rank Math account and license on your staging and live sites.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to install Rank Math on a staging site and what to do after migrating to a live site.

## Table Of Contents

- [Installing Rank Math on a Staging Site](#installing-rank-math-on-a-staging-site)

- [After Migrating to a Live Site](#after-migrating-to-a-live-site)

## 1  Installing Rank Math on a Staging Site

The process of installing Rank Math on a staging site is the same as if you were installing it on a live site. To get started, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins → Add New** on your staging site.

Once done, enter**Rank Math** into the search bar and click **Install Now**.

![Click Install Now](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Install-Rank-Math-plugin.png)

Once the installation is complete, click **Activate**. You can now begin using Rank Math on your staging site.

![Activate Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20696'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** If you ever need to back up your Rank Math settings or migrate them separately, you can refer to [this dedicated tutorial](https://rankmath.com/kb/import-export-settings/).

## 2  After Migrating to a Live Site

After migrating to a live site, you’ll need to configure a few settings to ensure Rank Math can accurately monitor your website. If you’ve previously configured these settings, you may need to reconfigure them.

### 2.1  Reconnect Your Rank Math Account

![Disconnect Rank Math account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201894%20909'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once Rank Math is disconnected, click **Connect Now**, as shown below.

![Click Connect Now on Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201896%20905'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be redirected to the plugin activation page. If you have multiple licenses, click the dropdown and select the applicable license, as shown below. Once done, click **OK, ACTIVATE NOW**.

![Activate Rank Math PRO subscription](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20850%20610'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Set Your Site to Index

To ensure search engines can begin crawling your site, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard →** **Settings → Reading**and deselect **Search engine visibility**.****Then, click **Save Changes**.

![Deselect Search engine visibility](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201889%20948'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Re-Verify Your Site on Google

If you have previously verified your staging site on Google Search Console, you will need to re-verify it using the URL of the live site. If you haven’t previously verified your site, you should do so using the URL of the live site. You can refer to this guide on [verifying your site on Google](https://rankmath.com/kb/google-site-verification/).

### 2.4  Delete Your Old Analytics Data

After verifying your live site to Google Search Console, you’d need to delete the analytics data of your staging site and begin a new fetch for the live site. You can refer to this guide on [deleting old analytics data in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/delete-old-analytics-data/).

In conclusion, using Rank Math on a staging site is a valuable strategy for optimizing your website before going live. Using the steps outlined in this article, you will seamlessly integrate Rank Math into your staging environment and ensure your SEO strategy is tested and perfected before launching your website.
