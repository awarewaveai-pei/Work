# How to Generate an Affiliate URL

			
			

	

Now that [Rank Math’s Affiliate Program](https://rankmath.com/affiliates/) is available and open for applications, it’s time to get started as an affiliate & start generating commissions for all of the sales you help Rank Math make…

If you’ve joined our affiliate program and your application has been approved — _Congratulations_! Without further ado, we’ll walk you through this tutorial to generate your unique affiliate URL for any page on Rank Math so that you can start promoting it.

## 1  Navigate to Rank Math Affiliate Dashboard

To get started, you need to log in to your Rank Math account and navigate to the [Affiliate Area](https://rankmath.com/affiliate-area/). Be sure to use the same email address that you used when applying to our affiliate program.

![Rank Math - Affiliate Area](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Rank-Math-Affiliate-Area.png)

Your Affiliate Dashboard will appear on your screen — it will look like this.

![Rank Math Affiliate Dashboard](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Rank-Math-Affiliate-Dashboard-1.png)

## 2  Enter the URL for Promotion

The **Referral URL Generator** section under the **Affiliate URLs** tab will help you create your unique Affiliate URL. Enter the page on Rank Math you wanted to promote in the **Page URL** field. By default, this field has the URL of our homepage (rankmath.com).

For this tutorial, we’re creating an affiliate URL for our [Rank Math features page](https://rankmath.com/wordpress/plugin/seo-suite/), another high-converting landing page that helps users with all the awesome features packed with Rank Math.

![Rank Math page URL for affiliate promotion](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20994%20105'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Enter A Campaign Name

Next, in the **Campaign Name** field, enter a name for your promotion campaign, say, for our [affiliate contest](https://rankmath.com/kb/affiliate-contest/). (Optional)

![Campaign Name](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20982%20102'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**PRO TIP:** Try to use a unique and distinguishable **Campaign Name** for each promotion channel. It is a good practice to do so as you’ll be able to identify which promotion channel is driving more affiliate sales for you, and you should be able to focus more on it to boost your affiliate sales.

## 4  Generate Your Affiliate URL

Now, you can click the **Generate URL** button to create your unique affiliate URL (referral URL).

![Generate Rank Math affiliate URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20991%20370'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can notice your Referral URL being generated with your Affiliate ID and Campaign Name included.

![Copy your Referral URL from Rank Math Referral URL Generator](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20989%20493'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And, that’s it! You can now copy and share your referral URL anywhere & start promoting Rank Math — _Good luck!_ If you’ve any questions about our affiliate program, feel free to email us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f0838580809f8284b082919e9b9d918498de939f9d), we’re always more than happy to help…
