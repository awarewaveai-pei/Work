## 1  Focus Keyword

The focus keyword is simply the search term that your post or page is optimized for in the search engines. If your post has been written so that it is optimized for that particular keyword, the audience will be more likely to locate it and find what they’re looking for.

**Rank Math** allows you to select up to 5 **[focus](https://rankmath.com/kb/score-100-in-tests/)****[keywords](https://rankmath.com/kb/score-100-in-tests/)** per post. This means you can optimize your post for 5 different **keywords** and attract a lot more traffic as compared to what you were optimizing for 1 focus **keyword**. This option is available in both the free and the PRO version of Rank Math. If you try to add more **focus keywords**, **Rank Math** would rather discard them. But, this limit is removed in Rank Math PRO allowing you to add unlimited focus keywords.

![Focus Keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201812%20350'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Keyword Autosuggest

Rank Math can also help you discover more keywords by pulling in automatic keyword suggestions from Google. As you start typing letters in the Focus Keyword field, Rank Math will get you keyword suggestions from Google. From the list of keyword suggestions, click the keyword to add it to the Focus Keyword field. You can repeat this for adding more focus keywords and optimizing your content accordingly.

![Rank Math automatic keyword suggestion](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20296%20482'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:** To enable Rank Math to provide you automatic keyword suggestions, you’ll need to first [connect your website with your Rank Math account](https://rankmath.com/kb/free-account-benefits/). If you haven’t already connected your website with Rank Math account, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Help** and then click the **Connect Now** button.

## 4  Keyword Suggestion by Content AI

As more businesses use AI to streamline content creation, it’s worth noting that [**47% of users are producing content faster**](https://rankmath.com/future-of-search/)**, and 35% are shifting their focus from quantity to quality.**

Rank Math’s Content AI suggests the potential or important keywords that you could add to your content. The data will be fetched on the basis of your Focus Keyword. You can utilize this opportunity for ranking particular keywords that you’ve not used yet.

**Note:** You must enable the[**Content AI**](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/#enable-content-ai) module from Rank Math’s dashboard, in order to utilize its features.

![Keywords tab in Content AI panel](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20616%201327'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 5  Generate Keyword Ideas Using RankBot

RankBot is one of Content AI’s tools that uses artificial intelligence to organize and suggest valuable keyword ideas based on your prompt.

To use RankBot, first make sure you’ve enabled the Content AI module. Then go to **Rank Math SEO → Content AI → Chat**. Here, you can quickly use the **Prompt Library** to access prebuilt prompts that help generate keyword ideas.

However, if you’re using the free version, you won’t have access to the Prompt Library. You’ll either need to [upgrade your Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) or manually enter your own prompt.

![RankBots Prompt Library button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20810'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the Prompt Library, search for “keyword” to filter all the keyword research related prompts. For example, if you’re looking for long-tail keywords, simply select the **Long-tail Keywords** prompt and click **Use Prompt** to insert it into the RankBot interface.

![Long tail keywords prompt list](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20906'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once the prompt appears, replace the `[Post Title]` placeholder with your actual post title, then click the **Enter** icon to generate the keywords.

RankBot will then provide a list of related long-tail keywords tailored to your topic.

![Long tail keyword ideas from RankBot](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%201375'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This is how you can make use of the Rank Math plugin to nail down a keyword, but you need to keep in mind that Rank Math is not a replacement for a dedicated keyword research tool. It simply makes your job easy with the help of its various features.
