# How to Use Google Keyword Planner Without Running Ads

			
			

	

Google Keyword Planner (GKP) is a free tool inside Google Ads, originally designed for pay-per-click (PPC) advertisers — but it’s also one of the most powerful tools for SEO and content planning. And here’s the good news: you don’t need to spend a dime to access it.

Many bloggers, marketers, and SEO professionals use Google Keyword Planner without ever creating a campaign or running an ad.

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll show you exactly how to set up your Google Ads account, skip the ad setup, and unlock Keyword Planner — step by step, without spending a cent.

## 1  Create Your Google Ads Account

To access Keyword Planner, you first need a Google Ads account. Go to [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com/) and click the **Start now** button, as shown below.

![Google Ads Start now button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20733'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’ll be taken to a page prompting you to create your first campaign. Click **Create your first campaign** to dismiss the pop-up.

![Create your first campaign](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20770'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

On the next screen, you’ll be asked to enter your business name and what happens when someone clicks your ad. Don’t worry — you’re not actually launching an ad; this is just part of the initial setup. Provide them and click the **Next**button.

![Add information](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201092%20998'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, you’ll be asked to select your campaign goal. Don’t choose any goal. Instead, scroll down and click the link that says **Set up an account only** at the bottom.

![Bypass the campaign creation process](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%201277'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This allows you to bypass the campaign creation process and move directly to your account settings.

Now, confirm your billing country, time zone, and currency. _Make sure these details are accurate_ — you won’t be able to change them later, and they may affect your accounts if you decide to run ads in the future. Once everything looks good, click **Continue**.

![Provide your billing information](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201201%20859'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Add Your Payment Method

At this stage, Google will require you to add a payment method to finish setting up your account — even if you don’t plan to advertise.

Enter your debit or credit card details when prompted. Then go to the payment settings and **switch from automatic payments to manual payments**. This is important because leaving it on automatic may trigger an initial $10 charge, depending on your region.

![Enter payment details](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20794%201399'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You might hear workarounds online about deleting accounts or using multiple emails to skip adding your payment details. Be careful, as this could put your account at risk.

Once you’ve added your payment info and selected manual payment, click **Submit** to complete the setup.

## 3  Access Google Keyword Planner

After your account is successfully created, click **Continue**.

![Your Google Ads account is successfully created](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20988%20869'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You may now be asked to complete your business profile setup. Since we’re not running ads, just click the **Cancel (X)** icon at the top left corner to skip it.

![Exit organization info page](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%201168'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you’re shown a verification page, simply click the **Google Ads logo** to go back to your main dashboard. You can also click the **+ icon** to start a campaign, then hit your browser’s **Back** button. This will land you on the **Campaigns** page.

![Skip the verifications page](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20604'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now you’re ready to access Keyword Planner. Click the wrench icon labeled **Tools**in the side menu. Under the Planning section, select **Keyword Planner**.

![Go to keyword Planner](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201360%20946'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’ll now see two tools inside Keyword Planner:

- **Discover New Keywords** – to get keyword ideas based on topics or websites.

- **Get Search Volume and Forecasts** – to check traffic and keyword trends.

![Accessing Google keyword planner](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20888'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

That’s it — you’ve successfully accessed Google Keyword Planner without launching a campaign.
