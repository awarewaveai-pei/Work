# How to NoIndex Paginated Pages

			
			

	

Rank Math adds `rel="next"` and `rel="prev”` tags for your paginated series as per Google’s guidelines that can help the search engines to understand the relationship between the individual URLs in a paginated series.

However, if you wish to prevent these paginated pages like /page/2 and others from appearing in SERPs, you can easily set the Noindex Paginated Pages with the help of Rank Math. Let us now look at exactly how you can do this…

![How to NoIndex Paginated Pages in WordPress? Rank Math SEO](https://img.youtube.com/vi/HdU0K1WrUXg/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

**1.**In order to do so you have to go to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Titles & Meta → Misc. Pages**.

![Rank Math > Titles and Meta > Misc Pages](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/misc-pages.jpg)

**2.** In the Misc. Pages you will find the **Noindex Paginated Single Pages** option.

![NoIndex-Paginated-Single-Pages](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20980%20200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can enable this option to set **noindex** on paginated pages in order to prevent /page/2 and any other archives to show up in the search results.

![Noindex in paginated pages](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201207%20539'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This is how [Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/wordpress/plugin/seo-suite/) makes it easy to NoIndex Paginated Pages, similarly, you can [noindex paginated archive subpages](https://rankmath.com/kb/titles-and-meta/#noindex-archive-subpages) and prevent them from showing up on search results. And if you still have any questions, feel free to [reach out to our support team](https://rankmath.com/support/) who are always more than happy to help.
