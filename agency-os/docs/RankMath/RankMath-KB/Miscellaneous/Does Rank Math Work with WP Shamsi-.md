# Does Rank Math Work with WP Shamsi?

			
			

	

In this knowledgebase article, we’re going to discuss whether Rank Math works with WP Shamsi or not.

## What is WP Shamsi?

[WP Shamsi](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-shamsi/) is a WordPress plugin that makes it possible to convert the default WordPress date format to Shamsi, aka Jalali or Iranian calendar and improves local UI to Farsi users.

## Does Rank Math Work with WP Shamsi?

**Yes,**Rank Math is fully compatible with the WP Shamsi plugin.

### 1  Post/Page Date

You can modify the date on the posts/pages with the help of the WP Shamsi plugin.

![date in WP Shamsi](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/date.jpg)

Rank Math will further display the date on your post/page in the same format.

![Published postdate in Jalali](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/published-date.jpg)

### 2  Quick Edit

Shamsi (Jalali) date in post quick edit works completely fine with Rank Math plugin. You can set the date in **Quick Edit**and then click on **Update**. The Jalali date will be updated on your post.

![Quick Edit](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/quick-edit.jpg)

### 3  Archive Widget

The functionality of Rank Math isn’t hampered even when the archive widget is displayed with the help of the WP Shamsi plugin.

![Rank Math working fine with Archive Widget-WP Shamsi](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20333'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 4  Comments

Rank Math also displays the Jalali date in the comments sections of your posts/page.

![Comments date displayed in Jalali](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20532'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! If you still have any questions about WP Shamsi’s compatibility with Rank Math – feel free to [reach out to our support team](https://rankmath.com/support/).
