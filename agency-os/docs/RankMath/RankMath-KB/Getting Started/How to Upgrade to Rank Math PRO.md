# How to Upgrade to Rank Math PRO

			
			

	

In this article, we will show you how to upgrade to Rank Math PRO. We have a full video of the process here as well:

![Rank Math PRO Installation & Activation](https://img.youtube.com/vi/nCDe67K_3rA/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

1  First, you need to[purchase a license](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) for Rank Math PRO.

2  Then, you need to update the Rank Math Free version to the latest version from your **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins → Installed Plugins**.

![update-the-free-version-of-rank-math](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/update-free-version-rm.jpg)

3  Then download Rank Math PRO from your [account area](https://rankmath.com/my-account/).

![rank math account area](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201702%20996'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

4  Once you have downloaded the Rank Math PRO version, you need to install it from the **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins → Add New**. Then click on **Upload Plugin**.

![Upload plugin on Add New plugin](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201446%20893'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Select the zip file that you downloaded by clicking on **Choose file** button. Then click on**Install Now.**

![Choose file and install now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201115%20448'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

5  Once the plugin is installed, you need to enable the Rank Math SEO PRO plugin. Once enabled, you can check the plugin in the Installed Plugins, as both the [Free and PRO versions of Rank Math have to be installed and activated](https://rankmath.com/kb/should-i-delete-free-plugin-after-upgrading/) on the site.

![Rank Math Free and PRO version installed](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

6  Activate the license from **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Help**.

**Note:** In case, your Free version is already connected to Rank Math, you need to [disconnect and reconnect with your Rank Math account](https://rankmath.com/kb/website-not-shown-in-account/#how-to-disconnect-your-rank-math-account) for the PRO license and updates to become available.

To disconnect Rank Math, click the **Disconnect Account** button.

![Disconnect account in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201260%20457'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Then to connect with your Rank Math account, click the **Connect Now** button.

![Connect Rank Math account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202184%20934'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once you click **Connect Now,**you’ll be redirected to rankmath.com, where you’ll be able to connect your account to activate your license. You must **use the same Rank Math account that you used when you subscribed to a Rank Math PRO plan**.

In the Plugin Activation dialog box, you’ll also be able to choose between PRO, Business, and Agency licenses, as shown below, when your Rank Math account includes multiple subscriptions.

![Activate Rank Math license](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20864%20612'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After clicking **OK, ACTIVATE NOW** Rank Math PRO will be activated. You can confirm that the activation has been successful by navigating back to the **Help**tab of the Rank Math Dashboard in your site’s WordPress admin area:

![Rank Math SEO PRO activated](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202140%20770'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! If you still have any questions about upgrading to Rank Math PRO, please feel free to [reach our support team directly from here](https://rankmath.com/support/), and we’re always here to help.
