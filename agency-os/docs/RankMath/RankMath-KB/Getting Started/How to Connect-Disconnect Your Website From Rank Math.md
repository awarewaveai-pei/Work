# How to Connect/Disconnect Your Website From Rank Math

			
			

	

At some point, you may be required to connect or disconnect your site from Rank Math. This may be part of the installation process if you’re a new user or may be necessary to enable a setting or troubleshoot an issue if you’re an existing user. Either way, the steps are pretty much straightforward.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to connect or disconnect your website from Rank Math.

## In this KB, we’ll cover:

- [How to Connect a Site With Rank Math](#connect-a-site)

- [How to Disconnect a Site From Rank Math](#disconnect-a-site)

## 1  How to Connect a Site With Rank Math

To connect a site, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Help** and click **Connect Now**.

![Click Connect Now](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Click-Connect-Now.png)

Next, review the listed email address to confirm you’re about to log into the appropriate Rank Math account. If your account is linked to a different email address, click **Login here**.

![Click Login Here](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Click-Login-Here.png)

If you have multiple licenses, select the applicable license from the **License**dropdown menu. Once done, click **OK, ACTIVATE NOW**.

![Click Ok Activate Now](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20637'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  How to Disconnect a Site From Rank Math

To disconnect a site, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Help** and click **Disconnect Account**, as shown below.

![Click Disconnect Account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

That’s how you can connect and disconnect a site from Rank Math. We hope you are able to follow the above steps. If you have any questions, you’re more than welcome to [contact our dedicated support team](https://rankmath.com/support/). We’re available 24/7, 365 days a year…
