# What is the Ideal Meta Title Tag Length?

			
			

	

The title tag is one of the most important pieces of a web page. The main goal of a meta title tag is to convince visitors to click your page on the search results.

In this knowledgebase article, we will discuss the ideal meta title tag length that you can follow on your posts and pages.

## What is a Meta Title Tag?

Title Tag is the HTML element that specifies the title of a web page. They are displayed on the SERPs as clickable headlines. Title tags play an important role in usability, social sharing and also enhance the SEO of your website. The best practice is to keep the title tag accurate and write a concise description of your content that reflects the page’s purpose.

Here is what a title tag looks like:

`<title> How to Setup Rank Math SEO Plugin Properly </title>`

And here is what it looks like in the SERPs:

## Why Are Title Tags Important?

Title tags provide visitors with quick insight into your content. This is the primary piece of information that helps to determine which results to click on the SERPs. Hence, the title tag is considered [one of the most important elements for SEO](https://rankmath.com/blog/best-seo-practices/#write-title-tags-meta-descriptions). Ensure your website has a unique and descriptive title for your content that signals relevance to both users and search engines.

Here are a couple of reasons why title tags are important:

- **Creates the First Impression:** The title tag is the first thing that a visitor will notice while performing a search. So this is your chance to make the first positive impression that sparks interest.

- **Leverage the Brand:** Make sure you display your brand name in your title tag, as people trust brands. This entices the click and increases the website traffic over time.

## What is the Ideal Length for Meta Title?

The ideal length for the meta title is 50–60 characters. Google typically starts to cut off the title tag after 50-60 characters, though it may adjust based on pixel width (around 580 pixels). Hence, it is a good practice to keep your title tag around this length for full visibility.

## How to Write a Good Meta Title Tag

Here are a few tips for creating titles of the web pages:

- Keep your title brief and descriptive that is relevant to the content of your pages.

- Avoid [keyword stuffing](https://rankmath.com/seo-glossary/keyword-stuffing/), i.e., repeating the same words or phrases multiple times.

- Avoid repeated titles.

- Create unique titles for each page of your website.

Title tags are essential from a search engine optimization, user experience, and social media point of view. They aren’t as complicated as they seem so; they take some time and effort to get right. We recommend putting in efforts to continually improve your title tags as they can get you enough traffic to your website.
