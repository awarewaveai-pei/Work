# Benefits of Creating a FREE Rank Math Account

			
			

	

Creating and connecting your website with your RankMath.com account is completely optional.

But, there are a few **features and benefits** you might be missing out on if you are not connecting your website with your free Rank Math account.

![Benefits of Creating a FREE Rank Math Account - Rank Math SEO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201280%20720'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
__

## 1  Get Google Keyword Suggestions

Rank Math’s focus keyword field can be extended into a keyword research tool very easily.

Once you connect your website with your account, you will start seeing **keyword suggestions from Google** right when you start typing your focus keyword.

**Keyword suggestions from Google** can help give you an idea of what keywords people are using with similar words to the one you typed. You can use this information to target your content for more keywords and increase your reach.

![keyword-suggestion-rank-math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201044%20860'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Free Access to Content AI

Rank Math’s Content AI plan lets you access the AI-powered features and tools available in Rank Math. These include content generation, improvement, and internal linking AI tools. You can read more about our Content AI in its [dedicated knowledgebase article](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/).

By creating a free Rank Math account, you will be given some free feature uses that let you to take our Content AI for a spin. If you run out of uses, you can easily subscribe to a [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that aligns with your requirements.

![Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201264'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  In-Depth SEO Analysis

Some of the **SEO Analyzer tests** utilize our proprietary API to run and work properly. To run these API-based tests, you have to connect your website with your free RankMath.com account.

![SEO Analyzer by Rank Math inside WordPress](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201438%20634'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Install Analytics Code

Once you’ve created your free Rank Math account, you’ll be able to connect Rank Math SEO plugin with your Google Account, which is essential for accessing the [advanced Analytics module](https://rankmath.com/kb/analytics/) inside Rank Math.

Further, Rank Math can [install the Analytics code](https://rankmath.com/kb/install-google-analytics/) on your website by itself so that you don’t have to rely on any additional plugin for installing the code.

![install analytics code](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201478%201200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**This is just the beginning. We will be adding many other features to the list that you wouldn’t want to miss out on.**

So, you’ll need to [create a free rankmath.com](https://rankmath.com/#signup) account and connect it to your website by navigating to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Help** and clicking the **Connect Now** button.

![Connect Rank Math Account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202286%201066'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! If you’re facing any issues creating or connecting your Rank Math account, feel free to [get in touch with our support team](https://rankmath.com/contact/), as we’re always here to help.
