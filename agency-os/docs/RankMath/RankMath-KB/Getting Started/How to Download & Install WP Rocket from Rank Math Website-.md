# How to Download & Install WP Rocket from Rank Math Website?

			
			

	

When you choose our special bundle, you can purchase ****[Rank Math and WP Rocket together](https://rankmath.com/pricing/?include_bundles=1)** at a discounted price**.

![Rank Math + WP Rocket bundle](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Rank-Math-WP-Rocket-scaled.png)

This way, you get the best of both worlds: [Rank Math to optimize your website](https://rankmath.com/kb/score-100-in-tests/) for search engines and WP Rocket to boost your site speed and performance.

But after making the purchase, how do you download and install the WP Rocket plugin on your WordPress site?

This knowledgebase article will walk you through the exact steps.

## 1  Downloading the WP Rocket Plugin

There are two simple ways to download the WP Rocket plugin after purchasing it with Rank Math.

### 1.1  Download WP Rocket from the Thank You Page

After successfully purchasing the Rank Math + WP Rocket plan, you can easily download the WP Rocket plugin directly from the thank you page.

### 1.2  Download WP Rocket from Your Account Page

If you didn’t download it from the thank you page or prefer to do it later, you can download it anytime from your Rank Math account.

To do this, log in to your Rank Math account by clicking **[My Account](https://rankmath.com/my-account/)** at the top of the page. From your account dashboard, you’ll see the option to download both Rank Math PRO and WP Rocket.

Click on the **DOWNLOAD**button next to WP Rocket to save it to your computer.

![Download WP Rocket from Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20859%20505'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Installing the WP Rocket Plugin

Once downloaded, log in to your WordPress admin.

In the WordPress admin menu, go to **Plugins → Add Plugin**. On that page, click the**Upload Plugin** button, as shown below.

![Upload Plugin in WP](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201070%20765'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click **Choose File** to upload the WP Rocket plugin you downloaded, then click **Install Now**.

![Install WP Rocket](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20420'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Wait a few moments, then click the **Activate Plugin** button once the installation is complete.

![Activate Plugin](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201062%20459'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After activation, go to **Settings → WP Rocket** from your WordPress admin menu. On your WP Rocket dashboard, you’ll see a successful activation notice.

Below it, you’ll also find your license details and expiration date, as shown below.

![WP Rocket activated on your site](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20816'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

And that’s it! WP Rocket is now installed and activated on your website.

If you need help setting up WP Rocket on your site, you can check out their official [documentation here](https://docs.wp-rocket.me/article/59-getting-started).
