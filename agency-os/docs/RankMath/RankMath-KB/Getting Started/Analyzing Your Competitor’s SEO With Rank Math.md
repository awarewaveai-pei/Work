# Analyzing Your Competitor’s SEO With Rank Math

			
			

	

Are you looking to gain an edge in the ever-competitive world of SEO? One effective strategy is to closely monitor your competitors and analyze their SEO tactics.

Knowing what works for your competitor is a low-hanging fruit because your competitor has already tried and tested things. This means you skip the trial and error and get more remarkable results with less work.

In this knowledgebase article, we will show you how to gain valuable insights into your competitor’s SEO strategies using Rank Math.

## In this guide, we’ll cover:

- [Using the SEO Analyzer](#using-the-seo-analyzer)

- [Research Using Content AI](#using-content-ai)

- [Generating a Competing Article Using Content AI](#generating-a-competing-article-using-content-ai)

## 1  Using the SEO Analyzer

The SEO Analyzer allows you to inspect your competitor’s webpages and gain valuable insights into their SEO strategy.

Rank Math PRO users can access the SEO Analyzer from their WordPress dashboard. If you don’t have PRO, you can use our free [SEO Analyzer tool](https://rankmath.com/tools/seo-analyzer/). Enter your competitor’s URL into the available field and click **ANALYZE**.

![Enter your competitor’s URL into the available field ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you’re using Rank Math PRO, head to **WordPress Dashboard**→**Rank Math SEO**→**SEO Analyzer**→**Competitor Analyzer**. Once done, enter your competitor’s URL into the field and click **Start Audit**, as shown below.

![Enter your competitor’s URL into the available field and click Start Audit](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**Note:**If the SEO Analyzer is not displayed, head to **WordPress Dashboard**→**Rank Math SEO** and enable the **SEO Analyzer** module.

The SEO Analyzer will run a series of tests on your competitor’s URL and return with the results. Below, we have listed the results you want to pay attention to.

### 1.1  Common Keywords

This section lists the most common keywords detected on your competitor’s page. These are the same keywords search engines use to understand the intent of your competitor’s website. You may want to optimize your page for these keywords.

![Common keywords](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.2  SEO Description

This section lists the SEO meta description your competitor included in their content. You can use a similar SEO description for your competing page. An [ideal SEO description](https://rankmath.com/kb/ideal-meta-description-length/) should be between 50 and 160 characters.

![SEO Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.3  H1 Heading

This section contains the H1 heading of your competitor’s content. You’ll have to create a better H1 for your page.

![H1 Heading](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.4  H2 Headings

This section displays the H2 headings in your competitor’s content & gives an outline of your competitor’s article. You can get an idea of what your competitor is covering and try to cover in your article as well.

![H2 Headings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.5  Keywords in Title & Description

This field compares the keywords on your competitor’s page with those on their webpage’s SEO title and description. Keywords that appear on the page and its SEO title and description will be displayed here.

![Keywords in Title and Description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20607'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.6  Links Ratio

The Links Ratio field compares your competitor’s internal and external links. You can review the number of internal and external links to guide you on the number of links to include in your content.

![Links Ratio](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20607'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.7  SEO Title

This section lists the SEO title of your competitor’s content. You can use a similar SEO title in your content. An ideal SEO title should be between 50 and 60 characters.

![SEO Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now that you’ve gathered insights into your competitor’s SEO, it’s time to replicate their results [using Content AI](#generating-a-competing-article-using-content-ai).

## 2  Research Using Content AI

If you wish to skip the previous process of manually collecting insights on your competitors, you can use our Content AI research feature.

**Note:** To use our [Content AI](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-use-content-ai/) features, you’ll need an active [Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/).

If you already have a Content AI membership, head to **WordPress Dashboard**→ **Rank Math SEO**and enable the **Content AI**module, as shown below.

![Enable the Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20609'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, head to the post or page you’re creating to compete with your competitor’s content. If you have not begun creating the content, head to **WordPress Dashboard**→**Posts**or**Pages**→**Add New** to begin drafting one.

![Head to Add New](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20610'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, click the **Content AI**icon at the top-right corner of the page.

![Click the Content AI icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20613'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, enter your keyword into the FOCUS KEYWORD field. If your target audience is from a specific country, select the country from the dropdown. Once done, click **Research**, as shown below.

![Enter your keyword into the Focus Keyword field ](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will analyze the top content ranking for your desired keyword and return with suggestions on the headings, media, links, questions, and keywords to include in your content.

## 3  Generating a Competing Article Using Content AI

After gaining insights into the top-ranking content, the next step is to generate a competing article using Content AI.

To get started, scroll to the bottom of the analysis results. You will be presented with some tabs. Now, we will show you how to convert the data in the Keywords and Questions tabs into high-quality content using Content AI.

![Keywords and Questions tabs](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20617'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 

- [Step 1: Generate an Outline](#generate-an-outline)

- [Step 2: Generate an Article](#generate-an-article)

- [Step 3: Generate an FAQ Block](#generate-an-faq-block)

- [Step 4: Generate an SEO Meta](#generate-an-seo-meta)

### 3.1  Step 1: Generate an Outline

Here, scroll down to the **Keywords**tab. Once done, click the **USE KEYWORD IN** dropdown and select **Headings**, as shown below.

![Click the USE KEYWORD IN dropdown and select Headings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20611'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with some keywords to include in your headings. Hover over the keywords to view their ad competition, cost per click (CPC), and search volume. Once you decide on the keyword to use in your H1 heading, click on it to copy it.

![Select the Blog Post Outline AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20617'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After copying the keyword, scroll up and click the **AI Tools** icon, as shown below.

![Click the AI Tools icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, select the **Blog Post Outline** AI tool.

![Select the Blog Post Outline AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, paste the keyword into the Topic field. Once done, fill in the other fields and click **Generate**.

You can refer to this guide on [using the Blog Post Outline AI tool](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-ai-blog-post-outline-tool/).

![Paste the keyword into the Topic field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with an outline for your content. Hover over the outline and click **Insert**.

![Hover over the outline and click Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The outline will be inserted into your editor, as shown below.

![The outline will be inserted into your editor](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Step 2: Generate an Article

Now, it’s time to turn your outline into paragraphs. To get started, click on a heading and select the **Content AI** icon. Once done, click **Run as Command**.

![Click Run as Command](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will generate paragraphs for the heading. To add the content to your article, click **Use**. You can repeat this step for as many headings as you desire.

![To add the content to your article, click Use](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, return to **Content AI** Metabox → **Keywords**and select **Content**from the KEYWORDS IN USE dropdown. You will now proceed to include those keywords in your content.

![Select Content in Keywords in Use dropdown](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20611'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  Step 3: Generate an FAQ Block

After creating your content, it’s time to create a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) block. To do that, click the **Questions**tab and then the **Copy**icon, as shown below.

![Click the Questions tab](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, head to the editor screen and click **Ctrl+V**on your keyboard. A FAQ block containing the questions will be added to your content, as shown below.

![A FAQ block containing the questions will be added to your content](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20609'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, it’s time to get Content AI to generate answers to those questions. To do that, head to a question and click the **Content AI**icon, as shown below.

![Head to a question and click the Content AI icon](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20614'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Content AI will generate an answer to the question. You should repeat this step for the other questions.

### 3.4  Step 4: Generate an SEO Title and Description

To generate an SEO title and description, return to **Content AI** Metabox → **Keywords**. Once done, click the **Use Keyword In** dropdown and select **SEO Title**.

![Click the Keywords in Use dropdown and select SEO Title](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20618'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with the keywords to include in your SEO title. Click on a keyword to copy it. Once done, scroll up and click the **AI Tools**icon. Then, select the **SEO Meta** AI tool, as shown below.

![Select the SEO Meta AI tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20615'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, paste the keyword you just copied into the **Focus Keyword**field. Next, fill in the other fields and click **Generate**.

**Note:**You can also copy the keyword in the SEO Description and paste it into the Focus Keyword field.

![Paste the keyword into the Focus Keyword field](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, hover over the SEO meta title and description you just generated and click **Insert**.

![Hover over the SEO meta and click Insert](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The SEO meta title and description will be added to your content. To view them, click the **Rank Math SEO** Metabox → **Edit Snippet**, as shown below.

![Click Rank Math SEO icon and Edit Snippet](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20619'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will be presented with your SEO title and description, as shown below.

![You will be presented with your SEO title and description](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20655'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can now proceed to edit the article to your satisfaction. Once done, click **Publish**or **Update**, as shown below.

![Click Publish or Update](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201366%20617'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In conclusion, Content AI allows you to dive deep into your competitor’s SEO tactics. By analyzing their keywords, backlinks, and content format, you can skip the errors and uncover valuable insights to enhance your SEO strategies.
