# What is a Focus Keyword?

			
			

	

The focus keyword is simply the search term you want your post or page to be ranked for in the search engines. So when the audience looks out for that specific phrase or keyword in the search engines, they should probably locate you.

In this knowledgebase article, we’re going to walk you through what a focus keyword is and show you how Rank Math helps you choose the right focus keyword.

## 1  What is a Focus Keyword?

As mentioned, the **focus keyword** is a specific word or phrase that represents the main topic of a piece of content, such as a blog post or webpage. The primary goal of this focus keyword is to shape your content and optimize it to achieve higher rankings on search engines like [Google](https://rankmath.com/blog/google-indexing-api/) or [Bing](https://rankmath.com/blog/bing-indexing-api/).

Rank Math can help you ensure that the crafted content is optimized for your focus keyword. If your content isn’t optimized already, it gives you recommendations to optimize further for rankings.

**Note:** Technically, a keyword is a single word (e.g., digital), while a keyphrase is a group of words (e.g., digital marketing). However, the term “keyword” has become more common when referring to any search term that you want to rank for, and we’ve followed the same in all our articles.

## 2  Choosing a Focus Keyword

If you want to drive more organic traffic, you should choose your focus keyword based on proper keyword research. Since keyword research itself is a detailed topic on its own, you can refer to our [keyword research guide](https://rankmath.com/blog/keyword-research/).

Once you’ve found a set of keywords that you would like to target, find out the keywords’ search volume.

- If the keyword’s search volume is low, then targeting that particular keyword or keywords can be of no help. So it will rather not help.

- Choosing a generic term either isn’t particularly achievable for most websites.

- And to add up, we recommend not to choose highly competitive focus keywords.

## 3  How to Set a Focus Keyword in Rank Math?

[Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-setup/), your favorite SEO plugin, has a separate field to add focus keywords for your post/page. To set a [focus keyword in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/general-tab/), you need to edit a post/page and then go to the **General Tab** of the Rank Math SEO. Then, enter your focus keyword in the **Focus Keyword**field.

![set focus keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201036'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

By adding relevant keywords in the **Focus Keyword** section, you can make the most of Rank Math’s content analysis power. Rank Math by default lets you add up to **5 focus keywords**per post.

But all **[Rank Math PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)** users can add an unlimited number of keywords here. While if you’re using the free version, you can add additional keywords using this [developer hook/filter](https://rankmath.com/kb/filters-hooks-api-developer/#unlimited-keywords). With this said, please don’t over-use or stuff the focus keyword within your post/page.

## 4  Google Autocomplete for Focus Keyword Suggestions

If you’ve connected your Rank Math account, then you can use the Google Autocomplete feature to find more relevant focus keywords for your content. Just start typing a phrase in the Focus Keywords field to see a set of relevant keyword suggestions.

![Google Autocomplete for focus keyword suggestions](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20299%20418'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

These suggestions are only to back your keyword research. Ultimately, you should pick your keywords based on their search volume, keyword difficulty for ranking, and your website’s authority, so that you can be sure your content can rank for the picked keywords.

## 5  Choosing Your Primary Focus Keyword

As we mentioned, Rank Math lets you add more than one focus keyword. And when you add multiple focus keywords, the first focus keyword automatically becomes your primary focus keyword. The primary focus keyword is denoted by a star icon in front of it.

![Choosing primary focus keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20776%20688'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In general, your primary focus keyword should be the one that more people are looking for. Only then your content crafted around this keyword can rank for this search term to reach more people.

## 6  Checking the Search Intent of Your Focus Keyword [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

Search intent is crucial when writing content. It’s the reason behind a user’s search query — their goal when typing something into a search engine. Knowing this helps you create content that meets their needs and offers real value.

Within the Focus Keyword box, Rank Math can help identify the search intent of your keyword if you’re a PRO user. Just hover over the icon next to the focus keyword and click the **Search Intent** label. Rank Math will analyze and display the detected intent beside the keyword. You can hover over the icon again anytime to view it.

You can also reorder your focus keywords (details below) and choose one that you’re primarily targeting, as demonstrated below.

![Check search intent for focus keyword](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201196%201080'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 7  Reordering Focus Keywords [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

Your primary focus keyword should be considered the most important one among the bunch of keywords you’ve added. And Rank Math would perform additional on-page SEO tests for the primary focus keyword compared to the other focus keywords. Given that, it is practically possible to add unlimited focus keywords; at times, you might prefer changing the primary focus keyword or reordering your focus keywords.

Rank Math PRO users can simply drag and drop the focus keywords to reorder them, as shown below.

![Reorder focus keywords in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20800%201189'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

However, in the Free version, If you try to rearrange focus keywords, you will see a popup notification as shown below, since this feature is only available in the PRO version.

![Upgrade to re-order Focus Keywords](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20821%20291'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To change the primary focus keywords, Rank Math SEO free users will have to remove the added focus keywords and then add them once again.

## 8  Google Trends for Focus Keyword [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

When you start using Google Trends, you can understand how your keywords have fared against each other over a period of time. Rank Math lets you access Google Trends right inside your favorite editor in the WordPress dashboard. You can access it by clicking the Google Trends icon in the Focus keyword section.

![Access Google Trends in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20726%20528'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you’ve already added your focus keywords, then the Google Trends screen pulls out a graph with these keywords’ trends, as shown below.

![Google Trends for keywords](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20907%20574'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To add/remove keywords to compare, use the Keywords section on the left side of the screen. Ideally, the primary focus keyword should be the one more people are interested in. You can also check the Google Trends data for a specific region or for a longer duration. Once you’ve chosen your keywords, click the **Close & Use selected keywords** button to use the selected keywords as focus keywords. As expected, first keyword will be automatically considered as the primary focus keyword.

## 9  Optimizing Your Content for Focus Keyword

Rank Math gives you a proper analysis of your post’s content and helps you to optimize your content based on your focus keyword. You can notice the Basic SEO section below the **Focus Keyword** field in the General tab of Rank Math.

Rank Math analyzes your content to check if you’ve included your focus keyword in the SEO title, meta description, URL, and in your content. If you haven’t included it yet, then it displays errors as shown below:

![Focus Keyword Analysis by Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20594%20626'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

All these tests done by Rank Math to check if focus keywords are used inside your content are only recommendations. And these recommendations should be considered only as a framework to help you increase your chances of ranking.

## 10  Focus Keyword from Content AI Panel

The Content AI feature of Rank Math allows you to research focus keywords and to get started, you need to enable the Content AI module from Rank Math’s Dashboard.

Navigate to the left side of the WordPress Dashboard and click **Rank Math SEO → Dashboard**. Next, find and enable the **Content AI**module, as shown below:

![Enable Content AI module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201218%20694'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After activating the Content AI module, you can go to the **Settings** button directly or again navigate to **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → Content AI** for setting up site-wide configurations.

![Content AI settings in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202064%201320'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Here you can set the **Default Country**as “Worldwide” if you are targeting the global audience. It also gives you the option of selecting and [researching keywords](https://rankmath.com/kb/keyword-research-with-rank-math/) based on the target country.

![Default Country - Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20960%20427'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After selecting the **Default Country,**you can select the post types upon which you want to use Content AI, i.e. you can choose Posts/ Pages or both together.

Assuming that you’ve enabled and configured Content AI in the right way, let’s start researching for focus keywords. Go to Posts/ Pages that you want to optimize using Content AI and click the edit option. Now, open the Rank Math Metabox by clicking the **Rank Math SEO** icon in the top-right corner as shown below.

![Access Content AI from Rank Math Metabox](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20800%20524'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

From this panel, you can do research on the [focus keyword](https://rankmath.com/kb/what-is-a-focus-keyword/), with which you might want to optimize the web content. You can also enter a new keyword and do the research.

**Note:** You can upgrade to a [higher Content AI plan](https://rankmath.com/content-ai/) that suits your needs anytime if you require additional Content AI monthly feature uses.

![Content AI panel in Rank Math Metabox](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20612%20535'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After clicking the **Research** button, you will be able to find three tabs: Keywords, Questions, and Links. As you want to know more about Keywords, let’s see what all suggestions Content AI provides.

Content AI recommends a few important keywords based on the focus keyword. Here you are given four options, i.e. the **Content**, the **Headings**, **SEO Title**, or **SEO Description**.

![Use keyword in Content from Content AI](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20463%20486'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can select any one of the given options at a time. Based on the options that you’ve clicked, the suggestions for keywords might change. This feature helps you in avoiding stuffing the keywords which can affect user experience as well as search rankings. It only suggests the optimum amount of keywords that are required for your content.
