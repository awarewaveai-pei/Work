# Should I Delete the Free Version After Installing Rank Math PRO?

			
			

	

[Rank Math PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) is **built on top of the free version** to keep the plugins robust and lightweight. **You’ll need both of them to be installed and activated at the same time** so that the PRO version will work.

In this knowledgebase article, we’ll look at how Rank Math free and PRO versions are used all together on your website.

## Upgrading Rank Math

If you’ve recently purchased our [Rank Math PRO membership](https://rankmath.com/pricing/), you can download your Rank Math PRO plugin zip file from your [account area](https://rankmath.com/my-account/) to your computer.

![Download Rank Math SEO PRO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201702%20996'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Before your install the Rank Math PRO version, you’ll need to ensure that the Rank Math free version is installed on your website; only then you’ll be able to activate and use Rank Math PRO.

If you haven’t installed the Rank Math Free version yet, head over to the **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins → Add New** and search for Rank Math. Click the **Install Now**button, and once the plugin is installed, click on the **Activate** button to activate the plugin.

![Installing Rank Math from WordPress repository](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20692'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, to install the PRO version, navigate to **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins → Add New**. Click on **Upload Plugin**. And choose the Rank Math PRO zip file you’ve downloaded and click I**nstall Now**. Once the plugin is installed, click the **Activate now** link.

![Install Rank Math PRO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201271%20406'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To activate your Rank Math PRO license, head over to the **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Help** and click on **Connect Now** to activate your Rank Math plugin.

![Click Connect Now to activate Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202184%20934'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You’ll be prompted to connect your Rank Math account to activate your license. If your Rank Math account is connected with multiple subscriptions (PRO, Business, Agency), then you can choose the correct license from the drop-down as shown below. Then click **Ok, ACTIVATE NOW** button.

![Activate Rank Math License](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20864%20612'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now your Rank Math PRO license will be activated on your website.

![Rank Math SEO PRO activated](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202114%20888'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

After your install and activate your Rank Math PRO version, you can notice that the Rank Math PRO features are unlocked in your existing Rank Math Dashboard. You can enable these PRO modules and start using them on your website.

![Rank Math PRO modules](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201447%202560'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You can also read our tutorial on [how to set up Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-setup/#step-1-import-data) to configure the recommended settings inside Rank Math.

## Deactivating Rank Math

If you deactivate the free version, Rank Math PRO will also be deactivated, and you’ll have to activate both of them again. You can activate both versions from****the**Plugins → Installed Plugins** section.

![activate the plugin](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%20391'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## Updating Rank Math

Similarly, whenever a new update is available for Rank Math, you’ll have to update the free version first. Only after you update the free version of Rank Math you’ll be able to update the Rank Math PRO version.

![Update Rank Math Free before PRO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202008%201308'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## Deleting Rank Math Free Version

Deleting the free version is not a good idea once you’ve installed Rank Math PRO. You **need**both versions of the plugin in order for them to work.
