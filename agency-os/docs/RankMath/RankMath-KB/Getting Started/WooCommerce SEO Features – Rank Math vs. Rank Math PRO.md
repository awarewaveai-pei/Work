## WooCommerce SEO Features – Rank Math [PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

These are the general settings available in Rank Math PRO – also visible when navigating to **Rank Math SEO → General Settings → WooCommerce**:

We have got a [dedicated Knowledge base article](https://rankmath.com/kb/content-analysis-tests-for-product-pages/) to go through in detail.
