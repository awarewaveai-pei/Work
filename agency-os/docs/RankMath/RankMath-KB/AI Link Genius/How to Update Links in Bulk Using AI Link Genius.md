# How to Update Links in Bulk Using AI Link Genius

			
			

	

[AI Link Genius](https://rankmath.com/ai-link-genius/) allows you to find and replace multiple links on your site at once, all without opening the individual posts or pages.

Instead of visiting your posts and pages to change the URLs or anchor texts one after another, enter them into the bulk update tool, enter their replacements, and Link Genius will find those URLs or anchor texts (or both) and automatically replace them across your site.

We will now show you how to do that.

## How to Use the Bulk Update Tool:

- [Head to the Bulk Update Tool](#head-to-bulk-update-tool)

- [Filter Links Settings in the Bulk Update Tool](#filter-link-settings)

- [Configure Update Settings in the Bulk Update Tool](#configure-update-settings)

- [Preview Changes Before You Bulk Update](#preview-changes)

- [Review and Apply Changes](#review-and-apply-changes)

- [Rollback the Bulk Update](#rollback-bulk-update)

## 1  Head to the Bulk Update Tool

To get started, we have to make sure we have enabled the AI Link Genius module. To do that, click **Rank Math SEO** from your WordPress dashboard. Once done, enable the **Link Genius** module, as shown below.

**Note**: AI Link Genius is available exclusively in Rank Math PRO. If you haven’t installed Rank Math PRO yet, you can get a [subscription of your choice from here](https://rankmath.com/ai-link-genius/).

![Click Rank Math SEO and enable the AI Link Genius module](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20697'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

You will now head to the bulk update tool in Link Genius. To do that, head to **Rank Math SEO → Link Genius**, as shown below.

![Head to Link Genius in Rank Math SEO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20694'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Then click **Bulk update**. You will now be presented with the bulk update tool.

![Click on the Bulk update tab](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20699'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Filter Links Settings in the Bulk Update Tool

The filter links setting is the first step in the bulk update tool. It lets you narrow down on the links you will later update. For example, you can set it to only update links that appear on your posts, or to only update internal links.

**Note:** This setting is optional, and you can click **Skip** to ignore it if you want to update all URLs or anchor texts that match the conditions you will set in the upcoming settings.

![Overview of the Filter settings in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201256'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The available options in the filter links settings include:

- Link type

- Nofollow status

- Anchor type

- Post types

- Post publication date

### 2.1  Link Type

Identify the type of links to show. The available options include:

- **All**: Identify both internal and external links

- **Internal**: Identify internal links only (These links point to other pages on your site.)

- **External**: Identify external links only (These links point to other pages on another site.)

![The Link type dropdown in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.2  Nofollow Status

This option lets you filter links based on their follow status. The available choices include:

- **All**: Identify all links regardless of their follow status

- **Nofollow**: Only identify links with a nofollow tag

- **Dofollow**: Only identify links without a nofollow tag

![The Nofollow status dropdown in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.3  Anchor Type

This lets you filter links based on their anchor type. The available options include:

- **All**: Identify all relevant links, regardless of their anchor type

- **Text**: Only identify links with text anchors

- **Image**: Only identify links with image anchors

![The Anchor type dropdown in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.4  Post Types

Select the relevant post types you want to work with. The available options depend on the content on your site. Some possible options include:

- **Posts**: Identify links in posts only

- **Pages**: Identify links in pages only

- **Products**: Identify links in product pages only

You can select multiple options. If you will update all post types, leave this field blank.

![The Post types option in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 2.5  Post Publication Date

Select the timeline of the posts to evaluate. The available options include:

- **All time**: Identify all posts regardless of when they were published

- **Last 30 days**: Identify posts published in the past 30 days

- **Last 6 months**: Identify posts published in the past 6 months

- **Last year**: Identify posts published in the past year

- **Custom range**: Identify posts published within a specific timeframe

![The Post publication date dropdown in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, click **Next**, as shown below.

![Click Next to proceed](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20935'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 3  Configure Update Settings in the Bulk Update Tool

The next step is to configure the links to bulk update. Here are the available settings.

- What to update

- Anchor text settings

- URL settings

Let us explain them.

### 3.1  What to Update

This allows you to specify the matching conditions of the links to bulk update. There are three options here:

- **Both anchor text & URL**: Bulk edit links that match both your anchor text and link settings

- **Anchor text only**: Bulk edit links that match your anchor text settings only

- **URL only**: Bulk edit links that match your URL settings only

![What to update dropdown in the Bulk Update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Anchor Text Settings

The anchor text allows you to specify the attributes of the anchor texts to bulk update.

**Note**: This setting will not show if you selected “URL only” in the “What to update” dropdown.

The dropdowns, fields, and checkboxes available in the anchor text settings include:

- Search type

- Find this text

- Replace with

- Case sensitive

- Whole word only

![Overview of the Bulk update anchor text settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### a. Search Type

Select the type of anchor text to match. The available options include:

- **Exact match**: Only update anchor texts that match your exact words in the “Find this text” field

- **Contains**: Only update anchor texts that contain the words in the “Find this text” field

- **Regex**: Only update anchor texts that match the regex in the “Find this text” field

![Anchor text search type dropdown in the Bulk Update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### b. Find This Text

Enter the anchor text you want to bulk update here. Make sure they match the option you selected in the “Search type” field.

![Find this text field option in the Bulk Update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### c. Replace With

Enter the new text to replace the previous anchor text.

![Replace with text field option in the Bulk Update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### d. Case Sensitive

Enable this option if you want the anchor text to match the exact letter casing used in the “Find this text” field.

![Case sensitive option in Bulk update settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### e. Whole Word Only

Enable this option if you want the text in “Find this text” field to only match the complete word.

![Whole word only option in Bulk update settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  URL Settings

The URL settings allows you to specify the attributes of the URLs to bulk update. The dropdown and fields available here include:

**Note**: This setting will not show if you selected “Anchor text only” in the “What to update” dropdown.

- Search type

- Find this URL

- Replace with

![Overview of the Bulk update URL settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### a. Search Type

Select the type of URL to match. The available options include:

- **Exact URL match**: Only update URLs that match the exact URL in the “Find this URL” field

- **Partial match (Contains)**: Only update URLs that contain the text entered in the “Find this URL” field

- **Domain match**: Update all URLs that match the domain in the “Find this URL” field

![URL settings Search type option in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### b. Find This URL

Enter the link you want to replace. This can be the full URL, parts of it, or the domain, depending on the option you selected in the “Search type” dropdown.

![Find this URL field in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

#### c. Replace With

Enter the link that will replace the previous link.

![Replace with field in the Bulk update tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once done, click **Next**,

![Click Next after entering your URL settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201133'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Preview Changes Before You Bulk Update

Generate preview shows you a summary of the links you are about to bulk update. The details shown here may vary depending on your settings. It can include:

- **Update type**: Indicates whether you are updating the anchor text, URLs, or both

- **Anchor text**: Shows the current anchor text and its replacement

- **URL**: Shows the current URL and its replacement

Review the details here. If you need to change anything, click **Previous** to return to the previous settings. If you are okay with it, click **Generate Preview**.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20972'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 5  Review and Apply Changes

Link Genius will now access your content for the posts that meet your requirements. Once done, it will display the links for you to review. Here, you will see the post titles along with the changes that will be applied.

Review it and uncheck the ones you do not want to update. Once done, click **Apply change**s.

![Overview of the review and apply changes in the bulk updates tool](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%201457'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Link Genius will start updating your links (URLs, anchor texts, or both) in bulk. Once done, you will see a success message, as shown below.

![Sample of the success message in the Bulk update settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20695'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 6  Rollback the Bulk Update

At the bottom of the bulk update tab, you will see a section called the Bulk update history. This contains every bulk update you have created on your site.

![Overview of the bulk update history](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20939'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If you change your mind about a bulk update, you can roll it back from this section. To do so, hover over the link you just updated. You will see some options. Click **Rollback**.

![Hover over the update and click Rollback](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20703'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Next, you will see the post title, anchor text, and URLs of the links that will be rolled back. All links are checked by default, indicating they will be rolled back.

- Leave links checked to roll them back

- Uncheck the links you do not want to roll back

Once done, click **Rollback selected changes**.

![Click Rollback selected changes](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20714'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The rollback will be effected, and you will see a success message, as shown below.

![Once rolled back, you will see a success message](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201400%20690'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

This is how to bulk update your links using the Bulk update tool in Link Genius. If you have any questions, you’re always more than welcome to [contact our dedicated support team](https://rankmath.com/support/), who are available 24/7, 365 days a year.
