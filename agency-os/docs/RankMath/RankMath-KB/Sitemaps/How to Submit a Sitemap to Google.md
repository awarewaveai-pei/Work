# How to Submit a Sitemap to Google

			
			

	

If you run a website and want it to appear in search, you’ll often hear about **sitemaps.** Fortunately, our plugin – Rank Math – automatically creates [sitemaps](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-sitemaps/) for your website that make it easier for search engines to understand your site structure. We then combine all the individual sitemaps – for posts, pages, videos, etc. – into a single sitemap index. We do this so that you only have to submit the sitemap index link, which will be automatically updated when you change your site structure and its content.

![How to Submit a Sitemap to Google? Rank Math SEO](https://img.youtube.com/vi/IicxbPtcCyI/maxresdefault.jpg)
__

In this knowledgebase article, we’re going to walk you through how to submit a sitemap to Google. Without further ado – let’s get started:

## Table of Contents

- [Submit Sitemap to Google](#manual)

- [Automatically Submit Sitemaps to Google](#automatic)

## 1  Submit Sitemap to Google

To submit your sitemap to Google, follow the exact steps we walk you through below.

### 1.1  Sign in to Google Search Console

To get started, you’ll need to sign in to the [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) using the same account you [verified](https://rankmath.com/kb/google-site-verification/) & connected the website in question with.

### 1.2  Navigate to Sitemaps

Select your website and click on Sitemaps as shown below:

![Access sitemaps in Google Search Console](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%201046'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.3  Enter Sitemap URL

Enter **“sitemap_index.xml”** in the **Add a new sitemap** field in order to complete the sitemap URL.

![Add a new sitemap](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20964%20233'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 1.4  Submit the Sitemap

Click on **Submit,** and your sitemap will be submitted to Google.

![Submit sitemap to Google](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20958%20235'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 2  Automatically Submit Sitemaps to Google

If you’re using the [Analytics](https://rankmath.com/kb/analytics/) feature of Rank Math, Rank Math will automatically submit the sitemap to Google Search Console. You don’t need to manually submit your sitemap as we walked you through in the previous method.

To take advantage of this feature, ensure that you’ve connected Rank Math with your Google account and you’ve configured the Search Console settings.

![Search console settings in Rank Math Analytics settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20755%20306'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

To connect Rank Math with your Google account, you can [refer to its dedicated tutorial](https://rankmath.com/kb/connect-your-google-account/).

You can read more about [Sitemaps](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-sitemaps/) in our Knowledge Base article and [fix the sitemap issues](https://rankmath.com/kb/fix-sitemap-issues/) if you have any. If you still have any questions about how to submit a sitemap to Google – you’re always more than welcome to [contact our dedicated support team](https://rankmath.com/support/), and we are available 24/7, 365 days a year…
