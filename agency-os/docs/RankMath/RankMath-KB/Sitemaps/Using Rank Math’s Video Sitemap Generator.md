# Using Rank Math’s Video Sitemap Generator

			
			

	

For any website that uses videos, it is pretty essential to have a video sitemap. Video sitemap helps Google and other search bots easily find more information about where videos are placed so that they can be indexed.

Rank Math automatically looks for the presence of video content inside your posts as you write them, and if it finds any video content, it will add that post/page/CPT to the Video Sitemap.

## 1  How to Enable Video Sitemap in Rank Math?

To enable the Video Sitemap, head over to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO →** **Dashboard →** **Modules** section. Then, from the list of different modules, enable the **Video Sitemap** module as shown below.

![Enable Video Sitemap module in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Enable-Video-Sitemap-module-in-Rank-Math.png)

**Note:**The **Video sitemap** module can be enabled only if you have already enabled the **Schema** and **Sitemap** module. If any of the modules (Sitemap or Schema) is not activated, you’ll be prompted to activate both the Schema and Sitemap modules, before you enable the Video Sitemap module.

## 2  Benefits of Having Video Sitemap

Using a video sitemap gives your video content the best chance of ranking in the **Videos** tab in the search result. It’s worth noticing that although the video is originally uploaded to Youtube, your website page where the video has been embedded gets the chance to rank for the keyword.

![Video search results](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201000%20560'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

**PRO Tip:** Include relevant Youtube videos (even if you aren’t the original creator) on your pages and make use of a video sitemap to improve the visibility of your pages.

## 3  Configuring Video Sitemap

Once you have enabled the Video Sitemap, click on the **Settings** button or head over to the Sitemap Settings at **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Sitemap Settings → Video Sitemap,** and it will look like this:

![Video Sitemap settings in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202359%202283'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now, let us look at the different options available for us to configure.

### 3.1  Hide Sitemap

You have an option to hide the sitemap from normal visitors and only show the sitemap to bots. By default, this option to hide sitemap is disabled, and it is completely based on your preference.

![Hide Sitemap - Video Sitemap settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20218'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.2  Video Post Type

You can choose what post types to be considered for the Video Sitemaps. Once you’ve chosen the post types, Rank Math will automatically detect videos only from these selected post types.

![Video Post Type - video sitemap settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20491'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.3  YouTube API Key

Enter your [YouTube API key](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-get-youtube-api-key/) to fetch video details automatically. If left blank, the default public API will be used, though it may have limitations and reduced reliability.

![Add YouTube API Key](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201532%20292'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.4  Custom Fields

And, you can instruct Rank Math to scan custom fields and look for the presence of video content. By default, we look for the videos in the default content.

Add only one entry per line so that Rank Math can understand them and include them in your sitemap.

![Custom fields - video sitemap settings](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201500%20448'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3.5  Finding Your Video Sitemap

After making the necessary changes, click the **Save Changes** button, and your Video Sitemap URL is available at the top of your page. You can copy the URL to submit it to search engines or simply access your video sitemap URL from this page.

![Rank Math Video Sitemap URL](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20826%20122'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## 4  Frequently Asked Questions

### Why Does My Video Sitemap Lead to a 404 Error Page?

Your video sitemap index is likely returning a 404 error because of caching issues. You can refer to this guide on [excluding sitemaps from caching](https://rankmath.com/kb/exclude-sitemaps-from-caching/).
