# How to Get a VAT/GST Refund from Rank Math

			
			

	

In this knowledgebase article, we’re going to walk through the steps on how to get a VAT/GST refund from rank Math.

If you wish to get a VAT/GST refund from us then you’ll need to fill out a form on our [FastSpring Consumer Support](https://fastspring.com/consumer-support-form/tax-vat-gst/) page.

This is how the form will look like:

![VAT/ GST Form](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/vatgst-form-1.jpg)

Please select the **VAT/ GST** option in the Primary Category and then fill out the required details including order details and contact information and click on **Submit**. Once you have submitted the information, the team will further process your refund. If you still have any questions about getting a tax refund on your purchase, feel free to [reach out to our support team](https://rankmath.com/contact/).
