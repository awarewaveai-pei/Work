# Why is My Subscription Not Showing Up in My Rank Math Account?

			
			

	

When you subscribe to a Rank Math subscription, you will have to provide the email address of your preferred Rank Math account, as shown below.

![Rank Math PRO sign-up page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/pro-sign-up.jpg)

After your purchase, log in to your [Rank Math account dashboard](https://rankmath.com/my-account/) using the email associated with your subscription to verify the subscription has been added.

![Confirm if the Rank Math PRO is added in your account](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/confirm-Rank-Math-PRO-addition.png)

If the dashboard still shows a FREE account, double-check that you’ve logged in with the correct email address used for the purchase.

To check this, click on the account menu at the top of the page and select ‘Edit Profile’ from the dropdown, or navigate directly to ‘Account details’ in the left sidebar, as indicated below.

![Navigating to the Account Details page in Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/navigate-to-account-details-page-in-Rank-Math.png)

On the account details page, scroll down to find your registered email address in the ‘Email address’ section.

![The Email address section in Rank Math Account page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Email-address-section-in-RM-Account-page.png)

If the email address doesn’t match the one used for your purchase, log out by hovering over the account menu and selecting ‘Logout’, as shown below. Then, log back in using the email associated with your purchase.

![Logout from Rank Math account page](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201399%20636'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

If the email you added to your PRO plan differs from the one on your [FREE account](https://rankmath.com/kb/free-account-benefits/), you must contact our support team to merge both accounts or change the email connected to your PRO plan. To open a ticket, visit the [Rank Math Support Forum](https://rankmath.com/support/) and click the **Submit a Ticket** button.

![submit a ticket](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201602%20358'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the **Ticket Title**section, enter a one-line summary of the issue. Once done, you can provide more details in the **Ticket Question**section, as shown below.

![Enter a Ticket Title and Question](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201768%201098'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In the **Sensitive Data** section, enter the email address(es) you want to connect to your PRO account. If you’re merging your FREE and PRO accounts, you need to select one of the emails as your preferred email address.

**Note:**You need to include the email address you used to purchase the PRO plan or a screenshot of the invoice you received from FastSpring.com. Once done, click the **SUBMIT YOUR TICKET**button.

![Enter sensitive data and submit the ticket](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201690%20906'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

With the details provided, our support team will locate your PRO account and change the email connected to it or merge it with your FREE account, as you requested.

For more details on how to install Rank Math PRO on your website, you can [refer to our guide](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-upgrade-to-rank-math-pro/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I purchased Rank Math PRO, but I’m unable to download It. What should I do?

Log into your [Rank Math account](https://rankmath.com/my-account/) and download Rank Math PRO from your dashboard. If it is unavailable, you may have upgraded to PRO using a different email address from your intended one. In that case, [contact our support team](https://rankmath.com/support/) to merge both accounts or change the email connected to the PRO account.

We hope you understand the process of merging or changing the email address connected to your PRO account. If you have questions, you’re always more than welcome to [contact our dedicated support team](https://rankmath.com/support/). We’re available 24/7, 365 days a year…
