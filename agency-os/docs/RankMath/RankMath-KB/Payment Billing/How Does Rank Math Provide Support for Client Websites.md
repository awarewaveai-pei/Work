# How Does Rank Math Provide Support for Client Websites

			
			

	

Rank Math offers 24x7x365 priority support for Business and Agency users. The priority support of the Business and Agency license is applicable for their clients too, but, capped to a maximum of 100 client websites for Business license and 500 client websites for Agency license.

If you’re a [Rank Math Business or Agency](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) user, this knowledgebase article will walk you through how to get priority support for Rank Math on your client websites.

## Support for Client Websites [Business](https://rankmath.com/pricing/)

If Business and Agency users’ clients have any support query, more specific to features available in the PRO version of Rank Math, then the support ticket in our forum should be raised by the **Business/Agency user holding the license** on behalf of the client.

In case if the client is opening a support ticket in Rank Math with a question related to a PRO feature, then we’ll request the client to **ask the license provider to open a support ticket** instead.

## Support for Client Websites on Free Version

However, if the client is facing any issue or a support query that is related to the **feature in the Free version**, then they can [create a free account at Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/kb/free-account-benefits/) and seek support like regular users.

That’s it! If you’ve absolutely any questions on Rank Math or looking for support with your client’s website, then feel free to [reach our support team directly from here](https://rankmath.com/support/), and we’re always here to help.
