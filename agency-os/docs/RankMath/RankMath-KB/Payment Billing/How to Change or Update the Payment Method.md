# How to Change or Update the Payment Method

			
			

	

Rank Math offers payment options like card, PayPal, and Amazon Pay to purchase a [Rank Math PRO subscription](https://rankmath.com/pricing/). And, if you wish to change the payment method, you can always do so yourself by following these steps we covered in the video:

In this knowledgebase tutorial, we’ll cover the steps to update your payment method in detail.

## 1  Access Rank Math’s FastSpring Checkout Page

To make any changes to your payment method, at first, you need to access your Rank Math FastSpring Checkout page.

### 1.1  Navigate to Rank Math Account Area

Start by logging in to your [Rank Math account area](https://rankmath.com/my-account/). Then choose **Subscriptions** from the left sidebar as shown below.

![Navigate to Subscriptions in Rank Math account area](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Navigate-to-Subscriptions-in-Rank-Math-Account-area.png)

### 1.2  Change Payment Methods

In the Subscriptions and Billing page, navigate to the **Payment Methods** tab and click the **Change Payment Methods** button.

![Change payment methods](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Change-payment-methods.png)

### 1.3  Access FastSpring Checkout

The [FastSpring Checkout Page](https://rankmathllc.onfastspring.com/account/) will now open in a new tab. To allow FastSpring to access your information, enter the email address you’ve used to purchase the Rank Math PRO subscription and then click the **Continue** button.

![Enter email address at Rank math Fastspring checkout page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Enter-email-address-at-Rank-Math-Fastspring-checkout-page.png)

Once you click **Continue**, you’ll see a notice about an email with login instructions being sent to your inbox.

![Email with instructions sent from Rank Math Fastspring checkout page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Email-with-instructions-sent-from-Rank-Math-Fastspring-checkout.png)

### 1.4  Login to FastSpring Checkout

Check your inbox for an email from our Rank Math SEO team with a unique login link to FastSpring. Choose the **Click here to manage your orders link** option as shown below.

![Manage your Rank Math orders](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Manage-your-Rank-Math-orders.png)

**Caution:** The FastSpring login link is unique to your account, and we request you not to share the link with anyone else, as they include your subscription details.

## 2  Update Payment Method for Rank Math PRO Subscriptions

Once you’ve logged in to FastSpring, navigate to the **Subscriptions** tab.

![Navigate to Rank Math subscriptions page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Navigate-to-Rank-Math-subscriptions-page.png)

Here you’ll find all your Rank Math subscriptions, and if you’d like to change the payment method for a subscription, click the **Manage** button against your subscription. From the drop-down list, choose **Update Payment Method**.

![Update payment method for your Rank Math PRO subscription](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Update-payment-method-for-your-Rank-Math-PRO-subscription-1.png)

Now you’ll see a popup, where you can click the **Add Payment Method** button.

![Choose Add Payment Method in the popup](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Choose-add-payment-method-in-the-popup.png)

From the drop-down list, you can choose your preferred payment method and then follow the on-screen instructions.

![Choose a payment method from the drop-down](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Choose-payment-methods-from-the-drop-down.png)

Once you’ve successfully added, the payment method for your Rank Math PRO subscription will get updated.

## 3  Add Payment Method

The previous steps allowed you to update the payment method associated with a Rank Math PRO subscription.

But you can always add more payment methods to your account from the **Account Details and Payment Methods** tab for future use. Here you will see all your current payment methods.

![Navigate to Account Details and Payment Methods tab](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Navigate-to-account-details-and-payment-methos-tab.png)

To add a new payment method, click the **Add Payment Method** button as shown below.

![Choose Add Payment Method](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Choose-Add-Payment-Method.png)

You can find various payment methods available here, and you can choose a method of your choice. Note that when you select **Apply to all my subscriptions** checkbox, all your existing PRO subscriptions will further use the new payment method being added.

Follow the on-screen instructions, and when the payment method is added successfully, you’ll find it listed here in the dashboard.

![New payment method added](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/New-payment-method-added.png)

## 4  Set Default Payment Method

**Note:** The option **Apply to all my subscriptions** will **only override** your payment method for existing subscriptions. But if you wish to choose a payment method for your **future subscriptions**, then you have to choose it as a **default payment method**.

To change the default payment method, click **Manage** against your preferred payment method and then choose the **Make Default** option.

![Set default payment method in Rank Math Fastspring checkout page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Set-default-payment-method-in-Rank-Math-Fastspring-checkout-page.png)

Now you’ll see a prompt to confirm changing your default payment method. Click **Yes** to proceed further.

![Confirm changing default payment method](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Confirm-changing-default-payment-method.png)

Now your default payment method will be changed and will reflect here in the dashboard.

![Default payment method changed](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Default-payment-method-changed.png)

## 5  Remove Payment Method

If you wish to remove a payment method, click the **Manage** button against the method you’d like to remove and then choose the **Remove** option.

![Remove payment method in Rank Math Fastspring checkout page](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Remove-payment-method-1.png)

**Note:** You will not be able to remove the payment method tied to an active subscription. To continue removing the payment method, please set another payment method for existing subscriptions at first, and then proceed to **Remove**.

Now you’ll see a prompt to confirm removing the payment method. Click **Yes** to proceed further.

![Confirm removing payment method](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Confirm-removing-payment-method.png)

The page will reload, and in a matter of seconds, your payment method will be removed.

And, that’s it! We hope the tutorial was helpful in changing or updating your payment method in Rank Math. If you still have absolutely any questions, please feel free to [reach our support team directly from here](https://rankmath.com/support/), and we’re always here to help.
