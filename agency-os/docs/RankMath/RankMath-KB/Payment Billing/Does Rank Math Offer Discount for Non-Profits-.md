# Does Rank Math Offer Discount for Non-Profits?

			
			

	

If you’re part of a non-profit organization looking to boost your online presence, you’re in the right place. At Rank Math, we understand non-profits work with limited resources. We also understand the importance of supporting organizations that impact our communities.

That’s why we packaged a sweet deal to support you in upholding your social responsibility. Read on to discover our discount policy for non-profits.

## How Does Rank Math Support Non-Profits?

**Rank Math offers a 30% discount on the pricing to all non-profits for the first year.** The discount is applicable for Rank Math PRO membership as well as Content AI membership.

Please note the discount is applicable only for the first year and not on renewals.

If you are a non-profit, please share one or more supporting documents listed below to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#580b2d2828372a2c182a39363335392c30763b3735). Our team will review it and get back to you as soon as possible.

- A copy of a document from your tax agency indicating you are a non-profit.

- A copy of your certificate of incorporation or a similar document recognizing you as a non-profit.

- A copy of your Internal Revenue Service (IRS) tax exemption certificate or a reference to your non-profit in the IRS list of tax-exempt organizations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if You Don’t Have Any Supporting Documents?

If you do not have the above documents but are affiliated with a parent organization, then please share the documents of your parent organization along with a signed statement from the organization recognizing you as a local nonprofit affiliate.

### Does Rank Math Offer Free PRO Membership for non-profits?

No, we don’t. We only offer a 30% discount on the Rank Math PRO membership for the first year. The discount will not be applicable on renewals.

That’s it. If you have any absolutely any questions, you’re more than welcome to [contact our dedicated support team](https://rankmath.com/support/). We’re available 24/7, 365 days a year…
