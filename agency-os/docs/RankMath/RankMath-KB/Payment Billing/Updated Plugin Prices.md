# Updated Plugin Prices

			
			

	

Over the years, Rank Math has grown into one of the most powerful and trusted SEO plugins in the WordPress ecosystem. We’ve built this journey together with you (our users) at the center of every decision.

It’s with this same commitment that we’re announcing a change that will enable us to continue serving you at the highest level: **an adjustment in our pricing**.

This is not just about numbers, but more about ensuring that we can continue to deliver the innovation, support, and reliability you’ve come to expect from our products and services.

Change, while not always easy, is an **inevitable part of growth**. This pricing adjustment allows us to continue improving Rank Math at the pace and quality you deserve. Let us walk you through the reasons that have led us to this decision:

## 1  New Features and Capabilities

We’ve been hard at work developing innovative features and capabilities that empower you to achieve even greater heights. These enhancements have been designed to make your interactions with Rank Math smoother, more efficient, and help you stay ahead of the competition.

### 1.1  **New Features** added to Rank Math

Since our last price revision in March 2024, we’ve rolled out some of the most requested and cutting-edge features to keep you ahead of the competition. Here’s a glimpse of what we had done:

✔️ **Added:** [HUGE!] Feature to [track AI Search Traffic](https://rankmath.com/kb/analytics/?play-video=L-yMbZO4Hks#traffic-source) from AI search engines like Gemini and ChatGPT directly within Rank Math Analytics
✔️ **Added:** [HUGE!] Feature to determine the [search intent of the Focus Keyword](https://rankmath.com/kb/search-intent-analysis/)
✔️ **Added:** [Side-by-side comparison](https://rankmath.com/kb/seo-analysis/#side-by-side-comparison) of your site’s SEO Analysis with your competitor’s
✔️ **Added:** Robots.txt validator tool in the [Robots.txt settings](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-edit-robots-txt-with-rank-math/#edit-robotstxt)
✔️ **Added:** Support for [LLMs.txt file](https://rankmath.com/kb/llms-txt/)
✔️ **Added:** Field to insert the Creator data in the [DataSets property of the Dataset Schema](https://rankmath.com/kb/dataset-schema/#datasets)
✔️ **Added:** Variable support to the [“Replace” field in Replacements](https://rankmath.com/kb/general-settings/#replacements)
✔️ **Added:** New field for the [YouTube API key](https://rankmath.com/kb/how-to-get-youtube-api-key/) in the [Video Sitemap settings](https://rankmath.com/kb/video-sitemap/) to auto-fetch data accurately for YouTube videos
✔️ **Added:** Product Carousel Schema will now be added to the Shop and Product Archive pages when [Remove Schema Markup on Shop Archives](https://rankmath.com/kb/general-settings/#remove-snippet-data) and [Remove Snippet Data](https://rankmath.com/kb/titles-and-meta/#product-categories-remove-snippet-data) for the archive are disabled
✔️ **Added:** [Power words](https://rankmath.com/blog/power-words/#power-words-list) support for French, Japanese, Spanish, Italian, Finnish, and Hungarian languages
✔️ **Added:** Option to [lock the modified date](https://rankmath.com/kb/prevent-modified-date-from-being-updated/) of a post when there is no substantial change in the content
✔️ **Added:** Option to remove the selected [About & Contact page from Local SEO settings](https://rankmath.com/kb/local-seo/#about-page)
✔️ **Added:** [Option to include Authors](https://rankmath.com/kb/configure-sitemaps/#include-authors-without-posts) without any posts in the Sitemap

Here’s a look at some of the newest features:

✔️ Added: [Filter](https://rankmath.com/kb/filters-hooks-api-developer/#remove-target-blank-attribute) to exclude certain domains from the [Open External Links in New Tab](https://rankmath.com/kb/open-links-in-new-window/) option
✔️ Added: [Filter](https://rankmath.com/kb/filters-hooks-api-developer/#html-sitemap-post-limit) to set a hard limit on the posts displayed in the HTML sitemap, defaults to 1000 posts
✔️ Added: [New filter](https://rankmath.com/kb/filters-hooks-api-developer/#exclude-canonicals-from-sitemap) to exclude posts with canonical URLs in the sitemaps

✔️ Improved: `rank_math/manage_posts/seo_filter_options` [filter](https://rankmath.com/kb/filters-hooks-api-developer/#remove-seo-filters-from-posts) can now be used to remove [SEO filters from Posts](https://rankmath.com/kb/bulk-editing-in-rank-math/#filtering)

## 5  Long-term Sustainability

As we grow together, it’s essential that we build a sustainable foundation. We do not want to be yet another plugin that abandons plugin development and leave their users stranded.

This pricing adjustment allows us to continue investing in research, development, and innovation. By securing our future, we can stay true to our promise of delivering high-quality solutions that stand the test of time.

## 6  **Inflation and Operational Costs**

The global landscape is ever-changing, and we’re not immune to the impact of rising costs. Running Rank Math at scale today is very different from when we started. Serving millions of websites securely and reliably requires far more resources than it used to.

- **Servers & Infrastructure**: We’ve massively scaled our backend systems to handle the growing data demands of Rank Math Analytics, Content AI, and other features you rely on daily.

- **APIs & Integrations**: The APIs that power features like Content AI have grown both in complexity and cost.

These ongoing costs have increased significantly. We’ve absorbed them for as long as possible to avoid raising prices, but to keep delivering the speed, reliability, and innovation you expect, an adjustment has become necessary.
