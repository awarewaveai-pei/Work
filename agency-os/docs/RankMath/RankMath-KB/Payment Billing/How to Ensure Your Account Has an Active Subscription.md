# How to Ensure Your Account Has an Active Subscription

			
			

	

In this article, we’ll walk you through how to check if your Rank Math account has an active subscription.

## Steps to Ensure That Your Account Has an Active Subscription

To ensure that your website is connected with an active Rank Math subscription, follow the exact steps we’ve discussed below.

### 1  Check Your Rank Math Subscription

You can head over to the Rank Math website and [log in to your account area](https://rankmath.com/my-account/). From the left sidebar, you can choose **Subscriptions** to check the current status of your Rank Math subscription.

![Rank Math Subscriptions](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Rank-Math-subscriptions-in-account-area.png)

### 2  Check if You’re Using the Latest Version of Rank Math PRO

Make sure that you are using the latest version of the [Rank Math PRO](https://rankmath.com/pricing/) plugin. To check if you’re using the latest version of Rank Math PRO, head over to **WordPress Dashboard → Plugins** and look for the version of Rank Math PRO installed on your website.

![Check the Rank Math PRO version in WordPress](https://rankmath.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/version.jpg)

You can then check with our [official changelog here](https://rankmath.com/changelog/) to ensure you’re using the latest version of Rank Math PRO. If not, you can simply update the plugin or download the latest version of Rank Math PRO directly from your Rank Math Account area.

![Download Rank Math PRO](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20341'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### 3  Check if Your Website is Connected

Finally, ensure that you have connected the website using the correct [account](https://rankmath.com/my-account/websites/). You can verify that by heading over to the **Websites** section inside your Rank Math account area. If your website is not shown here or you’re seeing **No connected websites found** message as shown below, it indicates that your website is not connected to your Rank Math account.

![No connected websites found with Rank Math account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20353'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In that case, you’d need to reconnect your website with this Rank Math account by navigating to **WordPress Dashboard → Rank Math SEO → Dashboard → Help**. If the page shows Connected, choose **Disconnect Account** so that you can reconnect your website with your correct Rank Math account.

![Choose Disconnect account](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20319'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Now you can click the **Connect Now** button.

![Click Connect Now in Rank Math](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20420'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

On the next page, ensure you choose the correct Rank Math account associated with your PRO license. You can click the drop-down list to choose the Rank Math PRO subscription if you’ve multiple Rank Math PRO subscriptions. Finally, click the **OK, ACTIVATE NOW** button.

![Activate Rank Math license](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20720'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Once your website is successfully connected with your Rank Math PRO account, you will see that status on the Help page of your WordPress dashboard and in your Rank Math Websites section.

![Check connected websites](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20900%20313'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

We hope this article has helped you ensure that your account has an active subscription. However, if you are still facing any problems or have any queries, please feel free to [contact our support team](https://rankmath.com/support/), and we’d be happy to help you at any given time.
