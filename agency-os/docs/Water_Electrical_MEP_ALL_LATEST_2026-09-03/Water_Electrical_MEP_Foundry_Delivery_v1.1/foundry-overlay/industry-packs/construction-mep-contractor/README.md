# Construction / MEP Contractor Industry Pack v1.1

Reusable industry layer for 10–25 person water/electrical/MEP contractor operations systems.

## What this pack adds
- project/site/workforce/dispatch semantics;
- construction field execution, drawings/photos/quality;
- procurement/material lifecycle;
- contract/progress billing/retention/project-cost semantics;
- bounded RWD offline continuity;
- separation of planned Dispatch from actual/approved labor time;
- replaceable accounting integration + reconciliation;
- AI authorization constraints.

The pack does **not** force a technology provider, native mobile app, installable PWA, multi-tenant SaaS, or online payment gateway. Those remain project choices.
