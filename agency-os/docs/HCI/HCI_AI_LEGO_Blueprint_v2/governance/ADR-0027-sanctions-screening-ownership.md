# ADR-0027 — Sanctions/Export-Control Screening Ownership

Status: **ACCEPTED**
Date: 2026-09-02
Decision authority: Operator-delegated (2026-09-02, full delegation to Claude); operator may supersede with a later explicit decision
Scope: Ownership of sanctions/export-control screening of HCI's own research Subjects; this ADR does not build a screening pipeline, integrate a real watchlist vendor, deploy to production, process real-person data, or make a compliance/legal representation.

## Context

`09_AI_LEGO_COGNITIVE_OS_BOUNDARY.md:10` assigns AI LEGO's Global Master Plan ownership of "Global jurisdiction," and `09:43` names a "Global jurisdiction" module handling "cross-border data protection, AI regulation, sanctions/trade constraints, localization, jurisdiction conflicts." The same file, six lines later, states the opposite constraint: `09:96-98` — "**AI LEGO must not own**: HCI Person records, HCI relationships, HCI trust scores, HCI public claims, HCI cooperation decisions." A Person under HCI research who turns out to be sanctioned is exactly HCI Person data, so the module delegated to screen it is the one product forbidden to hold what it would need to screen. `09:47` records this seam as `🔴 DECISION PENDING (HD-17, no urgency)`, sourced to `HCI30_RESEARCH_REVIEW/22_OPEN_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md:110-112` and `HCI30_RESEARCH_REVIEW/12_RED_TEAM_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md:393-408`. RT-15 (`12_RED_TEAM_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md:399,401`) states the resulting risk directly: "creates legal exposure for Aware Wave that no chapter assigns ownership to defend against," with "potential export-control/sanctions violation exposure from conducting or facilitating research on a restricted individual or entity." The cross-check at `12:473,506,515` confirms none of the three non-red-team reviewers' KEEP items address this gap and the security/privacy reviewer's KEEP list is empty — RT-15 "stands unmitigated" going into this decision.

Three existing, ratified architectural patterns bound the solution space rather than requiring a new one:

1. **Provider adapter rule** (`18_LEGO_CONTRACT_AND_COMPONENT_TAXONOMY.md:82-93`): "Search providers, LLMs, payment systems, CRMs, and other vendors must sit behind adapters," with the stated chain `capability → Router → Provider Adapter`, and a vendor-replaceability test already required for the analogous `llm.reason` capability (`18:78`) — the same shape a sanctions-watchlist vendor needs.
2. **Thin-reference crossing pattern**: the Shared Kernel (`09_AI_LEGO_COGNITIVE_OS_BOUNDARY.md:68-71`) already specifies `PersonRef`/`EntityRef` as the only way a full Person record may be referenced across the HCI/AI LEGO boundary, and `10_HCI_AI_LEGO_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md:71` already enforces the identical rule for `EvidenceRef` so "Evidence" cannot silently mean a full record on one side of the boundary.
3. **Control/Mitigation is already a scheduled Wave 1 entity**: `22_HCI_TOP_TIER_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_PLAN.md:47` lists the Wave 1 ERD deliverable as including "Case/Engagement, DataSubjectRequest, PersonMergeLog/PersonSplitLog, **Control/Mitigation**, subject_type stub" — a sanctions screen is a `kind` of the Control this ADR's Decision does not invent, only specifies.

The natural pipeline location is the identity-resolution stage: `06_HCI30_PUBLIC_INTELLIGENCE.md:73,82` already gates `MATCH_CONFIRMED` (ADR-0015, `HD-04=A`) to deterministic-identifier or human-confirmed evidence before anything is admitted downstream, and `22:97,99` places `identity match` in the Wave 6 pipeline alongside an already-scheduled purpose-attestation gate for Deep Due Diligence / Continuous Monitoring modes (`06:257-263`). The blocking scope this ADR references — "Case progression" — is itself conditional: `07_HCI30_DOMAIN_MODEL_AND_DATA_OWNERSHIP.md:56` records the Case/Engagement aggregate as `🔴 DECISION PENDING (HD-10)`, and `governance/wave1/HCI_WAVE1_DOMAIN_ERD.md` already writes the relationship as "Case/Engagement (when HD-10 is resolved)." This ADR does not resolve HD-10; §Required implementation gates below states the fallback explicitly.

## Decision

**HCI owns sanctions/export-control screening of its own Subjects, because it owns the Person data being screened.** AI LEGO must not own HCI Person records (`09:96-98`); an owner that is forbidden to hold the data cannot be the accountable screener of that data. Implementation:

1. **Control record, `kind = SANCTIONS_SCREEN`** — a subtype of the `Control/Mitigation` entity already scheduled for the Wave 1 ERD (`22:47`), owned by HCI, linked to the Person subject (via the `subject_type`/`subject_id` stub from ADR-0011) and to the pending Case/Engagement aggregate (HD-10) once it exists.
2. **Pluggable `ScreeningProvider` adapter** — implements the existing Provider adapter rule (`18:82-93`): `screening.sanctions` capability → Screening Router → `ScreeningProvider` adapter, so the watchlist vendor is replaceable without touching domain logic, on the same pattern already required of `llm.reason` (`18:78`).
3. **AI LEGO may be consulted, never own the data** — if AI LEGO's jurisdiction module (`09:43`) is used at all (e.g., for jurisdiction-conflict reasoning around a hit), it receives only a thin `PersonRef`/hashed-identifier payload via the Integration Contract, following the `EvidenceRef` precedent (`10:71`) exactly — never Person PII, and never the determination of HIT/CLEAR itself.
4. **A positive screen blocks progression until human review** — `Control.status = HIT` is a blocking Control: the enclosing workflow (Case/Engagement per HD-10, or its Wave 1 placeholder if HD-10 is not yet resolved when this lands) cannot advance to a PROCEED-equivalent Decision while an open `HIT` exists. Clearing requires a `HUMAN`-actor transition carrying an evidence reference — the identical deterministic-or-human, never-LLM-alone shape ADR-0015 already established for `MATCH_CONFIRMED` (`06:82`), reused rather than re-derived.

**Why the alternatives lose:**

- **Delegate screening fully to AI LEGO's jurisdiction module as originally sketched (`09:43`)** — requires handing AI LEGO the full Person record to screen it, which directly violates `09:96-98`. This is not a workaround of the RT-15 gap; it *is* the contradiction RT-15 names (`12:398-400`). Rejected.
- **Do nothing (leave HD-17 open)** — leaves RT-15's regulatory exposure unmitigated (`12:401,506`) with a live seam and no assigned owner; `22_OPEN_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md:110-112` exists precisely because that silence is an accident, not a decision. Rejected.
- **Screen inside AI LEGO on hashed identifiers, but let AI LEGO make the HIT/CLEAR call** — the thin-ref crossing pattern is right, but letting AI LEGO's runtime decide whether *HCI's own case* may proceed re-couples the two products at the control-flow layer that the confirmed peer-product independence verdict (`02_PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` "Confirmed correct... failure independence... deployment independence") exists to prevent — HCI must remain fully usable when AI LEGO is unavailable (`13_TESTING_REPLAY_ROLLBACK_AND_SRE.md` §Graceful Degradation, cited at `02_PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md`). Consulting AI LEGO behind a thin ref for reasoning support is fine; AI LEGO gating HCI's workflow is not. Rejected.

**This ADR does not authorize**: processing real-person data through any screening path, a production deployment of the `ScreeningProvider` adapter, a compliance or legal representation about sanctions-law coverage (that remains HD-20's regulatory-scope question), or a decision on HD-10 (Case/Engagement) or HD-09 (Shared Kernel extraction) — both remain independently open and this ADR's gates degrade gracefully if they resolve differently than assumed here.

## Required implementation gates

Each gate names the concrete artifact/test that must exist and fail without the control, and the Wave it lands in (`22_HCI_TOP_TIER_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_PLAN.md` Wave numbering). All gates are **OPEN**.

1. **Wave 1 — `Control` table in the domain ERD.** `governance/wave1/HCI_WAVE1_DOMAIN_ERD.md` gains a `Control` table entry (the `Control/Mitigation` entity already promised at `22:47`) with `kind` enum including `SANCTIONS_SCREEN`, subject FK (Person, via the ADR-0011 `subject_type`/`subject_id` stub), nullable Case/Engagement FK, `status` enum `{CLEAR, HIT, PENDING_REVIEW, CLEARED_BY_HUMAN}`, `screened_at`, `provider_ref`, `evidence_ref`. Test `CONTROL-SCHEMA-01`: a `Control` row with `kind=SANCTIONS_SCREEN` and no subject FK must fail an insert/constraint check.
2. **Wave 1 — enforceable block, not app-layer prose.** `governance/wave1/HCI_WAVE1_NEGATIVE_TEST_SPEC.md` gains a "Sanctions screen block" section with `SANCTIONS-NEG-01` (attempt to transition the enclosing workflow to its PROCEED-equivalent state while a `Control(kind=SANCTIONS_SCREEN, status=HIT)` row has no `CLEARED_BY_HUMAN` successor → denied at the state-machine/DB-constraint level) and `SANCTIONS-NEG-02` (a `HIT → CLEAR`-family transition recorded without a `HUMAN` actor and `evidence_ref` → rejected, mirroring the `DETERMINISTIC`/`HUMAN`-only constraint ADR-0015 already enforces for `MATCH_CONFIRMED`).
3. **Wave 3 — `ScreeningProvider` adapter contract.** A `screening.sanctions` capability defined under `packages/contracts/` per the Provider adapter rule (`18:82-93`), with a vendor-replaceability contract test — `SCREEN-PROVIDER-VENDOR-01` — asserting at least two interchangeable `ScreeningProvider` implementations (a mock/synthetic provider is sufficient) produce equivalent-shape `Control` output, the same test class already required for `llm.reason` (`18:78`).
4. **Wave 6 — pipeline placement and acceptance test.** The screening step is inserted into the Hardened Public Intelligence pipeline (`22:97`) immediately after `identity match` (i.e., after `MATCH_CONFIRMED`, `06:73-82`) and gated to Deep Due Diligence / Continuous Monitoring modes (`06:257-263`), alongside the purpose-attestation gate already scheduled for that Wave (`22:99`). Added to Wave 6's existing acceptance list (`22:103`): `SANCTIONS-SCREEN-01` — a Deep Due Diligence run against a synthetic-watchlist fixture subject produces a `HIT` Control, and the run's report cannot be exported or marked complete without a recorded `CLEARED_BY_HUMAN` transition.
5. **Wave 3 — Integration Contract addendum, thin-ref only.** `10_HCI_AI_LEGO_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md` gains a note, in the same form as its existing `EvidenceRef` enforcement note (`10:71`), that any optional consultation of AI LEGO's jurisdiction module carries only a hashed/thin `PersonRef`-class payload, never full Person PII, and never returns a HIT/CLEAR determination — HCI's `ScreeningProvider` always makes that call. CI check `CONTRACT-SCREEN-01`: a schema-conformance test failing if any wire field sent to AI LEGO's jurisdiction capability matches a full-PII Person field name.

## Consequences

- HCI, not AI LEGO, is the accountable owner of sanctions/export-control screening for its own Subjects — closing the RT-15 gap without violating `09:96-98`.
- `09_AI_LEGO_COGNITIVE_OS_BOUNDARY.md:47`'s `🔴 DECISION PENDING (HD-17)` block is answered by this ADR; the blueprint prose should be updated to cross-reference it (a documentation follow-up, not a gate this ADR performs — this ADR touches no file but itself).
- The blocking mechanism is written against the Case/Engagement aggregate conditionally: if HD-10 resolves to Option A (dedicated aggregate) the `Control` FK targets it directly; if HD-10 resolves otherwise, gate 1's Case/Engagement FK is nullable and the block instead attaches to whatever workflow unit HD-10 lands on — this ADR does not pre-decide HD-10.
- AI LEGO's jurisdiction module keeps a narrower, still-legitimate role (general cross-border/regulatory reasoning consulted via thin ref) without ever becoming a second holder of HCI Person data — consistent with `09:96-98` and the peer-product independence verdict.
- A later change to screening ownership, the blocking mechanism, or the thin-ref-only constraint requires a superseding ADR.

None of the five gates has an implementation yet (checked 2026-09-02); all remain open.
- Before implementation, rollback is to supersede this ADR with a later explicit operator decision — no data, schema, or production system is touched by this ADR itself, so no migration or data rollback path applies.
- After implementation, a `Control(kind=SANCTIONS_SCREEN)` block may not be weakened, bypassed, or auto-cleared in place; any change to the clearing rule (gate 2) requires a superseding ADR plus new negative-test evidence, the same rollback discipline ADR-0015 and ADR-0016 already apply to their own gates.
