# 15 — Brand & Go-To-Market Principles

> **V2 status note**: this chapter received no findings from the 9-reviewer architecture review (it is marketing/positioning scope, not architecture) and is carried forward from V1 essentially unchanged, with one structural cross-reference added below.

## Aware Wave

Recommended role: **Parent brand / intelligence technology company**

## HCI-30 positioning

Possible description: Human Collaboration Intelligence

Core promise:

> Understand a Subject, verify evidence, evaluate relationships, and collaborate with controlled risk.

Do not market as: "AI can read anyone", "95% lie detection", "predict bad people", pseudo-scientific certainty.

Strong brand statement:

> HCI does not promise to see through every person.
> HCI shows why you believe, where you may be wrong, and how to collaborate safely even when uncertain.

**Structural cross-reference (new in V2)**: this brand promise is now backed by a structural guarantee, not only report prose — see `04_HCI30_PRODUCT_SPEC.md` §Cooperation Safety Principle for the `Control`/`Mitigation` entity that makes "how to collaborate safely" an auditable record.

## AI LEGO Cognitive Engineering OS positioning

Core promise:

> Engineer how AI reasons, selects methods, verifies, replays, and executes complex cognitive workflows.

## Product relationship

Commercially separate SKUs:

```text
HCI-30
AI LEGO Cognitive Engineering OS
HCI-30 + AI LEGO Cognitive Integration
```

Do not force HCI customers to buy Cognitive OS. See `10_HCI_AI_LEGO_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md` §Usage Metering for the billing-independence clause this requires in practice.

## HCI commercial moat

The moat is not just UI or LLM usage. Potential core IP: Evidence Ontology, Relationship Ontology, Trust Domain Model, Confidence Engine, Contradiction Engine, Context Engine, Incentive Engine, Risk Engine, Permission Engine, Decision Engine, Calibration Engine, Public Intelligence Source/Claim Graph.

## Methodology brand

HCI should become a methodology, not only software. Potential future assets: HCI Collaboration Intelligence Standard (HCIS), HCI Methodology Whitepaper, HCI Institute / Research arm, Scientific Advisory Board, Certification / training, Benchmarks, Case studies, Method validation reports.

## Advisory board disciplines

Potential: organizational psychology, behavioral science, negotiation, risk, cybersecurity, OSINT/intelligence, privacy law, cross-cultural research, statistics, enterprise governance.

## Public methodology integrity standard

Recommended public commitments:

```text
We do not infer honesty from appearance.
We do not infer criminality from personality.
We do not treat allegations as facts.
We do not treat correlation as destiny.
We separate evidence from interpretation.
We preserve uncertainty.
We disclose method and source provenance.
```

**Recommended addition (new in V2, not yet a public commitment)**: ADR-0014 resolves `HD-03=A`; consider adding "We provide the Subject of our research a way to be heard" only after its runtime gate and the relevant legal/market decisions are complete. This must not be marketed from a synthetic skeleton alone.
