## The pattern behind this list

45 is a large number for a single disposition, and reading it as "V1 is 45 things incomplete" would be the wrong takeaway. Cross-referencing against `14_CONVERGENCE_MATRIX.md`, a large fraction of these items cluster into a small number of *root* gaps found by multiple independent methods: the researched-Person rights channel (DOM-05, SEC-*, RT-01, REG-02/REG-03 -- one root gap, five independent sightings), the identity-merge/split lineage gap (CI-8, RT-05 -- one root gap, two sightings), and the "principle stated, enforcement absent" meta-pattern that recurs across RLS, insert-only ledgers, break-glass access, and destructive-migration protection (CI-2/CI-4, SEC-06/SEC-07, RT-03/RT-04/RT-13 -- one meta-pattern, seven sightings). See `17_TECH_DEBT_PREVENTION.md` for the consolidated, deduplicated action list and `22_OPEN_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md` for the subset requiring an explicit operator decision rather than straightforward engineering follow-through.

**HUMAN** in the Confidence column marks the 19 of 45 items flagged `requires_human_decision=true` -- indexed in decision-ready form in `22_OPEN_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md`.
