The shift

## The future of search is not just rankings. It is references.

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of structuring your website so AI systems can understand, retrieve, cite, and summarize your content. SEO still matters, but AI discovery adds a new surface: the answer itself.

According to [Andreessen Horowitz research](https://a16z.com/geo-over-seo/), the $90+ billion SEO market is being disrupted as AI-native search experiences move into browsers, assistants, and product workflows.

The operating metric changes from click-through rate to reference rate: how often AI systems use your brand or website as part of an answer.

Measurement

## The four pillars of AI visibility

AI visibility is not one metric. Treat visibility, citations, mentions, and sentiment as separate signals because each represents a deeper level of AI engagement with your content.

01

### Visibility

Your brand appears in the AI system's search or retrieval results. Driven by SEO fundamentals, domain authority, and keyword relevance.

02

### Citations

The AI visits or references your URL as a source. Driven by useful titles, summaries, structured data, and extractable sections.

03

### Mentions

Your brand is named in the generated answer. Driven by brand recognition, category authority, and third-party validation.

04

### Sentiment

The tone AI uses when describing your brand. Driven by content quality, reviews, press, and reputation signals.

### Mentions can happen without citations

AI systems can mention brands from training data, search-result snippets, third-party sources, or direct URL citations. Citations provide the strongest signal for lesser-known brands because when AI reads your content, it is more likely to reference you accurately. Sentiment analysis then shows whether AI describes your brand positively, neutrally, or negatively. [Learn more about these metrics](/blog/understanding-ai-visibility-metrics).

Research finding

### Answer capsules are the clearest citation signal.

**72% of pages cited by ChatGPT had an answer capsule present**, making it the single most consistent predictor of AI citation. Over 90% of cited capsules contained no hyperlinks.

01

### Content Structure

- **Create answer capsules:** Include a concise 1-2 sentence answer, about 120-150 characters, at the start of each section that AI can extract verbatim.
- **Keep capsules link-free:** Avoid hyperlinks in your answer snippets because they reduce citation likelihood.
- **Answer-first formatting:** Lead with the answer, then explain. Do not bury key information.
- **Bullet-point formatting:** Helps LLMs extract and reproduce content effectively.
- **FAQ-style structure:** Question followed by answer is highly effective for citations.

02

### Technical Implementation

- **Create llms.txt files:** Help AI crawlers understand your content structure.
- **FAQPage schema markup:** Content with FAQ schema appears significantly more in AI answers.
- **dateModified freshness:** Include last-updated dates in Article schema because AI favors fresh content.
- **Fast indexing:** Use sitemaps and indexing APIs so AI can see new content quickly.
- **Monitor AI crawlers:** Check logs for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and BingPreview. Ensure they are not blocked.

03

### Build Authority with E-E-A-T

AI models gauge trustworthiness the same way search engines do. E-E-A-T signals help you earn citations.

- **Original research:** Publish proprietary data, surveys, or case studies. Original research is the 2nd strongest predictor of citations.
- **Author credentials:** Display author names, bios, and expertise clearly.
- **Cite reputable sources:** Back claims with references to known reliable sources.
- **Topic clusters:** Create pillar pages with supporting sub-pages for topical authority.

Technical guide

## Technical implementation

The goal is not to create AI-only content. The goal is to make high-quality content easier for crawlers, search indexes, and LLM retrieval systems to understand.

01
**Create an llms.txt file**_Root-level context for AI crawlers_

The llms.txt file is a simple text file placed at your website root that helps AI crawlers understand your site structure and key content. Think of it as a robots.txt for LLMs.

llms.txt

Place at: https://yoursite.com/llms.txt

```
# Ayzeo - Generative Engine Optimization Platform

## About
Ayzeo helps websites optimize for AI model citations (GEO) and traditional search engines (SEO).
Get cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek to drive AI referral traffic.

## Key Features
- AI Visibility Analytics: Real-time citation tracking with competitor benchmarking
- SEO Analysis: Comprehensive website optimization
- Content Recommendations: AI-powered suggestions for better citations
- Project Management: Multi-project dashboard with team collaboration

## Important Pages
- Homepage: https://ayzeo.com
- GEO Guide: https://ayzeo.com/geo-guide
- Pricing: https://ayzeo.com/pricing
- Dashboard: https://ayzeo.com/dashboard

## Contact
- Email: contact@fyrma.io
- Location: New York & Berlin
```

### llms.txt best practices

- Keep it simple and well-structured with clear headings.
- Include a brief description of what your site does.
- List your most important pages and their purposes.
- Update it when you add major new content or features.
- Use Markdown formatting for better readability.

02
**Enhance schema markup for AI**_Structured context for interpretation_

Structured data helps AI models understand your content context. Only about 12% of websites use schema, but LLMs grounded in structured data achieve up to 300% higher accuracy. Schema helps interpretation, ensuring AI properly understands your content so it is eligible to be cited.

### FAQPage Schema

Most effective for citations. Q&A pairs are directly quotable by AI. Studies show FAQ schema content appears significantly more in AI answers.

### Article/BlogPosting Schema

Include headline, author with credentials, datePublished, dateModified, and publisher. Aligns with E-E-A-T principles.

### HowTo Schema

For tutorials and step-by-step guides. Defines each step and tools required. AI can present steps and cite your content.

### Product/Review Schema

For ecommerce. Mark up product details, reviews, and ratings. AI answering shopping queries can incorporate this structured information.

03
**Add Organization schema**_Connect entity identity and authority_

Organization schema helps AI systems connect your brand, website, profiles, location, and category terms into one entity. Include `sameAs` references to authoritative profiles so AI systems can reconcile your entity across trusted sources.

organization-schema.json (LD+JSON)

Add to your page head

```
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "Ayzeo",
  "description": "Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform that helps websites get cited by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek",
  "url": "https://ayzeo.com",
  "logo": "https://ayzeo.com/favicon.svg",
  "foundingDate": "2024",
  "founders": [{
    "@type": "Person",
    "name": "Aykut Cevik"
  }],
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "New York & Berlin"
  },
  "sameAs": [
    "https://twitter.com/ayzeo",
    "https://linkedin.com/company/ayzeo"
  ],
  "keywords": "GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, AI SEO, ChatGPT citations, AI visibility"
}
```

04
**Add FAQPage schema**_Make Q&A content directly quotable_

FAQPage schema is highly effective for citation because the structure mirrors how users ask AI systems questions.

faq-schema.json (LD+JSON)

Add to pages with FAQ content

```
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "GEO is the practice of optimizing your website to be cited and referenced by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek. Unlike SEO which focuses on search rankings, GEO focuses on reference rates."
    }
  }, {
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "How is GEO different from SEO?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "SEO optimizes for search engine rankings and click-through rates. GEO optimizes for AI model citations and reference rates. SEO handles short keyword queries, while GEO handles AI search queries averaging 8.7 words."
    }
  }]
}
```

### Pro tip

Always include dateModified in your Article schema when you refresh content. AI models and users both value fresh information. Recently updated pages are favored for time-sensitive queries.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing your website to be cited and referenced by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek. Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on ranking in search engines, GEO focuses on getting your content included in AI-generated answers and increasing your reference rate.

### How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for search result rankings and clicks. GEO optimizes for AI-generated answers, citations, mentions, and sentiment. SEO targets Google and Bing with shorter keyword queries, while GEO targets ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek with AI search queries averaging about 8.7 words instead of 4 words.

### What is an llms.txt file and do I need one?

An llms.txt file is a simple Markdown-style file at your website root, for example yoursite.com/llms.txt, that helps AI crawlers understand your site structure and key content. It is not mandatory, but it can improve how AI systems understand your important pages, features, and contact information.

### Can I do SEO and GEO at the same time?

Yes. Many GEO best practices also benefit traditional SEO. Structured content, clear summaries, bullet points, semantic markup, authoritative sources, and crawlability help both search engines and AI models understand your content.

GEO results vary depending on content quality and implementation. Some websites see increased AI citations within a few weeks of implementing GEO best practices, while others may take 2-3 months. The key is consistent monitoring and iteration.

Basic GEO implementation can be done without advanced technical skills. AI sentiment analysis tracks how AI models describe your brand in their responses, whether the tone is positive, neutral, or negative. ## What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing websites to get cited and referenced by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek, rather than just ranking in traditional search engines. Unlike SEO, which focuses on click-through rates, GEO focuses on reference rates - how often your website is cited as a source in AI-generated answers. The key insight from the SimilarWeb 2025 AI Landscape Report: "It's visibility, not traffic, that is the main KPI in this new world."

## The Four Pillars of AI Visibility

Understanding the difference between these four metrics is crucial: Visibility (your brand appears in AI search results), Citations (AI actually visits your URL to read content), Mentions (your brand is named in the AI-generated answer), and Sentiment (how AI describes your brand - positive, neutral, or negative). Important: Mentions do not require citations because AI can mention brands from training data or search result signals alone. However, citations provide the strongest signal for lesser-known brands. Sentiment analysis helps you understand the quality and tone of AI mentions.

## GEO Best Practices - Answer Capsules

An answer capsule is a concise, self-contained answer (1-2 sentences, about 120-150 characters) placed at the top of a section or under a question heading. 72% of pages cited by ChatGPT had an answer capsule present, making it the single most consistent predictor of AI citation. Over 90% of cited answer capsules contained no hyperlinks. Keep them link-free and lead with the answer.

## Schema Markup for AI Citations

Only about 12% of websites use schema, but LLMs grounded in structured data achieve up to 300% higher accuracy. Key schema types: FAQPage (most effective for citations because Q&A pairs are directly quotable), Article/BlogPosting (with author credentials and dateModified), Organization (with sameAs links to authoritative profiles), HowTo (for tutorials), and Product/Review (for ecommerce). Always include sameAs references linking to authoritative profiles.

## E-E-A-T for AI Visibility

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals help earn citations. Original research/data is the 2nd strongest differentiator for cited pages. Display author names and bios with credentials. Back claims with reputable citations. Use topic cluster strategy with pillar pages. Backlinks and domain authority strongly correlate with AI citations.

## Technical Implementation - llms.txt Files

An llms.txt file is a simple text file placed at your website root (yoursite.com/llms.txt) that helps AI crawlers understand your site structure and key content. Think of it as robots.txt for LLMs. The file should include: brief site description, key features, important pages with URLs, and contact information. Format using Markdown for better readability.
