FINDING 3: Out-of-order concurrent Bash calls can erase each other’s attribution.

  `mcp/claude-userlevel/hooks/rules/file-claim-guard.js:224` -> `mcp/claude-userlevel/hooks/rules/file-claim-guard.js:226-236`
