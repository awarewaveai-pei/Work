Do not ship slice 1 yet: the lease fence plus client-side timeout cannot guarantee that an already-transmitted Telegram request will not arrive after lease expiry or recovery.

Findings:
- [high] AbortSignal.timeout cannot enforce the claimed external-send fence (agency-os/handoffs/2026-09-21-site-watch-plan.md:38)
  §8 I1/C3 treats a 10-second client abort, plus 30 seconds of lease slack, as proof that an external Telegram send cannot overlap a successor executor. That is not guaranteed: once fetch has transmitted the request, aborting the client only stops waiting locally; Telegram or an intermediary may already have accepted and deliver the message later. A renewal failure or lease expiry can therefore allow the next executor to start while the stale send is still externally effective, including after the episode was silenced by recovery. The acceptance scenario requiring no delayed delivery is not enforceable by this code alone. Add provider-level idempotency/deduplication or a durable send protocol with an explicit uncertainty state and recovery handling; otherwise weaken I1's guarantee and test the acknowledged duplicate/late-delivery case.
  Recommendation: Do not claim overlap is impossible from lease remaining time and AbortSignal.timeout. Add an idempotency key/deduplication mechanism at the delivery boundary, or model an aborted/in-flight send as indeterminate and prevent episode silencing or successor notification until it is reconciled.
