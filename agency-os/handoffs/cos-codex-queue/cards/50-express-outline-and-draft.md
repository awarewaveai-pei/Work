# Card 50 — EXPRESS: selection, outline, draft with every sentence traced to a record

## Decision
Required reading: `STEP34_SHARED_RULES.md` §11; `docs/platform/step34-cards/EXPRESS.card.txt` (every line is a requirement); `STEP2_SHARED_RULES.md` §2-§3 (evidence words, `evidence.checked_claims()`), `BLOCK_BUILD_GUIDE.md`. EXPRESS turns the person's own judgment into something for other people. Every sentence of a draft links back to a record; a sentence on a claim not yet checked, or with one origin, is marked; the person alone sends.

## Constraints
- Kinds in `kinds/express.py` (case-owned): `express.selection` (user; `items` = the ONLY input of an outline; each item must be a doc of THIS case that exists, re-checked on save), `express.draft` (authors `{"ai","joint","user"}`; `sentences: [{text, refs, unchecked, author}]`; a sentence with `author:"user"` is the person's own words; a sentence with `author:"ai"` is always shown as AI's and can never be treated as the person's stance: a gate test: no other block reads `express.draft` sentences as `human_only` content), `express.sent` (author `{"user"}` only; the ONLY act that marks a draft as sent; no AI action and no hook may write it: add a source-scan test).
- Formats the person picks: 摘要, 證據報告, 決策理由, 提案, 學習紀錄, 一封信, 談話稿, 論文段落. A format is a small ruleset `express.formats` in `platform/rulesets/express.py` (name, what sections it has, the reader it assumes); versioned like the others.
- Outline: a function in `block_hooks/express.py` building an outline ONLY from the selection's items (never reading other records of the case). Each outline node carries the refs of the items it uses.
- Draft AI actions (`ai_actions/express.py`, permissions as the contract's AI lines): `draft_from_outline` (reads only the selection's items and the claim status/origin counts of the claims the selection touches, via `evidence.checked_claims()`; nothing else), `adjust_for_reader` (rewrites wording for the reader; may not add a claim that is not in the refs). Shape check after the model: every sentence has ≥1 ref that is in the selection; a sentence without refs is refused (not marked, refused); a sentence whose ref is a claim that is `not usable_as_fact` or has fewer than 2 independent origins is saved with `unchecked: true`; the AI is told the person's confidence and uncertainty lines and the answer keeps them (a test: the person's 信心 text appears in the draft unchanged).
- Handback (existing `AiAction.handback` mechanism): when the selection touches an unchecked claim the AI stops BEFORE the model call and returns 「這裡有還沒查證的句子，要先處理還是照樣寫進去？」 as a question to the person; the person's answer is stored as `express.selection` option `unchecked_ok: true`.
- Someone else's words (a pasted message in a selected record) reach the model only through `AiAction.quoted` (see STEP2_SHARED_RULES §5).
- The draft is re-readable by the person with every sentence's source one click away (card 52 builds the page).
