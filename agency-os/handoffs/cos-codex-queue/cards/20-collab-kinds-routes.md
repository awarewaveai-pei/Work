# Card 20 — COLLAB: the group's records and routes (blind positions, stances, dissent, authority, closing, reopen)

## Decision
Required reading: `STEP34_SHARED_RULES.md` §2-§3, §6-§7; `docs/platform/step34-cards/COLLAB.card.txt` (every line is a requirement); cards 10-13 are on this tree (check: `git log --oneline` and the files exist; if not, `BLOCKED:`). COLLAB helps a few people decide together and keep their differences. The platform offers a procedure; it never says it made the decision fair, never says a consensus exists.

## Constraints
- Kinds in `kinds/collab.py`, all `owner="group"`, with the fields, authors, `blind`, `visibility` and rules of the table in §7 (add nothing the table does not list, except fields the card's lines need; list each such field in the checklist). Use card 11's `blind="round"` for `collab.position`, `collab.change`, `collab.interface_answers`; `visibility="self_and_govern"` for `collab.place`; `human_only` where the person's own words are required; the six interface questions come from the existing `INTERFACE_Q` constant of the supervisor data: find the engine-side copy (`grep -rn "interface" ai_lego/core/domain/kinds`) or create `kinds/collab_questions.py` with the six questions copied from `docs/platform/step34-cards/COLLAB.card.txt` and a test that compares them with that file.
- Routes in `apps/api/blocks/collab.py`, every one starting with membership via card 11 (`visibility`) and writing through `CaseDocs.save(tenant, Owner.group(id), ...)` (G1 applies): list/save each kind; read other members' items ONLY through `visibility.visible`; read a member's consented case records (their MAP, VERIFY results, EXPRESS proposals) ONLY through `visibility.shared_with` (card 13).
- Behaviours that must hold (each a test): (1) before a member wrote their position for round v, no route returns another member's position of v, not by list, not by id, not in the stance table, not in the agreement view; (2) after writing, they see all; (3) a `collab.stance` objection is stored with its reason and never changed into a vote; one live stance per (member, option); (4) `collab.dissent` is derived from objections by the hook and never deleted by a majority; the member chooses how they are shown (label or 匿名) once, in `collab.place`-like own record `collab.label` {label}; (5) `collab.authority`: a grant names `to`, `scope` and the grantors; revoking by one member removes only that member's share and the shown authority is recomputed from the remaining grantors (the card's example: three grant to A, B revokes, A represents two; the scope must be renegotiated: the hook writes a `collab.reopen`-style notice 「範圍要重新談」 as a system record); (6) `collab.closing` requires all four of `why_end`, `reopen_if`, `review_on`, `owner`; (7) `collab.reopen` by any member is never refused; the group's status stays open; (8) a closed group (card 10) refuses new records except `collab.review` and `collab.consequence` (flag `after_close`); (9) no record is shown with a vote count or ranking; no score/rank key (existing gate).
- The convener is not a judge: no route lets the convener delete another member's record; the convener can close the group, erase it, invite, confirm held members (card 12).
- The `consequence` write-back: `collab.consequence {decision, observed}`; the next round's page offers it (card 22).
- Handoff lines of the card: the group's joint judgment goes to DECIDE and its actions to ACT: build the link as a button that creates a DECIDE judgment draft / ACT task in the CONVENER's own case from the group's `collab.closing` (copying the text, with a pointer back; a copy is a pointer: store the group and record ids, not a frozen snapshot) — if DECIDE/ACT kinds make that awkward, implement the pointer record only and say so in the checklist.
