# Card 61 — FACILITATE: the leader's records, derived eligibility, supervision (ACCESS CONTROL: two reviewers)

## Decision
Required reading: `STEP34_SHARED_RULES.md` §5, §9, §11; `FACILITATE.card.txt`; cards 13 (consent: facilitator target), 30 (appeals), 41 (completion), 60 (scripts). A leader trains first, leads by the script, sees only what participants consent to show, logs each session (what followed, what deviated, why), and is supervised by someone who is trained and has led. Nothing here scores a leader.

## Constraints
- Kinds in `kinds/facilitate.py`: `facilitate.log` (person-owned by the facilitator, author user: `{group_id, script_version, followed, deviated: [{what, why}]}`), `facilitate.supervision` (person-owned by the SUPERVISOR: `{supervisee, feedback}`; saved only if `is_eligible_supervisor(supervisor)`; the supervisee reads their own feedback through a route, nobody else does, the participants never do). Logs and feedback live with the facilitator and are never attached to participants' cases.
- Replace card 13's stubs in `platform/visibility.py`: `is_eligible_facilitator(identity)` = a `teach.completion` of course `facilitator` exists (card 40/60); `is_eligible_supervisor(identity)` = eligible facilitator AND at least one `facilitate.log` as facilitator; checked by reading records ONLY for existence, never content. Make the supervisor reader class of card 30 real (`facilitate.supervision` link: supervisor reads appeals ABOUT the supervisee, masked unless named) and un-skip that test.
- Role `facilitator` in `group_members` can be set only for an identity that is `is_eligible_facilitator` at the time (the group routes of card 10/12: add the check; a test).
- What a facilitator reads of participants: only through card 13's `shared_with(grantee_kind="facilitator")`, plus the fact that a case names them as the leader (the intake field the card mentions: `assembler.intake.led_by` `{facilitator_identity_id}`, set by the participant, `human_only`; make the minimal addition to `kinds/assembler.py` (S's file) and say so in the commit message).
- Supervisor may be found by the facilitator or assigned by the unit: both are `facilitate.supervision_link` records `{supervisee, supervisor, assigned_by: self|unit}` written by the facilitator (self) or the convener of a unit group; the link is valid only if the supervisor is eligible at the time and after; the check is repeated on every read.
- Shape of what the supervisor reads: the supervisee's `facilitate.log` records, and appeals about the supervisee (card 30). Nothing else.
- Facilitator drift records for research: a log's `deviated` entries reach GOVERN only under card 31's rule (all consent); add the function `drift_records(study)` the exporter calls, with the consent check inside it.

## Acceptance
`tests/unit/test_block_facilitate.py`: an untrained identity cannot be set as facilitator; a trained one can; supervisor needs training AND a log; an ineligible supervisor cannot save feedback; the supervisor reads the supervisee's logs and masked appeals about them and nothing else (probe: a participant's case, another facilitator's logs: all 404); the facilitator reads only consented items (card 13) and cannot read an appeal about them (card 30's tests stay green and the un-skipped supervisor test passes); eligibility is re-checked after a lesson `teach.run` is deleted (the facilitator falls out of eligibility); the unit-assigned link is invalid when the supervisor loses eligibility; drift records exclude non-consenting participants. Mutations: skip the training check -> red; let the supervisor read participants' cases -> red; cache eligibility -> red; skip re-check on read -> red.
