# Super Page

Super Agent for Page Creation

> ✨ Super Page is a super-agent that scans SERP top-ranking competitors, extracts their winning CTA strategies, and creates complete, optimized pages, with perfect structure, relevant images, optimal word count, and strategic CTAs in minutes.

Opening the Super Page

In your Dashboard click on the Super Page.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/9UNLyvPIk2.jpg)

Also you can easily open Super Page from any of the pages.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ZVIoZAww0p.jpg)

Adding instructions

Add instructions for creating a super page.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/iuyk7R4f7r.jpg)

> Enter your keyword (or it will be determined automatically from the context), specify what you're selling, and define your desired customer action to create a high-converting page.

Examples:

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/H2F9U1vG4a.jpg)

1. Product Comparison Page

> Create a comparison of "top 10 washing machines in 2025". Include feature table, rating reviews, pros/cons, specifications, and Amazon affiliate links with format "https://amazon.com/dp/PRODUCTID?tag=myrefid-20". Add prominent "Buy Now" CTA buttons under each product and highlight an "Editor's Choice" recommendation.

2. Local Service Page

> Create a service page for "roofing in Chicago". Include service descriptions, an interactive map showing 123 N Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60601 as the business location, and prominent phone CTAs (555-123-4567) throughout the page.

3. Content with Reference URL

> Create an article with images about "environmental technology". Use "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_technology" as reference.

4. Listicle Blog Page

> Create a page with a review of "top 10 music videos from the 2000s", embed these videos from YouTube.

5. Guides with Product Recommendations

> Create a comprehensive guide featuring "America's 10 most stunning national parks" with spectacular imagery at the beginning of each section. For each park, include must-see attractions, best hiking trails, and ideal visiting seasons. Between the 3rd and 4th parks, the 6th and 7th parks, and after the 10th park, insert relevant camping gear recommendations with Amazon affiliate links (format: "https://amazon.com/dp/PRODUCTID?tag=myrefid-20"). Feature a quality hiking backpack, durable tent, and versatile hiking boots with image and with "Check Price" CTAs. Ensure each product recommendation naturally connects to the park activities mentioned in the surrounding content.

Choose Target Location

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ukqzwVNBny.jpg)

Selecting a Target Location ensures Super Page analyzes SERP results specific to that geographic area. This helps create content tailored to audiences in that particular location.

Select a Brand Voice

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/3a7aaBrcs8.jpg)

The [Brand Voice](https://docs.seowriting.ai/article/brand-voice) option allows you to customize the style and tone of your content.

Select AI Images model

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/3Dgd_RfCbF.jpg)

> **Note:** Nano Banana (Ultra) cost 5 credits per 1 Super Page generation.

Start Super Page Creation

After everything set up click on the button and start Super Page creation process.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/hPdLM1TSs-.jpg)

> Within minutes, your Super Page is ready.

Publishing to your website

Publish the page to your [WordPress](https://docs.seowriting.ai/article/wordpress-integration) website in 1 click.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/iAE4Z07PRu.jpg)

> If you have any questions or suggestions about using Super Page, please contact our support team in chat or email **support@seowriting.ai**. We're continuously improving our service based on your feedback and are here to help you create high-performing pages.

These AI models with 🔎 Real-Time SERP analyze the top 10 SERP results to reveal keywords, article type, number of headings and words in your niche, and automatically apply this when creating articles, bringing better rankings and more organic traffic 📈 to your website. These AI models maintain keyword frequency within recommended limits (under 2%). Also, these AI models don't require a title.

1.1 Uploading an Excel file

Click on the "Import from Excel" button.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/1T4FXNmdcf.jpg)

Select an Excel file:

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/q6XLzlOyAq.jpg)

> By default, values from the first 4 columns are loaded starting from the 1 line. But it can be done also from other columns if you name them **Main Keyword | Title | Keywords | Outline** (as in the picture above).
> -In the Keywords field, you can add values up to 2500 characters long. Keywords must be separated by commas.
> -All Excel formats are supported.
> -Upload file size - no more than 3 MB.

> To indicate tags H1, H2 and H3 in the Outline, it’s required to put the Markdown tags #, ## and ### before each of the headings. Headings without these tags will be removed when you copy & paste or upload from Excel file.

Headings in the Outline must be specified on one line (Click [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MA3whr0Uv_M-4X47Egt-w5LceK4cUPWRKw1-5TYcfnY/edit?usp=sharing) to view an example):

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/Nryj7k97rS.jpg)

You can also specify headings in the Outline each on a new line (Click [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hZxezZVfp4G8itKCB1biv5gGyq8h9874Wq7H-v-6H-U/edit?usp=sharing) to view an example):

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/PFmoe0BV41.jpg)

> In Outline you can set 1 heading H1, up to 18 headings H2 and up to 4 headings H3 under each of H2. The length of each heading is limited to 100 characters. Headings outside these limits will be removed when you copy & paste or upload from Excel file.

> "Conclusion" and "H3" settings from the Structure block do not work if the Outline fields are filled in.

If the Import from Excel is successful, the fields should be filled in automatically.

# Internal Linking

> Boost your website's SEO with our Internal Linking feature! 🌐✨ The feature indexes your website, identifies the most relevant pages and automatically inserts links within your new articles.

Turning on the Internal Linking.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/hwlDfgaZfC.jpg)

To be able to turn on Internal Linking you need to install our [WordPress plugin](https://docs.seowriting.ai/article/wordpress-integration) **version 1.7.1+** on your website and connect it to SEOWritingAI.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ckuFE7MJ7G.jpg)

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/tCh2OReIoQ.jpg)

Select your website.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/0T6LeIIPvS.jpg)

You can see how many pages already indexed and can be used in Internal linking.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/V1D0fZqsI0.jpg)

> If zero articles are indexed, no internal links will be added to the generated article. To ensure that internal linking works, you need to have articles on your website and get them indexed.

If it’s necessary you can press the “Refresh” button to add recently published pages.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/Wslj2lO3Mr.jpg)

> Our semantic search feature will identify the most relevant pages to link to within your article.

You can exclude or include specific pages on your site by adding paths, for example: /admin/*, /private-area, /products/*, /blog/, /category-ex423_*.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/tdlNp4JJT0.jpg)

You can select the number of internal links you want to be inserted in the content.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/fE-NdVTg8h.jpg)

For the Placement of the internal links, there are 3 options: At the Top, Evenly Throughout, At the Bottom.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/1f9KT4XX-g.jpg)

At the Top placement means that internal links will be inserted at the top part of the article.

Evenly Throughout placement means that the internal links will be inserted throughout the whole article.

At the Bottom placement means that internal links will be inserted at the bottom part of the article.

> Contextual Enrichment is a feature that not only allows the addition of links to other pages when writing an article but also reads and analyzes the content of those pages. This helps integrate relevant information seamlessly, ensuring the links enhance the quality and informativeness of the article.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/YdHbDsTI1o.jpg)

Select the links attributes: Dofollow or Nofollow.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/KTvgPLFLrK.jpg)

> Optimize your content by adding up to 150 keywords to guide our AI in creating SEO-friendly articles! 🚀✍️

> **Note:** DeepSeek R1 and DeepSeek V3 AI models with 🔎 Real-Time SERP analyze the top 10 SERP results to reveal keywords in your niche and automatically include them in the text, bringing better rankings and more organic traffic 📈 to your website. # CTA Button

The system automatically adds call-to-action buttons and images at the right place in your content. This functionality is only available with **"Llama 4 + Real-Time SERP + CTA"** model, making creating attractive, well-organized listicles much easier.

> When publishing articles on your website using our [WordPress plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/seowriting/), the ".button" style will be added to CTA buttons. To ensure the buttons display well on your website, you can add CSS styles.

How to add CSS styles for CTA buttons

1. Open your WordPress admin panel.

2. Go to Appearance → Customize → Additional CSS.

3. Paste the following CSS into the input area:

> .button {
> display: inline-block;
> border-radius: 4px;
> background-color: #6965f6;
> padding: 10px 24px;
> text-align: center;
> font-size: 20px;
> line-height: 30px;
> color: #ffffff;
> text-decoration: none;
> transition-property: background-color;
> transition-duration: 300ms;
> }
> .button:hover {
> background-color: #5855d0;
> }

4. Click Publish to save your changes.

Please email us with your suggestions and feedback to help us improve this functionality. We value your input and are committed to making this feature even better based on your experiences.

> 🚀 The WordPress MCP Server creates a direct bridge between Claude chat and your website, letting you create, analyze, and publish content without ever leaving the Claude chat interface. No more copy-pasting between platforms!

Quick Setup Guide

First, install the [SEO WRITING WordPress plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/seowriting/) on your website and connect it to your account.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/rCjJneemkn.jpg)

Step 2: Get Your MCP Server URL

Once your plugin is installed, go to your [SEO WRITING’s Integrations](https://seowriting.ai/settings/integration). In the Integrations section, you'll find your unique MCP Server URL.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/KkpRu_o5t2.jpg)

Step 3: Add Integration to Claude

In Claude, go to your [settings](https://claude.ai/settings/connectors) and add a new connector. Choose a name for the integration and add the MCP Server URL.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/eX5IHYQnxE.jpg)

Step 4: Start New Chat

Open a new chat in Claude and select your SEO WRITING WordPress integration from the available connectors list.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/8uq66odD6u.jpg)

You're all set! Claude can now directly access and publish to your WordPress websites.

# Rewriter Tool

Improve content for better search engine rankings

> Rewrite your content with SERP data to make it more SEO-optimized and search engine friendly, improving your Google rankings 📈.

Getting Started

On the Dashboard, click on "Rewriter Tool".

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/nv_Z6B_8w8.jpg)

You can also open the tool from any of the pages within the platform.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/lLoBw7oT9u.jpg)

How to Use the Rewriter Tool

Step 1: Select the Mode

Choose "Rewriter" mode from the dropdown menu. This will use 1 credit per rewrite operation.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/PVoDbKH1C7.jpg)

Step 2: Set the Main Keyword

In the "Main Keyword" field, you can either:

- Let the tool automatically detect keywords from your content.
- Manually enter your target keyword (0/80 characters limit).

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/TWfxd50JL6.jpg)

Step 3: Choose Language Settings

Select your language from the Language dropdown menu or let the tool automatically detect it from your content.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/eZ3I9PRMoq.jpg)

Step 4: Set Target Country

Choose your target country from the Target Country dropdown. This helps optimize the content for local search preferences and terminology.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/Bpn2_vaDxB.jpg)

Step 5: Add Optional Instructions

In the "Additional instructions" field, describe what specific changes you'd like to make to your content. This is optional but helps customize the rewriting process to your needs.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/eREVDHcJaf.jpg)

As an example, we added these additional instructions:

> Reduce the list to 5.
> Add a green check mark before each heading.

Step 6: Input Your Text

Paste your original text into the "Input Text" field. The tool supports up to 5,000 words (0/5000 limit shown at the bottom).

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/LW7S0uhnFh.jpg)

Step 7: Start Rewriting

Press the "Rewrite" button to begin the content transformation process.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ZZxbZTze_p.jpg)

Step 8: Wait for Processing

Wait a few minutes while the tool processes your content and creates the rewritten version using SERP data.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/if5q4q2g2m.jpg)

Step 9: Review the Results

Once processing is complete you can review the result and keep the new version by clicking "Confirm" button.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ebyll2V3ig.jpg)

You can rewrite again by pressing the "Try again" button.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ugvhzGuvGa.jpg)

To keep the previous version, you should click on "Return" button.

![](https://tawk.link/6305d89737898912e964d136/kb/attachments/ar-44aHiV1.jpg)
