## Notes for use

- **Adapt section sizes to decision complexity.** Simple decisions don't need huge tables; complex decisions may expand sections.
- **Keep TL;DR short and scannable.** 30 seconds maximum.
- **Use collapsible sections** for detailed phases, transcripts. Most readers want the recommendation, not the full debate.
- **Always include switch conditions.** Decisions don't stay decisions forever.
- **Always include why-not-others.** Builds trust with readers who would have chosen differently.
